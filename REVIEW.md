## Editorial decisions

- Mandible: original notes identify the anterior symphysis/parasymphysis windows described by Fatima et al. (2025). They do not generalise trauma results to complete visibility or normality.
- Maxilla: the draft uses the lateral maxillary sinus wall and infraorbital margin named in the McCann et al. original-study abstract, rather than repeating the potentially confusing medial-wall terminology in the newer article. Exact interfaces on an acquired scan still require review.
- Zygoma: notes distinguish named regional windows from a complete isolated bone. The zygomatic-complex study used the unaffected side as a comparison. No performance percentages or treatment recommendations are included.
- General cortical-shadow wording from the worker's additional PDFs was omitted because the coordinator could not independently retrieve those full sources. No claim of universal shadow or complete absence of visibility is made.
