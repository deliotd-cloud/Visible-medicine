The helper uses the immutable complete identities already in `content/abdominal-branch-imaging-pins.json`; it adds no geometry, source pin, image or modality.

The left colic artery was identified by dissection and ruler-measured, not measured by ultrasound. One waveform does not map continuity or adequate collateral perfusion. |

EUS uses an endoscopic window. IOUS uses surgical exposure. Neither is routine transabdominal scanning. No visibility frequency, patient-specific flow, velocity threshold, treatment direction or whole-arcade inference is proposed. Every other abdominal branch ultrasound field remains pending, including FMA70479/FMA70480 (inferior pancreaticoduodenal divisions), FMA14782 (anterior superior branch), FMA14828/FMA14829 (left-colic children) and the other pancreatic branches. Their anatomical/CT material cannot substitute for branch-specific ultrasound evidence.

These papers supply reading links and paraphrased facts only. No figures, passage, measurements, dataset, scan or media is imported. In particular Lan et al.'s NC-ND media are excluded; article access grants no product asset licence. The donor surfaces remain unvalidated and cannot be aligned to an acquired image or interpreted as a patient boundary. Exact-revision radiologist sign-off, source/review binding, application verification and any Website integration remain separate decisions by main. Clinical approval, deployment and patient registration are not asserted.
