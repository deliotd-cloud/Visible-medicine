## Evidence and editorial limits

- [Bink et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1097/GOX.0000000000005343): volunteer ultrasound feasibility; selected sites and either side measured. Read as acquisition-specific evidence, not a bilateral whole-course survey or diagnostic validation. The repeated notch wording in its SON/STN table is not adopted as proof of a shared anatomical exit.
- [Berchtold et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28712884/): anatomical and ultrasound investigation of the named nerves. The accessible abstract does not establish a separate ultrasound sample count or per-image verification. Only the abstract was verified for this source.

No figures, tables or article text are bundled. Bink's publication has CC BY-NC-ND terms and is not admitted as a reusable image asset. The original lesson prose cites findings; no article licence is represented as commercial asset clearance. No new dependency, font, texture, scan or dataset was added.
