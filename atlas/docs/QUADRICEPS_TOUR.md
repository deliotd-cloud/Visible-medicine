# Right knee: quadriceps to tibia

This six-stop draft Guided learning sequence compares right rectus femoris,
vastus lateralis, vastus medialis, vastus intermedius, patella and tibia.
The right femur supplies faded context. The tour uses an explicit Whole body
thigh/pelvis/leg union because the femur's source record spans all three
regions. Every source structure retains its own region membership.

## Source binding and references

`content/quadriceps-tour-pins.json` binds six targets, one context bone and
three existing BodyParts3D 4.0 display bundles to baseline
`dc0f5d9648b73e4baf25f468e52963b1d503df68`. The pin script reads the
`bodyDisplayCatalog` result, verifies the unchanged source catalog and bundle
bytes, then saves full displayed structure and bundle records. Runtime
admission checks entire tour, structure, source-coordinate, version, licence
and bundle identities. Missing, duplicate or altered participant records fail.
No source geometry, label, region or licence is rewritten.

Original short captions summarize facts from these teaching references,
checked 27 October 2026:

- [Rectus femoris](https://rad.uw.edu/muscle-atlas/rectus-femoris)
- [Vastus lateralis](https://rad.uw.edu/muscle-atlas/vastus-lateralis)

No reference prose, illustrations, media or scans are imported. University of
Washington teaching illustrations are not used. Existing BodyParts3D
attribution and licence remain in force.

## Playback and review

Each stop selects one source surface and fades neighbours. Its authored dwell
is 14 seconds; automatic playback applies the shared reading-time policy
(at least 24 seconds at 1×, including an 8-second camera arrival), scaled by
the learner's chosen 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× or 3× speed. The existing smooth camera
transition, reduced-motion behavior, pause and exit restoration are reused.
This is an orientation sequence, with
no contraction, knee movement or force simulation. A whole-muscle or
whole-bone surface cannot establish a tendon junction, retinaculum, patellar
ligament, enthesis, insertion footprint, cartilage or patient-specific
relationship. There is no acquired scan registration. Revision-bound
radiologist sign-off remains pending; source pins do not grant clinical
approval. Independent Atlas, case and lecture access remain separate.
