The module reuses version-4 source surfaces, their existing coordinate transforms and documented display corrections. Source files were converted to GLB, separated or grouped by identity, normal-smoothed where recorded, and recoloured. Diagrammatic shading is not measured muscle-fibre direction. Exploded positions are teaching layouts, not anatomical positions.

Root regional selections, eye layers, ventricular spaces, cerebral regions, brainstem/cerebellum, visual-pathway context, cricothyroid parts and cranial-artery source components retain their existing provenance and explicit limitations. Numbered vessel source parts are not validated named clinical segments. Source mesh counts do not establish complete anatomy, continuity or a patient registration.
