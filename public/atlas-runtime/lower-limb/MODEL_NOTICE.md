*A Three-Dimensional Lower Extremity Musculoskeletal Geometry Model of An Asian Male*,

This website export contains only five display GLBs and two catalogues from the
previously audited 67 source STL surfaces. No MRI, DICOM, segmentation mask,
private patient record, original source archive or clinical review payload is included.
GLB delivery preserves the audited positions/winding, with the recorded display
transform and removal of exactly zero-area triangles. It adds diagrammatic
colour, selection, labels and optional explanatory separation; no missing tissue
is reconstructed. Refer to each catalogue for immutable source/bundle hashes,
grouping, omitted-face records and source-quality cautions.

This independent right adult-male limb is not registered to the whole-body atlas
or another patient. It has no nerve or vessel meshes; motor-supply teaching
describes relationships, not a rendered nerve course. Pelvic and foot-bone
groupings are not separately numbered anatomy. Many small muscles, ligamentous,
capsular and fascial structures are absent. Teaching and geometry require
revision-bound Visible Medicine radiologist review. No diagnostic use,
simulation-ready topology, scan synchronization or lecture entitlement is claimed.
