# Girdle-bone ultrasound orientation

Five previously pending Ultrasound notes now provide original surface-orientation
drafts for the existing left clavicle, left humerus, left scapula and both hip
bones. The existing right shoulder-pilot notes are deliberately unchanged. This
is regional teaching coverage, not five new anatomical models or acquired scans.

Each note is admitted only for its complete pinned source identity, including
side, region, bundle and source components. Renamed, moved, re-sourced or otherwise
altered records do not inherit it. Named landmarks remain teaching context on
whole bones, not separately validated selectable parts.

## References and limits

- [ESSR shoulder ultrasound guidance](https://www.essr.org/content-essr/uploads/2016/10/shoulder.pdf): bicipital groove, scapular fossae and acromioclavicular relationships.
- [ESSR hip ultrasound guidance](https://essr.org/content-essr/uploads/2016/10/hip.pdf): anterior iliac landmarks and proximal tendon relationships.
- [RSNA/ACR musculoskeletal ultrasound](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/musculous): adult bone-surface and depth limitations.

Checked 8 October 2026. Original short summaries, not copied source prose,
images or procedural instructions. No new external asset or dependency.
The lessons separate adult bone surfaces from marrow, model rotation from probe
