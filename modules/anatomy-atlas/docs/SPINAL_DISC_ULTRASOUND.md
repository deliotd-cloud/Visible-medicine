# Spinal-disc ultrasound orientation drafts

Three regional drafts fill nineteen previously pending Ultrasound placements:
five cervical, ten thoracic and four lumbar source-labelled whole-disc records.
This is not nineteen independent studies or a claim that all discs are routinely
visible on ultrasound. The already taught axis, first-thoracic and fifth-lumbar
disc selections remain unchanged, as do all other topics and held identities.

The resolver reuses exact full source identities from `spine-imaging-pins.json`.
It does not use a name/FMA-only fallback. Source anatomy, masks, scans and the
unresolved source disc level are unchanged. These notes distinguish anterior
landmarks, posterior acoustic access and experimental direct disc imaging.
They supply neither procedural guidance nor patient-space synchronization.

## Factual reading sources

- [Nwawka et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37813455/), DOI
  10.14444/8546. Ten healthy-volunteer feasibility study of prevertebral tissues
  and anterior vertebral surfaces, not validation of disc diagnosis. ISASS
  copyright; no figures, tables or publisher prose reused.
- [Avramescu et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22354105/), DOI
  10.1097/AAP.0b013e3182464be2. Sixty-one adult volunteers; endpoint concerns
  ligamentum-flavum/dura visibility, not complete disc depiction. Reading-only
  citation; no reproduction rights claimed and no assets imported.
- [Winn et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1177/87564793211008342). A cadaveric
  study of L4–L5/L5–S1 using transabdominal and direct ligament access; not a
  broad clinical diagnostic-accuracy study. SAGE reuse permissions apply to
  source material; none is reproduced here.
- [University of Toronto spinal ultrasound module](https://pieweb.med.utoronto.ca/OBAnesthesia/OBAnesthesia_content/OBA_spinalUltrasound_module.html).
  Teaching about grouped vertebral/ligament/dural interfaces is cited as factual
  context only. No images, videos, text or site assets are copied.

Original wording and code introduce no new third-party asset or mandatory fee.
Reading accessibility is not a commercial licence for publication materials.
The retained model licences and attribution are unchanged.
