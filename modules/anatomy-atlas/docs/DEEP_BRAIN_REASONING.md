# Deep-brain formative practice

This extension connects existing deep-brain dissection surfaces with the existing
**Practice → Apply anatomy** mode. It adds no permanent panel or navigation tab.
Six original draft concepts cover caudate, putamen, globus pallidus, thalamus,
lateral geniculate body and medial geniculate body. Each uses its two already
admitted, independently labelled source representations; the question bank must
not repeat left and right versions of the same concept within one session.

The earlier 126 concepts remain ordered and unchanged. With this addition the
bank has 132 concepts and 250 exact source representations; these counts are
question coverage, not unique complete anatomy or validated examination items.
Neural organs are explicitly matched to the catalogue's `nerves` system and
`organ` category. They do not relax the preceding muscle or visceral-organ gate.

## Use and scope

In Head & neck (or Whole body), expose a deep-brain study through Dissect. Then
enter Practice, choose Apply anatomy and Current focus targets only when it is
available. Available loaded source structures supply the questions and choices.
Left/right filtering remains respected. Removing a needed alternative can
reduce available questions; an isolated answer is not an honest practice set.
The existing All visible anatomy selection also includes other topics.

References and explanations appear after answering or skipping, not as hints
beforehand. Existing retry and answer-once rules remain. The complete thalamic
surface is not a distractor for a geniculate-body relay question, because that
would make a parent structure compete ambiguously with its own subdivision.

These are formative anatomy distinctions, not imaging-localisation tests,
diagnostic competence or accredited assessments. Surface shapes do not validate
subnuclear boundaries, pallidal segments, circuits, axons or patient registration.
Revision-bound radiologist and educator review remains required.
