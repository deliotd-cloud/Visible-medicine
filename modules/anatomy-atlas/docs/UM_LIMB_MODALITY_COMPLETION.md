# Independent lower-limb modality-topic completion

Additive draft teaching for the original UM lower-limb specimen, not new
segmentation, patient-image interpretation or an anatomical registration.

The baseline is Atlas `33566ee21aa65ed1a370a5e7653337048656a13e`.
Its 67 selectable surfaces contain 65 explicitly mapped extended lessons and
two unresolved grouped selections. The 235 existing clinical/pathology/imaging
topics, self-checks, model limitations, attachments and motor descriptions are
retained unchanged. Missing-only additions provide 54 CT, 29 MRI, 34 X-ray and
38 ultrasound notes: 155 unique placements, giving each mapped lesson all six
extended tabs. These are introductory drafts, not comprehensive clinical sign-off.

## Source identity and limitations

The original source frame, catalogues, declared bundle hashes, mesh bytes,
region studies, identifiers, navigation and dissection behavior are unchanged.
The pelvis group and source foot-bone group retain their grouped anatomy and
function descriptions; no individual identities or new extended topics are
invented. Missing nerves, vessels, separate tendon slips and other anatomy do
not become present because a teaching note mentions their relationships.

Notes distinguish bone assessment from direct muscle/tendon imaging, accessible
ultrasound windows from deep/intraosseous coverage, and routine CT from contrast
arthrography. They do not diagnose a displayed injury or imply scan registration.

## Reading links and commercial provenance

New notes are original factual prose. Primary reading links are not licences
to redistribute publisher text, figures, tables, PDFs or datasets. No reference
images, scans or other assets are imported. Existing UM CC0 mesh provenance and
credits remain separate. Earlier external references are retained, not newly
cleared by this batch. No new font, model, texture, dependency, paid service or
mandatory fee is introduced. The unchanged aggregate 200-word-per-URL guard
counts every extended note and self-check against its linked references.

## Review and integration contract

Teaching is explicitly repinned only after inspecting the additive content.
Repinning is not radiologist approval. The protected review adapter supplies
named reading links for UM material, bound into its teaching fingerprint.
Across five overlapping regional contexts, 154 review packets change: 150
receive new lessons and four grouped packets change reference metadata only.
The 202 unrelated packets and all 356 source identities must remain unchanged.
Stale teaching revisions/material hashes and foreign frames must be rejected
before storage. No approval, independent entitlement or imaging gate is relaxed.

Generate learner and Clinical Review website exports through the existing
pipeline; do not hand-edit generated website files. A local source save does
not mean integration or publication has occurred.

## Verification and remaining clinical work

`npm run um-topic-completion:test` checks exact preserved historical content,
missing-only coverage, foreign identity rejection, detached copies, reference
budgets and revision-bound review rejection. The established learning, navigation,
geometry/export, renderer, specimen review, TypeScript and regional build checks
remain required. Test passes are neither browser nor clinical acceptance.

The owner radiologist must review each note against the source region, verify
relationships and modality limitations, check terminology and reading links,
and accept only the exact revision/scope shown in Clinical Review. Group identities,
missing anatomy, deeper arterial/nerve relationships, pathology breadth, actual
GPU/keyboard/accessibility presentation and cleared CT/MRI/X-ray/US registration
remain separate requirements. Clinical scans/masks stay in specialist ownership;
Didanix Education/light linking must not change the desktop clinical application.
