# Right orbital nerve orientation tour

This draft tour introduces six selected right sided surfaces in order: ophthalmic (V1), frontal, lacrimal, nasociliary, long ciliary and ciliary ganglion. Each stop selects its target while the corrected right eyeball remains a faded orientation landmark. Stops last 14 seconds and use the viewer's existing 1.8 second smooth camera transition. The tour changes no mesh geometry or source coordinates.

The anatomical teaching uses the [Texas Tech eye anatomy table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/nervous_system/eye_tables.html) and [autonomics module](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/modules/intro_autonomics_2_module/autonomics_09.html) as factual reading references. Captions are original short summaries, not copied prose or licensed publisher media. Existing per-structure quiz and imaging notes remain available at stops. The final stop contrasts a separate relay; the sequence does not imply a continuous pathway.

The display uses selected source exterior surfaces and partial courses. Geometry and boundaries remain anatomically unvalidated. Mesh proximity does not verify nerve continuity, a synapse, fibre tracing, a complete orbit, a functional or nuclear map, or patient registration. The ganglion source lacks reconstructed roots and connecting branches. This educational draft requires radiologist sign-off tied to the exact source and tour revision; automated tests do not grant clinical approval.

`content/orbital-nerve-tour-pins.json` binds the six targets, corrected globe, coordinate system and three display bundles to baseline `0bb3cf88d6862a605f7cb54bd315b90cd38843e5`. `lib/orbital-nerve-tour.ts` captures the immutable tour signature and checks exact structure and bundle identities. The focused check is `node scripts/test-orbital-nerve-tour.mjs --source-only`; after registration, use the full command without that flag to compare the prior 38 tours and review evidence against the new 39-tour state. This source check is not browser, GPU, radiologist or deployment evidence.
