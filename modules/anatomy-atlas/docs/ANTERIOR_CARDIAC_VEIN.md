FJ2730 surfaces: 730 triangles, one stable selection, no inferred branch names.
Thorax and Whole body expose it through search and the existing system/layer,
selection, isolate, separation and removal controls. **Cardiac veins & heart
surface** adds one collapsed Study choice, comparing the group with the existing
heart, right coronary trunk, great cardiac vein and middle cardiac vein. Remove
the heart and Undo; rotate posteriorly for the middle vein. No extra toolbar.

Measured-box tests cover both sides, top/bottom boundaries and neighbours; the
actual cardiac-vein browser journey checks clearance at desktop/mobile sizes.

## Source and anatomical limits

The official v4 ISA ZIP directory, member CRCs/sizes and SHA-256s are checked.
Both components are closed consistently oriented combinatorial manifolds, with
no duplicate/degenerate faces reported. All 1,816 source files belonging to the
previous 1,103 root, 104 nested and nine shoulder selections were hash-checked
and screened. No exact or translated-similarity duplicate was found. Thirty-six
comparisons include neighbouring heart and coronary surfaces. Sparse proximity
measurements and topology do **not** prove self-intersection freedom, separate
lumens, normal relationships or anatomical validity. The two components touch
closely; they remain separate unchanged surfaces, never welded or bridged.

The local four-view source review shows the group in the anterior cardiac region.
Original vertex order, face order and common transform are retained; Float32
storage and recomputed shading normals are the only export changes. Exact
round-trip arrays are checked. The archived main catalogue and every previous
bundle/selection remain unchanged. The supplemental record is admitted atomically
against complete context records and bundles, not by name/FMA alone.

Owner-radiologist review must assess source identity and extent, apparent contact
with the right coronary artery/right atrium, crossing depth, component junction,
absence of demonstrated ostia, and whether this diagrammatic source is useful
for the intended teaching. No drainage territory, vein count, dominance, flow,
patient registration, diagnostic accuracy or safe procedural plane is established.
