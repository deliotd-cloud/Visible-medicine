# Axial connective clinical teaching

## Scope — 9 September 2026

Seventy-two original Clinical/Pathology drafts cover 36 existing surfaces: 14 costal cartilages and 22 intervertebral discs. Four teaching groups retain separate selectable identities: bilateral ribs one to seven, six cervical discs, 11 thoracic discs and five lumbar discs.

Clinical context distinguishes chest-wall pain from emergencies, cervical radiculopathy from myelopathy, thoracic cord-related concerns and lumbar root-related symptoms. These are introductory regional lessons, not exhaustive diagnoses attached to each disc or an assertion that every cartilage is equally susceptible to a condition.

No UI controls, geometry, dependencies, scans, database writes or review approvals are added. Existing Anatomy and Function content remains unchanged. A whole-disc mesh does not independently expose annulus, nucleus or endplates. Source vertebral names are NOT validated radiological level annotations. Missing levels are neither interpolated nor silently renamed, and the 11 thoracic surfaces do not prove complete thoracic disc coverage.
