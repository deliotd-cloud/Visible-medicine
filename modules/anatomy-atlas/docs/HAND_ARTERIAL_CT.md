# Hand arterial CT/CTA orientation draft

Scope: six original teaching topics bound to the 26 existing hand arterial selections pending CT: two deep arches, two grouped palmar metacarpal selections, two princeps pollicis selections, two radialis indicis selections, eight common palmar digital selections and ten proper palmar digital selections. The existing superficial-arch CT/MRI teaching and every other topic remain outside this addition. No source geometry, case data or image asset is added.

The teaching distinguishes a contrast-timed CTA from routine CT. The static source surfaces are orientation aids, with no scan registration, lumen measurement, flow or collateral test. Small branches may not resolve on a study; nonvisibility alone does not prove absence or occlusion. Source sections should be checked when projections overlap. Set separation to zero before comparing model relationships.

Source labels and inventory need care: each palmar metacarpal selection is grouped, rather than a numbered branch set; the two meshes within each princeps or radialis selection identify one source vessel. The common digital first–fourth labels are historical source numbering, not a validated standard inventory. The ten proper digital selections cover six right and four left only.

References for factual orientation: [TTUHSC upper-limb arterial anatomy](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html) and [the authored upper-extremity CTA review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11736060/). Text is newly written; no figures, tables, scans or prose are reproduced. References grant no figure or image licence.
