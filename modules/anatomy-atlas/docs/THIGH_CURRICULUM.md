# Hip and thigh muscle teaching drafts

## Scope

Twenty-seven definitions supply **108 new Anatomy/Function drafts for 54 existing bilateral muscle, head and portion entries**. All selected source names, explicit FMA identities, component counts and existing coverage notes are retained. Overlapping regional views show the same lessons without duplicating identities. No new mesh, control, scan, dependency or clinical approval is introduced.

The batch covers adductors, gluteals, deep rotators, iliacus/psoas major, sartorius, gracilis, tensor fasciae latae, quadriceps and posterior-thigh entries. Notes distinguish biceps femoris heads and their joint crossings/motor supplies; adductor-magnus portions and overlapping innervation; iliacus versus psoas supply; gemelli and obturator nerves; shared quadriceps tendon/patellar ligament; and pes-anserine versus semimembranosus attachments. Adductor minimus is explicitly a variably separate portion, not proof of a universal boundary from magnus.

Typical attachments are not verified mesh footprints. Named limb nerves are textual teaching, not rendered courses. Motion, fibre subdivisions, tendon slips, variants and patient registration are not modelled by these notes. Clinical, pathology, CT/MRI/ultrasound and quiz topics remain unchanged.

Current body totals: Anatomy 343 draft / 679 identity-only; Function 399 draft / 146 identity-only / 477 pending. Thigh's 81 entries have 56 draft Anatomy / 25 identity-only and 56 draft Function / 20 generic / 5 pending. These are editorial counts, not complete anatomy or clinical acceptance.

- [Texas Tech lower-limb table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_lowerlimb.html): selected obturator, quadratus and pectineus facts/cross-checks.

These are factual teaching references, not source-mesh validation. Their broader explanations include inconsistencies: a biceps short head does not extend the hip, minimus and medius have different iliac origin intervals, and fixed nerve/root/variant maps must not be inferred from generic descriptions. Precise anatomy and wording require specialist review. A source citation does not endorse every statement on that page.

Only original brief factual explanations are authored. No textbook chapter, table dataset, illustration, scan, translation or question bank is redistributed. StatPearls' CC BY-NC-ND material is **not** admitted as commercial assets. Texas Tech/research rights remain separate. Original MIT code/text and existing BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0/DBCLS obligations remain; citations do not license copying. No new font, texture, model, library, paid API or runtime reference-fetching service is added.
