# Forearm superficial-vein MR orientation drafts

23 September 2026. Four new MRI draft placements cover the right and left cephalic veins (FMA13325/13326) and basilic veins (FMA22909/22910). They retain their Forearm and Shoulder & arm navigation. CT remains pending for all four selections because the checked primary evidence does not establish useful superficial forearm CT visibility for this source. Existing ultrasound drafts and all other lessons are unchanged.

The drafts distinguish superficial cephalic and basilic routes from deep brachial veins. MR venography is an acquisition family, not a property of any ordinary forearm MRI. Visibility is sequence-dependent. The atlas has only named source surfaces, not MR signal, vascular lumina, flow, calibre or patency. No patient scan is registered.

## Evidence and limits

- [UAMS selected upper-limb veins](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/vein-tables/selected-veins-of-the-upper-limb/) supplies the lateral cephalic and medial basilic superficial relationships. Its table, diagrams and wording are not reproduced.
- [Harigai et al., Clinical Imaging 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22920352/), DOI 10.1016/j.clinimag.2012.01.016, compares noncontrast fresh-blood and time-of-flight upper-limb MR venography. The abstract reports better basilic-vein and small-branch depiction with fresh-blood imaging and equivalent cephalic-vein depiction. It does not validate routine forearm MR series, all venous variants or diagnosis in this atlas.
- [Upper-limb cross-sectional MRV mapping study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12598988/) supplies forearm cephalic context in a pre-fistula mapping cohort. Its cohort-specific patency findings are not transferred to the atlas or a patient.
- [DETECT prospective CT venography study](https://www.jthjournal.org/article/S1538-7836%2825%2900533-1/fulltext) evaluates PICC-related deep upper-extremity thrombosis and reports some upper-limb segments as poorly assessed. Its cohort and deep-vein endpoint cannot establish cephalic/basilic forearm CT depiction. No CT lesson is promoted from it.

All copy is original synthesis with outbound references. No scans, diagrams, tables, publisher wording or datasets are imported. References are not licences for third-party media; BodyParts3D attribution and source licensing remain unchanged. These drafts add no fee, access rule or dependency.
