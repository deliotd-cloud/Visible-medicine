# Upper-cervical muscle X-ray context

Six existing root-body selections now have an original X-ray teaching draft:
the paired longus capitis, rectus capitis anterior and rectus capitis lateralis.
Three distinct lessons orient the C3–C6-to-occiput, atlas-lateral-mass-to-occiput
and atlas-transverse-process-to-jugular-process relationships respectively.
These are anatomical orientation facts, not newly validated attachment geometry.

The notes distinguish those 3D relationships from a lateral radiograph's regional
prevertebral soft-tissue contour. They do not claim separate visible muscle
outlines, a labelled X-ray mask, attachment integrity or guaranteed CT/MRI
visibility. The contour/boundary cautions are educational inferences from source
anatomy and projection/measurement evidence, not a study validating identification
of each source mesh on radiographs. No numeric trauma threshold or clinical
imaging recommendation is encoded. Normal regional thickness cannot establish
an individual muscle's integrity or exclude injury. Source-level, population and
technique limits remain explicit; radiographic norms are not transferred to CT.
