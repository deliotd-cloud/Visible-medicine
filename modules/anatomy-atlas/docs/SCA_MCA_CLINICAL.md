# Superior cerebellar and right middle cerebral artery teaching

17 September 2026. Six Clinical/Pathology drafts for three admitted selections:
right SCA FMA50574/FJ1726, left SCA FMA50575/FJ1726M, and right MCA FMA50082
with FJ1662/FJ1663/FJ1692. No new geometry or datasets. The SCA sources each
retain one connected component and their original small proximal midline crossing.
The MCA is a PART-OF representation with six disconnected components, not a
complete vessel, named angiographic segments or permission to invent a left MCA.
File order is source identity order, never an anatomical sequence.

The existing panels distinguish clinical context from what the surface actually
shows: gait disturbance and posterior-fossa swelling for SCA, right-hemisphere
spatial neglect and arterial obstruction for MCA. They are introductory drafts,
not full vascular-neurology teaching, diagnosis, screening or treatment guidance.
