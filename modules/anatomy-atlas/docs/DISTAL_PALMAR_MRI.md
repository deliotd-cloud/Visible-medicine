# Distal palmar arterial MRI teaching

Six previously pending MRI sections now have three original paired draft texts:
palmar metacarpal artery, princeps pollicis and radialis indicis. Existing arch and
digital-artery lessons remain unchanged. Content is bound to each full source
identity, including ordered component files; generic metacarpal groups are not
renamed as numbered arteries and paired-component sources are not split.

The primary reference is [Lim et al., 2009, initial hand MRA experience](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2734893/).
Only short factual summaries are used; no publisher images, tables, scans or
copied passages are imported. Teaching concerns specialised MRA, not routine
hand MRI or a recommended acquisition protocol. Geometry cannot establish flow,
patency, a joined lumen, collateral adequacy or a patient-specific origin.

The owner's revision-bound radiologist review remains required. Atlas, imaging
case and paid lecture permissions remain independent; no real imaging connection
or approval is created. Unsupported CT/US sections retain their prior state.

`npm run distal-palmar-mri:test` checks exact source pins, historical transition,
all 9,930 unchanged topics/recipes, review binding, malformed-identity rejection,
and conservative factual-prose limits. Published/browser status is recorded in
the dated coordination checkpoint, not inferred from source tests.
