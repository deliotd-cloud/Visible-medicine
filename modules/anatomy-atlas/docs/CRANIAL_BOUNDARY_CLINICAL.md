# Tentorium and lamina terminalis clinical drafts

Three new draft placements use the existing Clinical panel in head/neck and
whole-body: tentorium Clinical/Pathology and lamina terminalis Clinical.
No extra control, model, scan, dependency or paid service is introduced.

## Source and evidence boundaries

- Tentorium FMA83966/FJ1843 is a right-sided portion of an unpaired fold.
  The complete notch, opposite side, attachment footprints and dural venous
  sinuses are not supplied. Clinical concerns lesion localisation; Pathology
  distinguishes an imaging observation from established involvement.
- Lamina terminalis FMA61975/FJ1764+FJ1812 comprises two original pieces, not a
  reconstructed continuous membrane. Clinical addresses individual variation.
  Existing CT/MRI drafts stay unchanged. Pathology stays pending: normal-cohort
  observations do not establish a disease-specific lesson.

## Reading references and commercial reuse

- [Soffar et al., 2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41983-021-00340-1):
  lesion attachment/extension context. Publisher CC BY 4.0 verified; no figures,
  patient images, tables or source prose imported.
- [Helie et al., 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8636802/): abstract consulted
  for the limitation of adjacent linear enhancement. No full-text or commercial
  redistribution right claimed. Involvement is not histological invasion.
- [Hoz et al., 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41952733/): abstract consulted
  for variability/chiasm association, not disease or operative guidance.
  Publisher media are not admitted as commercial assets; no figure, dataset,
  measurement table, angle threshold or source prose redistributed.

Short original factual notes and reading links, not copies of publications.
Original code/text retain MIT terms; BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 attribution remains.
Citation does not license linked media.

## Binding, verification and radiologist review

Complete immutable source identities and exact supported topics gate rendering.
Changed sides, source members/order, coordinates, coverage or review state fail
closed. Historical snapshots preserve all other topics, shoulder teaching and
dissection recipes; prior baselines/transitions are not rewritten.
`cranial-boundary-clinical:test` checks identity mutations, fresh arrays, mixed
and unrecorded transitions, source bytes and all body content-schema records.

Owner radiologist review must identify the exact note/source revision and check
clinical scope, terminology and model limitations. Geometry remains unvalidated;
references and tests do not confer clinical approval. No patient registration,
safe surgical route, diagnostic threshold or CSF-flow simulation is claimed.
Atlas, Didanix Education cases and paid lectures remain independently entitled.
Private-data clearance, hosted review, model staging and device acceptance remain
separate gates. Actual browser/recovery evidence belongs in the checkpoint.
