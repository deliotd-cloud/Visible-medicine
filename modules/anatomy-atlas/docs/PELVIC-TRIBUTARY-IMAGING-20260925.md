## Evidence boundaries

- [Hamid et al., iliolumbar anatomy/venograms](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17373712/)
  supports variable drainage and terminology, not MRI/duplex branch visibility.
- [Berberoglu et al., corona mortis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11210207/)
  supports superior-pubic-ramus venous connections; no such channel is newly
  represented by the obturator mesh or guaranteed on MRI/ultrasound.
- [Hare et al., presacral cadaveric anatomy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40168452/)
  supports sacral-foraminal/piriformis-fascial context and communicating veins.
  Its female-cadaver distances are not transferred to this reference donor,
  converted into operative margins, or presented as imaging validation.
- [Asciutto et al., pelvic MR venography](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18718774/)
  concerns ovarian/hypogastric/pelvic-plexus assessment in symptomatic women,
  not detection accuracy for these small named tributaries.
- [Bhatt et al., transvaginal duplex](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40537052/)
  concerns ovarian/internal-iliac reflux in women. No scanning route, numerical
  diagnostic threshold or branch-level accuracy is transferred to this model.

to copy figures; no images, copyrighted passages or scan data were imported.
