## Teaching and evidence boundaries

Separate routine foot MRI from dedicated pedal MRA. Use acquired source sections
to establish side, orientation and branch continuity before comparing a source
surface. Projections, red surface colour, explode or clipping are not evidence
of lumen, patency, signal, patient registration or an acquisition plane.

The five anatomical landmarks distinguish the posterior tibial plantar division,
lateral contribution to the arch, dorsal-to-plantar connection and independently
labelled superficial branch. No absent digital branch or plantar nerve is added.
Arch contribution varies; source geometry remains unvalidated for clinical use.

Primary-reference scope, with original short summaries only:

- [TTUHSC lower-limb artery table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_lowerlimb.html): anatomical relationships, not copied tables or figures.
- [Gabrielli et al., arch constitution/topography](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11694970/): cadaveric variation, not this donor's measured lumen.
- [Papon et al., deep plantar anatomy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9787393/): dorsal-to-plantar connection through the first intermetatarsal space; not clinical clearance of a bypass target.
- [Unger et al., foot/ankle MRA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7696797/): an older time-of-flight comparison in a selected disease cohort, not validation of routine joint MRI or every fine branch.
- [McDermott et al., distal-extremity MRA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7489621/): acquisition-specific difficulty depicting arch/retrograde flow; signal loss is not automatically occlusion.
- [Liu et al., unenhanced foot MRA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24758556/): dedicated study with nondiagnostic segments affected by motion, weak signal or venous contamination. Performance for named larger vessels does not separately validate our deep or superficial branch surfaces.

The last statement is a limit on transferring the study's assessment to the
catalogue, not a claim that those branches cannot ever be seen. No patient
measurement, diagnostic cutoff, sequence prescription, contrast advice,
revascularisation target or surgical safe zone is inferred.

## Source, access and commercial-use boundaries

`content/plantar-arterial-mri-pins.json` captures the complete independent
identities and the pre-change teaching snapshot at source `e6168496`. Admission
requires exact identity equality, not just matching name/FMA. Both original
sides remain independently sourced; none is mirrored or connected artificially.

Only original app text and reading links are added. No external images, article
text, tables, meshes, textures, fonts, dependencies or API fees are introduced.
Publisher/TTUHSC works are references, not redistributable product assets.
Existing MIT app and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 source notices/credit remain required.
This is not an exhaustive legal opinion or a free-hosting guarantee.

Atlas, case and paid-lecture entitlements remain independent. No learning
resource mapping, acquired scan or viewer approval is introduced. Didanix
Education/light remains the future learner-image target; desktop PACS and
CT-head masks/accepted boundaries stay out of scope.

## Validation and radiologist review

Run `node scripts/pin-plantar-arterial-mri.mjs --check`, then
`node scripts/test-plantar-arterial-mri.mjs`. The validator compares actual
current lessons with the immutable pre-change resolver: ten MRI placements,
9,926 other topics, ten changed teaching worksheets and 1,094 unaffected
fingerprints. It exercises changed identities, malformed Review packets and
stale submissions before storage. Historical shoulder assertions replay their
completed source epoch; current foot lessons are tested separately.

The ten changed teaching worksheets need fresh revision-bound radiologist
sign-off. Review terminology, donor extent/shape, anatomical relationships,
case-independent MRI/MRA distinctions and each reference's scope. Actual image
examples require separate rights/privacy clearance, adequate source sequences
and validated structure correspondence. No software test supplies this approval.

Pending Ultrasound/X-ray sections and dorsal venous arches are not made draft
merely to improve coverage counts. Source holds stay intact. Generated website
import, browser/GPU acceptance and publication each need their own evidence.
