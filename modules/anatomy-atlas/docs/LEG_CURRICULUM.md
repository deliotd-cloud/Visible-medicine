# Lower-leg muscle teaching drafts

## Scope

Fourteen definitions provide 56 new Anatomy/Function drafts for 28 existing bilateral muscle/head entries. The existing panel explains compartment, typical attachments, action and motor supply, retaining exact names, FMA IDs, component counts and coverage warnings. No meshes, controls, dependencies, scans, nerve routes or clinical approvals are added.

The drafts distinguish gastrocnemius heads from soleus, anterior fibularis tertius from lateral longus/brevis, digital flexor/extensor attachments, tibialis anterior from posterior, and popliteus's free-tibia versus fixed-tibia action. Plantaris variation and unsegmented tendon slips remain explicit. Textual compartments and attachments are not validated fascial boundaries or measured surface footprints; actions are not motion simulations.

Body totals: Anatomy 371 draft / 651 identity-only; Function 427 draft / 146 identity-only / 449 pending. The 60-entry leg region has 30 draft Anatomy / 30 identity-only and 32 draft Function / 18 generic / 10 pending. All 28 represented leg muscles have basic drafts. Specialist topics and quizzes remain unchanged, not clinically complete.

## Factual references and rights

Checked 7 September 2026; each lesson links its sources:

- [Anterior leg](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539725/) and [tibialis anterior](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK513304/).
- [Lateral leg](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519526/) and selected [Texas Tech lower-limb facts](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_lowerlimb.html).
- [Posterior leg](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537340/), [calf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459362/), [popliteus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK526084/) and [tibialis posterior](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539913/).

These are teaching references, not mesh validation. Attachment extents and variants differ between descriptions. The drafts avoid fixed fractional origins where references differ, do not call the hallux joint a proximal interphalangeal joint, and do not generalize posterior-compartment plantarflexion to popliteus. No universal root maps, prevalence, diagnostic rules or treatments are inferred.

Only original brief factual teaching is authored. No chapter, table dataset, figure, scan or question bank is copied/imported. StatPearls CC BY-NC-ND material is not a commercially admitted asset; Texas Tech retains copyright. Citations do not license copying. Original MIT code/text and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0/DBCLS obligations remain separate. No font, texture, package, paid API or runtime reference-fetching service is added.
