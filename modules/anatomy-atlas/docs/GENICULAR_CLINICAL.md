# Genicular artery clinical drafts

Ten existing bilateral genicular-artery selections now provide source-bound
Clinical and Pathology drafts through the existing teaching panel. Five paired
families distinguish the middle, superior medial/lateral and inferior
medial/lateral source labels. The notes do not assert donor-specific junctions,
continuous lumens, perfusion territories, a complete network, a variant,
embolisation targets, injury, diagnosis or treatment.

The same exact selections remain available in the Leg and Thigh workspaces.
Their Anatomy, Function and Quiz teaching, source identities, coordinates,
arterial-connection guides, separation behavior and 403,484-byte
`genicular-arteries` bundle are unchanged. The two disconnected components in
each middle-genicular source remain unbridged. CT, MRI, X-ray and Ultrasound
remain pending: 40 fallback bodies distinguish the Clinical and Pathology
drafts from unreviewed modality teaching. No scan or modality lesson was added.

`npm run genicular-clinical:test` checks all 10 canonical identities, 60 declared
payload changes (20 new drafts and 40 pending-copy corrections), all 20 actual
React note renders, changed-source and changed-topic rejection, the 1,102-record
schema, exact before/after reconstruction, mixed-state and unrelated-drift
rejection, unchanged recipes and geometry, and exact bundle bytes/hash. Its
machine-readable report is `docs/genicular-clinical-validation.json`.

Reference and reuse evidence is recorded separately by the main task. These are
introductory educational drafts, not anatomical validation, clinical approval,
browser/device acceptance or publication. Revision-bound radiologist review and
independent Atlas, case and paid-lecture access remain required.
