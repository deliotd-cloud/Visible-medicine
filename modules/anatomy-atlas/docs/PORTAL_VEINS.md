## Compact portal exploration
Select a supported vein and expand **Portal venous drainage**. Eleven selections combine the five additions with existing portal, superior/inferior mesenteric, ileal and middle/right colic sources. Ten original factual relationships provide reciprocal receives-from/drains-towards navigation. **Show available veins & bones** isolates immediate neighbours with abdominal bone context in one reversible visibility step. Selection is retained; separation/cutaway/camera reset follows the existing systemic workflow. Hide individual context objects and use Undo as elsewhere. No new permanent control or stage is added.

IMV-to-splenic and middle-colic-to-SMV routes are explicitly examples, not universal or donor-confirmed terminations. Right gastroepiploic/right colic collecting routes remain qualified. The generically labelled pancreaticoduodenal source is excluded rather than assigned an invented superior/inferior outlet. Portal inflow is not directly connected to hepatic veins or the inferior cava; liver sinusoids and portal-systemic collaterals are not supplied. The previous systemic map, same-side pairing and selection behaviour are preserved.
