# Kidneys to bladder: cross-region Guided learning

This five-stop draft belongs in Whole body → Guided learning. It selects the
right kidney and ureter, then their left counterparts, and finally the urinary
bladder. Each unilateral stop frames its kidney, ureter and the bladder; the
last stop shows all five. The existing player provides pause, previous/next,
step selection, reading references and the smooth 1.8-second quintic-orbit
camera transition. Reduced-motion support is unchanged.

The tour joins existing abdomen and pelvis selections from the same root source;
it neither combines independent specimens nor changes meshes or coordinates.
Both ureters are recovered source surfaces. Their proximity to other surfaces
does not establish continuous lumens, patency, collecting-system anatomy,
ureteric orifices, flow or peristalsis. The male root specimen is not a validated
female pelvic model. No acquired imaging or patient-space registration is added.

Captions are original concise factual summaries of the
[Texas Tech kidney and retroperitoneum table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/gastrointestinal_system/kidney_tables.html),
not copies of its prose, images or tables. No new dependency, asset licence or
fee is introduced. Existing model attribution obligations remain in force.

Source baseline: `cb412b50a1415c5662a346e509b23326799247f9`.
The tour pins five full display records, three bundles, the source version,
coordinate system and licence. Changes or ambiguous duplicate identities refuse
the tour. Clinical Review binds the complete sequence and its camera frames,
so previous teaching approval cannot carry over to this revision.

Focused validation: `npm run urinary-tract-tour:test`, plus the existing guided
library, player, camera, review and content checks. Automated checks establish
contracts, not clinical acceptance or actual GPU/browser appearance. Radiologist
review of these exact surfaces and captions remains required before approval.
