# Orbital and neck muscle imaging — draft teaching

## Scope

Adds 112 original orientation notes to 42 retained, explicitly sided selections:
42 CT, 42 MRI and 28 ultrasound. Seven orbital pairs cover recti, obliques and
levator; six neck pairs cover subclavius, platysma, scalenes and sternocleidomastoid;
eight hyoid-related pairs cover digastric, mylohyoid, geniohyoid, stylohyoid and
the four strap muscles. All pairs are left first, right second in the curricula.
Existing attachments and representation cautions are reused rather than recoded.

The 14 orbital ultrasound topics remain pending: no generic normal-image or
routine visibility claim substitutes for an appropriately reviewed ocular-US
lesson. Cervical rotatores already have spine teaching and are not overwritten.
Tongue, palate and intrinsic laryngeal detail remain separate lower-priority work.
This is not a complete orbital/neck radiology curriculum or a new scan collection.

## Learner experience and identity

Select one of the existing muscles in head/neck or whole-body 3D, then open its
existing CT, MRI or Ultrasound tab. No toolbar, route or permanent panel is added.
Exact complete source identities bind the notes; altered side, bounds, source
hash, bundle, category or catalogue identity must not acquire a lesson.
No source positions, meshes, dissection recipes or clinical decisions change.
Explode distance does not represent muscle contraction or patient anatomy.

Important distinctions include superior rectus versus levator, the superior
oblique trochlear turn, the anterior origin of inferior oblique, the second-rib
attachment of posterior scalene, geniohyoid versus genioglossus, and thyroid
cartilage versus thyroid gland. Digastric's left three/right two source files
are not belly counts; omohyoid's single file is not a separately validated sling.

## Evidence and licensing

Original brief synthesis, not copied articles, figures, tables, protocols or
datasets. Source links remain under their publishers' terms; PMC access is not
a blanket commercial-redistribution licence. Existing mesh licences and notices
remain intact. No dependency, image, font, texture, paid API or service is added.
No source scan, mask or private patient derivative is used.

Reading references checked on 13 September 2026:

- [Microscopy-coil orbital MRI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7342734/): plane-specific depiction and limits of routine resolution.
- [Extraocular muscle enlargement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9581877/): muscle/tendon/complex assessment and non-specific enlargement. Indexed publication text was available; a subsequent full-page request presented a browser check, which was not bypassed.
- [Imaging in orbital trauma](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3729297/): CT appearance does not settle clinical entrapment.
- [Mechanics of the orbit](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2268111/): gaze and pulley-dependent geometry, not a validated atlas motion simulation.
- [Infrahyoid sonoanatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3552675/) and [thyroid-region ultrasound anatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4499539/): superficial/deep muscle landmarks and limited windows.
- [Posterior-triangle sonoanatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3063345/): scalene and neurovascular relationships; no procedure is taught here.
- [Suprahyoid sonoanatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3558093/): submental layers and lateral landmarks.
- [Sublingual/submandibular imaging](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5990998/) and [mylohyoid pseudolesions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7973941/): compartments, posterior communication and variant tissue.
- [Normal brachial-plexus sonographic mapping](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7973677/): subclavius landmark.
- [Subclavius posticus case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4349834/): distinguish a reported variant from the ordinary muscle; not population prevalence or a diagnostic rule.
- [Omohyoid ultrasound study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10468463/): superior-belly identification, not atlas-derived swallowing measurements.

Existing curriculum references supply the retained original attachment facts.
The validator budgets each distinct new referenced fact once (shared left/right
display is not a new article summary). No verbatim source passages are imported.

all 1,101 displayed schema records, 112 changed and 9,797 preserved topics, source

runtime migrations and cannot transfer clinical approval.

to the trauma paper; the new, uncommitted transition was amended only for those
two hashes. No previously committed baseline or expected historical hash changed.
The accompanying validation report records final executed checks, not intent.
This turn adds no new browser/device evidence; no scan is linked by these tests.

muscle/tendon and fascial limitations, scan-plane descriptions, ocular motility
versus structural imaging, entrapment wording, small-muscle identification,
mylohyoid variants and superficial/deep neck relationships. Verify the retained
models against reference anatomy separately from these notes. Real device and
Education journeys remain open; never infer diagnosis or clinical acceptance.

case and paid-lecture rights stay independent; a generic donor model has no
patient registration. CT-head masks and accepted margins stay with that task.
