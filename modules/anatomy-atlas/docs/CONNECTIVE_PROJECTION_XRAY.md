# Wrist and long plantar X-ray orientation

Four exact existing BodyParts3D selections gain source-bound original drafts:
FMA40120/40121 (right/left wrist flexor retinaculum) and FMA44249/44250
(right/left long plantar ligament). Baseline: ac71406105da66f77115117bbab2c38428f7e5c3.

These are explicitly **indirect bony orientation**, not direct ligament
visibility. They appear through the existing Imaging → X-ray panel; no permanent
controls, images, overlays, quizzes, meshes or patient-space correspondences are
added. Anatomical names/attachment neighborhoods must not certify the recovered
surface's boundaries, integrity or a diagnostic result. Wrist terminology stays
qualified rather than silently renaming the source mesh.

## Evidence and rights

Primary references read 16 October 2026:

- [Stecco et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20346594/): dissection,
  histology and MRI correlation. Not a plain-film or source-mesh validation study.
- [Aggarwal et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7911075/): an
  exceptional single case, not sensitivity/prevalence or a routine imaging sign.
- [Ebraheim et al., 1999](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/107110079902000307):
  prepared cadaveric bony radiographic experiment, not ligament visualization.
- [Edama et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7792160/): dissection,
  not in-vivo radiographic diagnostic performance.

Research facts were synthesized into original project-MIT text. Citation/public
access does not clear media rights. No images, figures, article prose, tables,
measurements or patient examples were copied. Existing geometry retains its
BodyParts3D CC BY4.0 terms and DBCLS attribution; no asset/dependency or fee added.
The narrower four-source synthesis omits unverified additional guideline and
neighboring-ligament claims from the research proposals.

## Source and review safeguards

The resolver reuses full geometry identities from the unchanged connective and
foot-surface pins, not their old MRI/US topic permissions. It refuses any changed
identity, side, source component, bounds, metadata or added field, and supplies
fresh arrays. Only X-ray for these four records changes; other placements,
geometry, tours, nested teaching, source holds and independent entitlements remain.

Normal generation refreshes teaching snapshots and Review fingerprints. Draft
readiness is not clinical approval; no decision is migrated or written. Old
worksheets/teaching revisions must reject before storage. Radiologist/editor
review remains required for wording, landmarks/projection limitations, bilateral
use and exact source surfaces. No spatial registration is established.

Reproduce with `npm run connective-projection-xray:test`, normal revision,
content-contract and requirements checks. Browser/GPU and clinical acceptance
remain separate from automated source/refusal tests.
