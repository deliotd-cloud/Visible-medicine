# Thoracic mediastinal arterial X ray teaching

Five existing selections now have original angiographic orientation drafts:
brachiocephalic trunk, bronchial artery, variant-labelled bronchial artery,
oesophageal artery and the grouped oesophageal branches of the thoracic aorta.
Select one in Thorax or Whole body and open X-ray. Clinical Review exposes its
exact revised teaching packet; no extra toolbar or acquired scan is added.

## Anatomy and examination scope

The brachiocephalic trunk is distinguished from the paired veins and its usual
right-sided destinations are not inferred from the source's midline field.
Bronchial notes distinguish systemic from pulmonary supply. The variant source
retains its FMA14177 alias without becoming an additional selection. Grouped
oesophageal branches remain grouped, not newly named individual arteries.

Contrast catheter angiography and digital subtraction angiography use X-rays;
plain chest radiographs, CT angiography and MR angiography are not equivalent
examinations. Native source surfaces do not supply a contrast-filled lumen,
patient-side marker, procedural route or validated patient projection.

## Reading references and rights

[UAMS thoracic artery teaching](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/artery-tables/arteries-of-the-thorax/)
supports the usual branch destinations and supply distinctions. A
[25-cadaver arch study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2588331/)
documents anatomical variation, not the identity of this donor surface.
[RadiologyInfo catheter angiography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiocath)
supports the contrast X-ray examination distinction.

The [600-patient haemoptysis study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8183775/)
supports demonstrated bronchial origins in a selected disease cohort, not a
routine normal visibility rate. The
[five-patient oesophageal bleeding series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2702045/)
supports examples of demonstrated arterial feeders, not universal anatomy or
procedural guidance. The latter two publications have non-commercial media
reuse terms. No publisher wording, figure, table, image, scan or dataset is
reproduced or adapted; original factual summaries and citations do not grant
permission to reuse their media. New prose/code use project MIT terms. Existing
model notices remain intact; no new asset, dependency or mandatory fee is added.

## Revision bound review

The resolver reuses the unchanged identities in
`content/thoracic-branch-imaging-pins.json`. Source components, hashes, geometry,
coordinates, grouped labels, laterality and validation status must match exactly.
The five X-ray lessons and teaching fingerprints change from source 792d6623;
all other topics, model identities and unrelated review packets remain unchanged.
Earlier decisions cannot approve the revised teaching.

The radiologist should check branch wording, identity limitations, projection
distinctions and the interpretation of selected disease cohorts at each exact
revised packet. The notes remain drafts, not clinical or release approval.
Accepted CT/MRI masks, private scans, held anatomy, independent lecture/case
access and the desktop clinical PACS are unchanged.
