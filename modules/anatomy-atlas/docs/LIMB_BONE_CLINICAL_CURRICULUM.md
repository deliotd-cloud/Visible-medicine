## Evidence and commercial-use boundary

- AAOS OrthoInfo: clavicle, scapular, hip, pelvic, acetabular, proximal-tibial and patellar fracture pages. These support the location, associated-injury and functional distinctions, not atlas validation.
- AO Surgery Reference: adult forearm clinical/radiographic examination, Galeazzi and Monteggia definitions, and the adult proximal-fibular/medial ankle-injury definition. Only brief factual relationships are synthesised; no classification tables, reduction approaches, fixation instructions or treatment thresholds are imported.
- NCBI Bookshelf, Humeral Shaft Fractures: surgical-neck/axillary and shaft/radial relationships. This is a secondary educational reference, not independent validation of nerve geometry. No incidence, recovery estimates or procedural details are adopted.

Original short factual text only: no publisher prose, diagrams, photographs, scans, tables, question banks or article files are included.
