## Evidence and limits

[The 2015 primary case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4397006/)
describes normal pelvic/hip bone appearances on radiographs with an intramuscular
adductor longus injury identified on MRI. It illustrates a soft-tissue limitation,
not diagnostic sensitivity, normal-muscle visibility or a proximal tendon avulsion.
The lesson uses that distinction without reproducing the patient, images,
measurements, article prose or treatment recommendations. No acquired image is
added; matching an atlas side does not establish a patient's side or projection.
No synthetic attenuation image, scan correspondence or registration is implied.

Perrone's separate NC-ND cadaveric article/figures/data are **not** used or imported.
Only original factual prose and the reading link enter the application. No
publication prose, figure, table, photograph, scan, mesh or dataset is copied,
adapted or relicensed; access to a paper is not commercial asset clearance.
Project MIT code/prose and existing BodyParts3D CC BY4.0 credits remain in force.
No dependency, font, texture, runtime service or mandatory fee is introduced.
