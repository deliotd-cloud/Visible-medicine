Draft revision: `um-foot-depth-20261005-v1`, added after Atlas

muscles gain original relationship, typical arterial-supply and clinical-pitfall

These are additive lessons, not new anatomy meshes. The exact original surface,

display; a saved event cannot combine calf, thigh and foot depth scopes. Previous
approval cannot authorize the new teaching revision.

## Reference and commercial-use boundary

Reference pages are linked for factual checking, not copied, bundled, imported
as datasets, or relicensed. No third-party image, mesh, font, texture, scan or new
dependency is added. The factual prose is authored for this project. Linked
articles and the copyrighted Texas Tech tables retain their own rights; their
presence in the reference list is not permission to reproduce their illustrations
or text. No payment or external service is needed to display these notes.

- [Texas Tech lower-limb anatomy table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_lowerlimb.html): usual attachments and dorsal muscle context.
- [Orbay et al. plantar vascular dissection](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17413886/): reported plantar muscle arterial contributions, not a vessel map of this specimen.
- [Harej et al. tarsal-tunnel study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37701055/): why a muscle silhouette alone cannot identify a compression site.
- [Quadratus plantae morphology study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29166622/): variable heads and attachments.
- [Chen et al. ADM/Baxter systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40836398/): the proposed MRI association is not established as a stand-alone diagnosis.
- [NCBI foot-muscle anatomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539705/): related long-flexor and dorsal muscle context.

The vascular references differ on quadratus plantae contributions. The draft
states that difference; no single universal arterial branch is asserted. A
separate selectable extensor hallucis brevis is not invented. Individual digital
slips, quadratus heads, fascia, retinacula, nerves and vessels remain unresolved.
