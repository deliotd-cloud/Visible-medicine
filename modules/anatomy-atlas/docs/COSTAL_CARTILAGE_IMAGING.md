# Costal cartilage imaging teaching

Four modality texts provide56 draft placements on the existing14 first-through-
seventh bilateral cartilage selections. They reuse Imaging→CT/MRI/Ultrasound/
X-ray in thorax and whole body. No new controls, scans or models are added.
Each numbered side retains its complete source identity and unvalidated status.

CT describes cartilage clefts/contour rather than treating the selection as a
bony rib. MRI addresses tissue signal; ultrasound distinguishes acquired dynamic
observations from explode animation; X-ray explains a visibility limitation.
These are descriptive teaching notes, not diagnostic performance estimates,
fracture grading, acquisition protocols, treatment or patient registration.

## Binding and clinical review

The56 prior modality lessons were pending. All other9,862 body topics, shoulder
teaching and dissection recipes are snapshot-preserved. Exact identity matching
includes ordered source files, side, level/name, source frame fields and review
state. Caller mutations cannot change later notes; unsupported topics fail closed.

`npm run costal-cartilage-imaging:test`. Tests do not certify anatomy or diagnosis.

Owner radiologist review must verify the14 labels/source boundaries, terminology,
clinical applicability and scope of both studies against the exact revisions.
No complete chest wall, segmented joints, eighth–tenth cartilage additions,
validated footprints, calcification or fracture motion is supplied. Static
reference separation does not demonstrate instability or reproduce ultrasound.

Clinical sign-off, private-data clearance, hosted-review/model-staging and device
acceptance are separate release requirements. The coordinating checkpoint records
actual checks, browser coverage and GitHub/D recovery; this is not a pass log.
