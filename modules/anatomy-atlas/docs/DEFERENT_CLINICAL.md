# Deferent-duct Clinical and Pathology drafts

Four introductory placements fill the existing Clinical and Pathology tabs for
left FMA19236/FJ3135 and right FMA19235/FJ3140. They share two original short

No new control, route, model, image, dependency, patient data or paid service.

## Teaching scope

Clinical localisation follows the epididymal, inguinal and posterior-bladder
route, distinguishes the ureteric crossing and ejaculatory-duct junction, and
separates sperm transport from production. Vasectomy supplies a high-level
example of interrupted transport, not an operative guide or outcome assessment.

Pathology introduces congenital bilateral absence and its CFTR association,
without equating an absent duct with a genetic diagnosis. It distinguishes
congenital absence from acquired interruption and explicitly separates absent
rendering, incomplete source coverage and incomplete imaging from disease.

The two surfaces are normal reference geometry, not simulated pathology. They
do not establish a continuous lumen, patent transport, congenital status or
fertility in a patient. Detailed unilateral variants, inflammatory/traumatic
lesions, postoperative imaging and management remain outside this introductory
increment and need separately sourced review. Four non-pending tabs do not mean
all clinically relevant duct teaching is complete.

  no procedural text, image, outcome promise or third-party material imported.
