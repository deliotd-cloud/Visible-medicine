# Renal splenic and gastroduodenal angiographic drafts

Four exact existing arterial selections now have original X-ray orientation
drafts in Abdomen and Whole body. Catheter angiography is separate from plain
radiography, CTA and MRA; the reference model is not an acquired examination.
Only the X-ray lessons change. Geometry, all other topics, source identities,
guides and clinical holds remain unchanged.

## Evidence and limits

[Lionel et al., 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10075363/),
DOI 10.1046/j.1440-1622.1999.01526.x, describes 16 potential kidney donors.
Catheter angiography and source CT depicted 39 renal arteries; MIP and
shaded-surface reconstructions depicted 37. This small historical comparison
supports examination-specific orientation, not universal modality equivalence,
diagnostic accuracy or complete visibility.

[Covey et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12147854/),
DOI 10.1148/radiol.2242011283, supplies public DSA examples of the splenic
coeliac branch and gastroduodenal/hepatic junction. The series also documents
gastroduodenal origin and branching variants. No caption, figure or table is
reused, and a selected example does not establish every patient's configuration.

The [UAMS abdominal artery table](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/artery-tables/arteries-of-the-abdomen/)
supports usual origins and named branch relationships. The
[ACR/RSNA catheter angiography overview](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiocath)
supports the examination distinction. Projection, reconstruction, surface
overlap and explode separation do not establish depth, a lumen or perfusion.

## Source identity and review

Exact FMA14773, FMA14752 and FMA14753 bindings retain the splenic and paired
renal source identities. Four splenic source files are one grouped selection,
not four separately named arteries. Retain FMA76574 as the gastroduodenal trunk;
it is not silently relabelled as whole-GDA FMA14775. `unspecified`, right and
left source labels do not provide patient-space correspondence.

Each resolver checks the complete canonical source signature, including node,
geometry hashes, bounds, metadata, provenance and validation. Altered or
incomplete identities refuse the draft. Radiologist review must bind the actual
teaching revision and assess factual scope, variants, source completeness and
modality limits. Passing tests do not approve anatomy or imaging registration.
Atlas, Education-case and paid-lecture access remain independent.
