# Foot Clinical / Pathology drafts

9 September 2026: 72 short original sections cover 36 existing bilateral muscle/head/slip representations in the current Clinical and Pathology tabs. Twelve shared teaching definitions preserve each selection's exact source identity. No new controls or geometry.

## Teaching scope

- Medial-plantar first lumbrical versus lateral-plantar lumbricals 2–4; toe imbalance is not an isolated muscle diagnosis.
- Plantar interossei use the second-toe axis, not hand anatomy; they do not replace missing dorsal interossei.
- Abductor digiti minimi and Baxter neuropathy: MRI fatty infiltration is not a proven stand-alone diagnostic marker.
- Short little-toe flexor versus metatarsal-attaching opponens; the latter remains a variable, unadjudicated source slip without a validated disease-specific lesson.
- Abductor hallucis, short hallux extensor, quadratus plantae and short digital flexor: distinct motor supply, attachment and joint-action context.
- Short hallux-flexor heads and the plantar complex: turf toe, sesamoid symptoms and normal bipartition are distinguished. Unresolved FMA45097/FMA45098 groups are not relabelled medial/lateral.
- Adductor heads: bunion alignment is not proof of an isolated muscle lesion; explode does not simulate corrective surgery.

These are initial educational drafts, not a comprehensive disease catalogue, patient findings, diagnostic rules, treatment advice or independent clinical approval. Named nerves, joint instability, diseased tendons and individual slips are not newly segmented. CT/MRI/Ultrasound and Quiz readiness are unchanged.
