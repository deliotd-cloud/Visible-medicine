## Clinical/source boundaries

Root source labels are not complete drainage trees or validated confluences.
Right hepatic tributary: four files/seven disconnected components; left:
five files/five components. They remain two grouped selections, not12 newly
named veins. No bridging, lumens, vessel patency, pressure, collateral flow,
territories or tissue viability is inferred. Nested liver-branches sources
remain separately identified. MRI/CT/ultrasound need actual acquired studies
and verified registration; Atlas/case/paid-lecture access stays independent.
The user's revision-bound radiologist sign-off is still required. This bounded
licence/source audit is not project-wide legal or clinical clearance.
