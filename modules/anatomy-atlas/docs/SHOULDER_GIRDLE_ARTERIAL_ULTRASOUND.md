## Evidence and limits

| Family | Primary reading | Bounded evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Posterior circumflex humeral | [van de Pol et al., 2016/2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27255398/) | Original elite-volleyball ultrasound study: origin/course distinction from the deep brachial artery. Cohort detection and measurements are not universal visibility or diagnostic thresholds. |
| Circumflex scapular | [Dabernig et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20489408/) | Cadaver and volunteer Doppler investigation of the circumflex scapular axis and cutaneous perforators. A perforator is not a complete parent-trunk or collateral map. |
| Thyrocervical | [Kayashima and Yamasaki, 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27909611/) | One adult sonographic observation relative to subclavian artery/internal jugular vein and vertebral artery. No population detection rate or fixed scan depth. |
| Costocervical | [Demondion et al., 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12917117/) | Deep cervical daughter-branch landmark near lower cervical roots. This is indirect evidence for the parent; no complete costocervical-trunk visualisation rate is established. |
| Dorsal scapular | [Murata et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22523422/); [Lai and Ho, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37179441/) | Volunteer sonographic detection depends on probe position. Separate cadaveric origin variation is anatomy evidence, not ultrasound performance. |
