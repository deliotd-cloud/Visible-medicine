# Hand-bone ultrasound surface orientation — draft

Forty-six existing source selections are intended for the current Ultrasound tab:
38 bilateral metacarpals and phalanges, plus the paired capitate, trapezium,
trapezoid and triquetrum. Eleven original family lessons distinguish the thumb,
which has no middle phalanx, from the other four rays. Sided repetitions are
placements, not 46 independent curricula or new anatomy. Every note is a draft
awaiting revision-bound radiologist review.

## Factual reading and reuse

- Wang et al. (2021), [carpal sonoanatomy and reciprocal landmarks](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4418/11/10/1834), CC BY 4.0. The paper describes ray-specific metacarpal/carpal sequences and separate proximal/distal tunnel landmarks.
- Russo et al. (2022), [clinical applications of ultra-high-frequency ultrasound](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4426/12/10/1733), CC BY 4.0. Its illustrated finger examples locate the sagittal band near a metacarpal head, central extensor band at a middle-phalanx base, and terminal extensor/deep flexor relationships near a distal phalanx.
- Balbach, Corty, Hill, Frech, Aslam and Chew (2024), [hand-pain ultrasound protocol](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4418/14/7/669), CC BY 4.0. Its dorsal metacarpophalangeal account separates head cortex, cartilage, capsule and extensor tendon. Its systemic-sclerosis examination context does not establish generic diagnostic performance for these atlas meshes.

Only brief original factual interpretation and links are included. No article
wording, figure, sonogram, protocol table, scan, dataset or derivative visual is
imported. Article permissions do not clear any separately credited third-party
material. Existing BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 source credit remains. No new mesh,
dependency, font, texture, paid service or required media licence is introduced.
The CC BY-NC-ND finger-anatomy article (PMC7418857) was excluded. Publisher
fetches that returned a rate limit or HTTP-200 challenge page were not accepted
as article evidence; indexed primary full text and explicit PMC licence statements
were used for the factual/rights screen, checked on 4 October 2026.

## Exact-source and clinical limits

`content/hand-bone-ultrasound-pins.json` records each complete displayed identity,
ordered source component, frame, bundle and previous pending lesson. The adapter
requires full canonical equality and a pinned topic; it does not attach teaching
by name, FMA or laterality alone. Returned bullets and citations are detached.
The eleven family definitions and resolver live in
`content/hand-bone-ultrasound.ts` and `lib/hand-bone-ultrasound.ts`.

The model is an unregistered static outer surface, not a sonogram. One local
acoustic window does not show an entire bone. Neither a normal-looking mesh nor
a limited ultrasound view establishes internal marrow condition or excludes a
fracture. The source has no measured tendon insertion, cartilage, motion,
acoustic shadow, patient coordinate frame or diagnostic accuracy. Ultrasound
orientation does not grant access to a case or separately paid lecture.

Radiologist review should check all exact sided records, carpal/ray neighbours,
thumb-versus-finger joint terminology, tendon-level interpretations, landmark
visibility in the actual source and displayed copy, and every modality limit.
Approval must bind the eventual source, teaching and renderer revisions. Software
validation, future browser checks and any later imaging-case clearance are
separate from clinical sign-off; no publication or approval is asserted here.
