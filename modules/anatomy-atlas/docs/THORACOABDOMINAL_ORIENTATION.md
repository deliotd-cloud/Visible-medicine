# Thoracoabdominal orientation drafts

Eight exact existing selections receive nine previously pending topics:

| Selection | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| Right/left superior epigastric artery | MRI |
| Right/left musculophrenic artery | MRI |
| Right/left musculophrenic vein | MRI |
| Linea alba | MRI |
| Mesoappendix | CT, MRI |

These are source-bound orientation notes in the existing regional/whole-body
information panel, not new controls, anatomy, scans, approved imaging appearance
or registered patient structures. The mesoappendix is an admitted, independently
selectable FMA16549/FJ3397 membrane; its extent, attachments, leaves and embedded
structures remain unvalidated. Locating an appendix on CT/MRI does **not** establish
a separately resolved mesoappendix. No statistical threshold or acquisition,
diagnostic, treatment or surgical recommendation is imported.

Arteries and veins retain separate identities. Routine MRI/MRA is not guaranteed
to resolve these small channels. Linea-alba teaching cannot turn an exploded gap
into diastasis or produce a patient measurement. Notes require exact complete
source identity and the pinned modality; changed names, sides, geometry metadata,
source hashes or unrelated topics do not inherit a lesson. All remain drafts for
revision/source/scope-bound radiologist sign-off. Case, Atlas and paid-lecture
access remain independent; Didanix Education/light is not the clinical desktop PACS.

## References and attribution scope

Inspected 2 October 2026. The prose is original factual synthesis. Citation links
are reading context, not permissions to redistribute article text or media.
No external figure, scan, diagram, mesh, texture, font, dataset or dependency is
imported, and no new paid service or mandatory asset fee is introduced.

- [TTUHSC thoracic arteries](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_thorax.html)
  and [abdominal wall](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/abdo_wall_tables.html):
  expected arterial course/relationships, not MR visibility or verified mesh joins.
- [TTUHSC superior mediastinum](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/cardiovascular_system/sup_med_tables.html):
  musculophrenic/internal-thoracic venous relationship, not a complete donor drainage map.
- [RadiologyInfo MRA](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiomr): the small-vessel
  visibility caveat only. Applying that general limit to these selected vessels is
  an explicitly qualified inference, not a dedicated study of their depiction.
- [Read et al., 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8913712/): abdominal-wall
  anatomical and MR orientation context; no measurements, clinical protocols or
  illustrated patient material are reproduced.
- [Johnson et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16819637/) and
  [Nitta et al., 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15666398/): CT/MR identification
  of the appendix, not proof that the mesoappendix membrane is separately visible.
- [Patra et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37691509/) and
  [TTUHSC peritoneum](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/gastrointestinal_system/peritoneum_tables.html):
  expected fold relationship and anatomical variation, not CT/MR detectability.

Attribution is claim-scoped: anatomy body/pitfall wording is supported by its
anatomy reference; the MRA reference supports the visibility caveat, not every
word of the artery/vein anatomy paragraph. New unique body/pitfall attribution

words; the deduplicated arterial/venous MRA visibility caveats total 41 words.
No verbatim passages are used. Repeated left/right displays are not separate
source facts. These are this batch's scoped counts, not a claim that naive
full-paragraph-to-every-link counting across all historical notes stays under200,
nor a licence audit or reproduction grant for the linked publications.

## Validation and delivery

`npm run thoracoabdominal-orientation:test` verifies the unchanged original
9,936-topic baseline, nine new drafts and9,927 preserved placements, actual
current note renderings, full-source refusals, eight changed versus1,096 unchanged
teaching worksheets, and stale synthetic POST refusals before private storage.
The exact transition helper restores only those nine recorded placements for
historical tests. It rejects mixed/unrecorded transitions and foreign identities;
original pins/goldens and clinical holds are not regenerated to hide differences.

Historical shoulder checks distinguish their recorded milestone from separately
verified live targeted lessons, renders, worksheets and stale POST refusals.
Current generated display/renderer pins must match the new build; this changes
review evidence, not private decisions or clinical approval. Full visual, image
licensing/privacy, registration and revision-bound sign-off are separate gates.

Website delivery requires the normal generated learner and protected Review
import; saving this Atlas source alone does not change the running website.
