# Hand arterial ultrasound orientation — draft

Twenty-eight existing bilateral selections receive seven original source-group
summaries through the existing Ultrasound tab: superficial/deep palmar arches,
palmar metacarpal arteries, princeps pollicis, radialis indicis, common digital
and proper digital arteries. No additional unique anatomy, control or media.

The binding uses the complete accepted identity, source components, geometry
hashes, two existing bundles and coordinate frame at `6e134825`. Source labels
and counts remain unchanged: one metacarpal selection per side, compound thumb
and index selections, historical common-digital numbering and only ten proper
digital selections (six right/four left). Do not infer complete branches, junctions,
patency, flow, collateral adequacy or procedural suitability from these surfaces.

Reading sources: [TTUHSC arterial table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html),
[1984 Doppler Flowmeter study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1164307/)
and [digital ultrasound review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8535079/).
The historical technique is not modern duplex. Digit-artery observations are not
nailfold microvascular scoring. These are original bounded factual summaries,
not imported papers, figures, ultrasound images, datasets or licensed asset grants.
The NC-ND palmar-muscle article screened during research supplied no content or
media for this addition. Existing asset/dependency notices remain unchanged.
