# Right-hand arterial guided learning

Seven stops are available through the existing Guided learning selector in Hand
and Whole body: superficial arch, deep arch, palmar metacarpal selection, princeps
pollicis, radialis indicis, historical first-common digital selection and medial
index proper digital selection. Muscle and carpal tours retain their defaults.
No extra toolbar, model, texture, font, dependency or paid service is introduced.

The sequence keeps the exact seven source IDs, FMA identities, mesh membership,
bundle hashes and coordinates. Each stop frames a small explicit target union.
The existing player requests 1,800 ms quintic camera transitions, supports pause
and reduced motion, and starts assembled at zero separation. These are source
and handler properties, not proof of rendered visibility or clinical accuracy.

The existing imaging-notes panel follows the selected structure, including the
previously authored CT/CTA drafts. It loads no scan and establishes no patient
registration. Atlas, imaging-case and paid-lecture access remain independent.

## Source and clinical limits

Separate exterior surfaces do not establish joined lumens, patency, flow, calibre
or a procedural route. The two meshes in each princeps/radialis selection are
not new branch identities. Common digital numbering is a historical source label;
the six right/four left proper digital selections are incomplete. The chosen
first-common and medial-index proper segments are not asserted to join directly.
No nerve, complete arterial tree or missing counterpart is invented.

Captions are original concise drafts informed by the
[TTUHSC upper-limb arterial table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html)
and [the authored upper-extremity CTA review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11736060/).
No figures, publisher prose or additional asset were copied. Reading references
are not model licences; existing commercial-compatible asset credits remain intact.

Revision-bound radiologist review must assess nomenclature, variant caveats,
clinical wording, actual source extent and relationship, and camera visibility.
Visual/device and clinical acceptance remain separate from automated checks.
