## Source and review limits

Full pinned canonical source records, original single-file identities and existing
digit/segment membership are required; a label or FMA alone is insufficient.
The foot-skeleton bundle remains unchanged. The selection does not segment joints,
cartilage, marrow, fracture lines or nail-bed anatomy. Hallux and held grouped
sesamoid teaching are not promoted; ultrasound is not filled with generic bone
fracture advice. Atlas, imaging-case and lecture access remain independent.

Clinical sign-off must review digit/side/segment binding, projectional wording,
actual acquired-case applicability and physical-device use against the exact
revision. Neither a source identity nor passing tests constitute approval.
