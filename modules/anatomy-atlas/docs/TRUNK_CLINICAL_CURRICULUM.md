# Trunk and coccygeus Clinical / Pathology drafts

9 September 2026: 80 short original sections cover 40 existing muscle, part and group selections (52 source components). Seventeen shared definitions populate the existing Clinical and Pathology tabs. No extra controls, geometry or dependencies.

## Teaching scope

- Chest-wall layers: distinguish muscular pain from other causes, different internal-intercostal portions, and a reference cutaway from a safe procedure. UK emergency escalation is explicit where chest-pain red flags are taught.
- External oblique: a surface is not the complete abdominal wall or a demonstrated hernia.
- Pectoralis minor: coracoid/scapular relationships and congenital context, without diagnosing a compression syndrome. Pectoralis major: injury location and appropriate imaging coverage; the absent clavicular component is not a tear.
- Diaphragm: position alone does not prove paralysis. Dynamic movement assessment is different from CT investigation of an underlying cause. This atlas has neither a patient study nor respiratory motion.
- Trapezius: accessory-nerve injury context shared across its three selectable source parts, not independently validated motor territories.
- Deep back: distinguish named muscle columns/groups, sparse or unresolved components and clinical localisation. A nearby mesh is not a proven pain generator; lumbar neurological red flags require emergency assessment.
- Posterior serratus: not serratus anterior. Its disputed respiratory role is retained as uncertainty, not a validated action or disease mechanism.
- Coccygeus: posterior pelvic-floor relationships, impaired relaxation versus weakness, and the limits of muscle-only teaching. IC-specific physiotherapy context is not a generic exercise prescription.

These are introductory drafts, not a complete disease catalogue, clinical approval, diagnostic rules or treatment protocols. Source grouping is not independent muscle count. FMA19728 remains a broad unresolved perineal category: Function, Clinical and Pathology are still pending. Four existing Function holds and two sesamoid Anatomy identity-only entries are unchanged. CT/MRI/Ultrasound and Quiz readiness are unchanged.
