## Acceptance gate and next route

Require the actual mesh and its version/hash; explicit zero-fee commercial hosting, redistribution and adaptation rights; complete attribution/provenance for every included component; independently selectable anatomical identities; verified laterality, coordinate system, anatomical relationships and branch continuity; and the owner's exact-revision radiologist sign-off. Keep article figures, uncleared donor geometry and patient-specific data out of repository and hosted artifacts.

Continue the existing authorized Cevallos request only when a usable communication route is available. Any request to a different creator needs the corresponding owner authorization. In parallel, refine the original schematic through private review, without presenting it as a clinical spatial model or a 3D-to-scan registration solution. Remaining admitted Atlas teaching and functionality can proceed without admitting these alternatives.
