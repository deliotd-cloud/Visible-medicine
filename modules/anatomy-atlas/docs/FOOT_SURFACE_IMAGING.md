# Foot ultrasound surface-orientation drafts

Eight topic placements on existing left/right calcaneus, talus, navicular and
long plantar ligament selections. Four shared teaching families, not eight
unique lessons or additional meshes. All remain revision-bound clinical drafts.
Ultrasound is a surface window: no marrow, patient plane, acquisition simulator,
diagnostic accuracy, validated measurements or model-to-scan registration.
The two long plantar CT topics remain pending; ultrasound evidence is not CT
evidence. Other topics, geometry and independent resource access are preserved.

## Verification and clinical acceptance

`npm run foot-surface-imaging:test` checks exact pins/transition, eight intended
changes with 9,928 other placements retained, full identity mutation refusals,
fresh arrays, all model bytes, displayed Review materials and rejection of
malformed or independently stale submissions before storage. Historic adapters
restore the exact recorded pending epoch; they are test-only, never learner
dispatch or approval migration. No real private review record is read or changed.

Radiologist review must verify each actual rendered source, side and landmarks;
the distinctions between FDL/FHL and long/short plantar ligament; the scope of
surface visibility; reference interpretation; and framing/readability. Approval
must be bound to current teaching/display/source revisions. A future case needs
separate rights/privacy clearance, approved correspondence and case entitlement.
No technical pass is clinical approval or evidence of a complete foot atlas.
