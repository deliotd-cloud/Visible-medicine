# Hip and left-shoulder bone imaging drafts

Nine original topic texts supply twelve draft placements: CT/MRI/X-ray for the
right and left hip bones, and CT/MRI for the left clavicle, humerus and scapula.
The existing shoulder X-ray drafts, ultrasound pending state, right-shoulder
pilot and all other teaching remain unchanged. Existing regional and whole-body
panels are used; no new controls, meshes or scan assets are introduced.

## Evidence and reuse

- [TTUHSC El Paso: lower-limb bones](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_lowerlimb.html)
  supports hip-bone components and orientation landmarks.
- [TTUHSC El Paso: upper-limb bones](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_upperlimb.html)
  supports clavicular ends, humeral landmarks and scapular orientation. Obvious
  errors elsewhere in the table (humeral head called ulna; medial epicondylitis
  called tennis elbow) are not used. A cited page is not blanket validation.
- RSNA/ACR [Body CT](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/bodyct),
  [Musculoskeletal MRI](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/muscmr) and
  [Bone X-ray](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/bonerad) support the limited
  modality distinctions: reformats, soft-tissue assessment and projection imaging.
