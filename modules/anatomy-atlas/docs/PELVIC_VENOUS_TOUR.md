## Learner journey

Pelvis or Whole body → Guided learning → **Pelvic veins: tributary orientation**.
Ten stops highlight the existing left iliolumbar, inferior gluteal, obturator
and superior gluteal surfaces, then the separately supplied right iliolumbar,
inferior gluteal, internal pudendal, lateral sacral, obturator and superior
gluteal surfaces. Paired common/internal iliac veins, hip bones and sacrum remain
faded context. Each close-up frames its selected vein with the same-side iliac
surface; all source coordinates remain assembled (separation zero).

The existing 1.8-second quintic-orbit transition, pause, step picker, reduced
motion, keyboard controls and exit restoration are reused. No extra toolbar,
permanent panel, geometry, asset dependency or paid service is introduced.
The website needs a generated import from the saved source before this appears
in its integrated runtime; source completion does not prove hosted availability.

## Scope and rights

Ten targets and seven contextual representations are pinned to exact existing
records, source hashes and four model bundles. No new meshes are admitted.
BodyParts3D v4 CC BY 4.0 attribution remains unchanged. Incomplete left-sided
coverage is intentional: FMA18919 (left internal pudendal) and FMA18907 (left
lateral sacral) remain held. Do not mirror, crop or rename them to fill a gap.
The superior gluteal definitions are short supplied surfaces, not full networks.

Captions are original inspection prompts, not copied diagrams or text. Factual
reading references checked 10 October 2026:

territories, flow, patency, a procedural route or CT/MRI registration. The male
reference model is not patient-specific anatomy. The radiologist must review
the actual named source extents/laterality, camera visibility and captions at
this revision. Older worksheet decisions do not approve the added tour.

step-picker and camera checks are separate. Tests do not confer clinical or
