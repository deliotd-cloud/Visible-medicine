# Thoracic respiratory-wall motor relationships — draft

This Thorax-only entry extends the existing collapsed **Muscles by nerve** panel. It binds four exact BodyParts3D source identities already used by the thorax respiratory lessons: FMA13295 diaphragm to typical phrenic motor supply, and FMA9756 external, FMA9757 internal, and FMA9758 innermost intercostal layers to typical segmental intercostal motor supply. The anatomical facts are checked against [Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center El Paso, Muscles of the Thoracic Region](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_thorax.html); the displayed language is original.

The source surfaces are compound and midline-labelled. Left and right filters each retain the same complete surfaces; neither isolates a side. The catalog does not show nerve geometry or course, individual rib spaces, hemidiaphragms, contraction, breathing motion or a clinical finding. No individual intercostal level is assigned, and the diaphragm is not split into motor territories. These are unsigned teaching relationships, subject to revision-bound radiologist review.

Only `Thorax` is admitted. The regional motor helper validates the complete Thorax muscle and skeleton source scope, coordinate system, license and used bundles before showing a relationship. Whole-body support would require a separate source-scope contract and is not inferred from shared identifiers. A group loads the normal reversible visibility view with source bones retained; Undo restores the prior dissection state. No nerve becomes a selectable mesh.

Verification: `node scripts/pin-thorax-motor.mjs --check` and `node scripts/validate-thorax-motor.mjs`. These are source, logic and component checks, not clinical, browser or device acceptance.

Bounded local-browser QA at `/regions/thorax` on 24 September confirmed that **Explore** kept the motor panel out of view, **Dissect → Systems & tools** showed one collapsed **Muscles by nerve** control, and opening its selector showed **Phrenic nerve · 1** and **Segmental intercostal nerves · 3**. Selecting phrenic displayed the compound-side/no-nerve caveat; **Show muscles & bones** selected the diaphragm and enabled Undo. Undo restored the assembled view. The intercostal choice showed the three named layers, and its focus rendered the source muscle with bones as context and a no-nerve-path notice. This was one desktop browser and visual sample, not a physical-touch, 200% text, screen-reader, anatomical or clinical acceptance pass.
