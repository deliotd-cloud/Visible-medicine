# Thoracic respiratory-wall motor relationships — draft

This Thorax entry extends the existing collapsed **Muscles by nerve** panel in the Thorax and whole-body views. It binds four exact BodyParts3D source identities already used by the thorax respiratory lessons: FMA13295 diaphragm to typical phrenic motor supply, and FMA9756 external, FMA9757 internal, and FMA9758 innermost intercostal layers to typical segmental intercostal motor supply. The anatomical facts are checked against [Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center El Paso, Muscles of the Thoracic Region](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_thorax.html); the displayed language is original.

The source surfaces are compound and midline-labelled. Left and right filters each retain the same complete surfaces; neither isolates a side. The catalog does not show nerve geometry or course, individual rib spaces, hemidiaphragms, contraction, breathing motion or a clinical finding. No individual intercostal level is assigned, and the diaphragm is not split into motor territories. These are unsigned teaching relationships, subject to revision-bound radiologist review.

`whole-body` is an explicit alias for this Thorax study only; upper- and lower-limb groups are not admitted to whole-body by it. Both views validate the same pinned 52-entry Thorax muscle and skeleton source scope, coordinate system, licence, version, used bundles and unique target IDs before showing a relationship. A Thorax plan hides other Thorax structures; a whole-body plan hides every catalog structure except the selected group's complete both-side targets and pinned Thorax skeleton context. This is the normal reversible visibility view: Undo restores the prior dissection state and Redo reapplies it. No nerve becomes a selectable mesh or a second model or card.

The whole-body scene derives its camera fit from rendered, enabled structures after the hide mask. The motor handler enables muscles and skeleton, turns removed ghosts off, returns to spatial layout, clears the saved camera and requests a reset, so the visible target and context can define that fit. The generic scene may still use an active joint close-up camera bound or expand to the full source frame when origin guides are on; this change does not override those independent controls. This is source-level framing reasoning, not a new browser or device observation.

Verification: `node scripts/pin-thorax-motor.mjs --check` and `node scripts/validate-thorax-motor.mjs`. These are source, logic and component checks, not clinical, browser or device acceptance.

Bounded local-browser QA at `/regions/thorax` on 24 September confirmed that **Explore** kept the motor panel out of view, **Dissect → Systems & tools** showed one collapsed **Muscles by nerve** control, and opening its selector showed **Phrenic nerve · 1** and **Segmental intercostal nerves · 3**. Selecting phrenic displayed the compound-side/no-nerve caveat; **Show muscles & bones** selected the diaphragm and enabled Undo. Undo restored the assembled view. The intercostal choice showed the three named layers, and its focus rendered the source muscle with bones as context and a no-nerve-path notice. This was one desktop browser and visual sample, not a physical-touch, 200% text, screen-reader, anatomical or clinical acceptance pass.
