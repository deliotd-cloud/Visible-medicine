# Orbital muscle teaching drafts

## Scope

Seven definitions provide 28 new Anatomy/Function drafts for 14 existing bilateral source entries: four recti, two obliques and levator palpebrae superioris. Notes use the existing panels; no new controls, meshes, dependencies, scans or approvals. Exact FMA pairs, source components and laterality are retained.

The recti's orbital-apex origin is not assigned to inferior oblique. Superior oblique retains the distinction between its bony origin and reflected tendon through the trochlea. Globe actions distinguish adduction/abduction and torsion, including the different gaze positions that emphasize vertical rectus versus oblique action. Levator elevates the upper lid, not the globe; its skeletal motor supply is not confused with superior-tarsal sympathetic supply. CN III superior/inferior divisions, CN IV and CN VI remain explicit.

These are typical teaching relationships, not verified surface footprints, insertion-distance measurements, pulley segmentation, motor territories or an eye-movement simulation. The held levator-tendon candidate is not admitted or validated by text.
