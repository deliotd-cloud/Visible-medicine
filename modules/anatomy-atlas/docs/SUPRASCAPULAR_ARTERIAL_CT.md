# Suprascapular artery CT orientation

This two-selection source draft replaces only the pending CT topic for the
right/left suprascapular arteries, FMA10698 and FMA10681. MRI, ultrasound,
other teaching and geometry remain unchanged. Both complete source identities
and their model bundles are pinned; names alone cannot enable the draft.

## Evidence and applicability

- [Zardi et al., 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3029979/): a
  primary case report describes CT angiography of a suprascapular artery
  aneurysm with a direct subclavian origin. It does not establish normal-vessel
  detection accuracy or routine CT visibility.
- [Pyrgakis et al., 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3624714/):
  cadaveric evidence demonstrates variation in origin and course. This is
  anatomical context, not validation of CT resolution or a prevalence estimate
  applicable to every patient.

Use the archived vessel to orient the named artery; do not treat that surface
as a patient lumen, proof of an aneurysm or a complete variant map. No clinical
management recommendation, scan protocol, patient registration or new model is
provided. Exact-revision radiologist review remains required.
