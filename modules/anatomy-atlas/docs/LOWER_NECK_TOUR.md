# Right lower neck: scalenes and vessels

Six optional Guided learning stops compare existing right anterior and middle
scalenes, subclavian artery and vein, common carotid artery and internal jugular
vein. Sternocleidomastoid and omohyoid are faded context. This complements the
existing bilateral lower-neck study; it does not replace its left/right controls.
The tour is discoverable in Head & neck and the Whole body tour library without
adding a permanent toolbar. Existing regional defaults are unchanged.
