# Leg arterial contrast-X-ray teaching drafts

Eight original topics now target sixteen exact bilateral source selections in
the existing Leg and Whole body explorer: popliteal, anterior/posterior tibial,
superior medial/lateral, inferior medial/lateral and middle genicular arteries.
They use the current X-ray tab and protected source-bound Review, not a new
navigation surface. Editorial draft is not clinical approval or scan availability.

## Source identity and limitations

Complete source signatures must match the existing popliteal-vessel-study,
limb-vascular-study and genicular-imaging pin files. Names or FMA identifiers alone
cannot authorize teaching; copied, altered or foreign identities are refused.
Only X-ray lessons change. Other topics, geometry, dissection, tours, nested and
specimen material remain unchanged. Later source revisions need fresh review.

The middle genicular groups each contain two disconnected source components,
not two validated branches. Other retained arterial segments and genicular groups
do not establish a continuous lumen, exact origins/junctions, complete anastomotic
network, territories, calibre, patency, flow or perfusion. Reassemble before
comparison; display colour, projection overlap and source side are not patient
markers or patient-space registration.

The drafts concern acquired contrast catheter/DSA images, not vessels outlined on
ordinary unopacified plain films. CTA, MRA and ultrasound remain separate exams.
No acquired image, procedural recommendation or diagnostic threshold is provided.

## Checked factual reading references

- [Kim, Orron and Skillman, 1989](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2589890/):
  arteriographic branching variability, with differing assessable subsets.
- [Kil and Jung, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18982387/):
  selected lower-extremity DSA observations, not universal bilateral anatomy.
- [Taheri Amin et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40640408/):
  genicular origins/connections in a selected osteoarthritis intervention cohort,
  not normal-population rates or proof of this source network. Do not use the
  unrelated PMID 40925979 found in a commentary's reference list. Inconsistent
  abstract/table counts and uncalibrated measurements are not used in teaching.
- [O'Grady et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34657976/):
  cadaveric observations compared with acquired angiographic appearances.
- [Blagojević et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26130286/):
  middle-genicular cadaveric dissection/injected-cadaver CTA, not live DSA.
- [UAMS artery table](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/artery-tables/arteries-of-the-lower-limb/)
  and [RadiologyInfo catheter angiography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiocath)
  support naming and modality context.
