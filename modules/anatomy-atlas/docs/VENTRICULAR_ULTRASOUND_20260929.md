## Evidence read on 29 September 2026

- [ACR–AIUM–SPR–SRU neurosonography practice parameter](https://gravitas.acr.org/PPTS/DownloadPreviewDocument?DocId=42): indexed primary-source examination text describes anterior-fontanelle coronal views, lateral-ventricle parasagittal views, midline third/fourth-ventricle landmarks and mastoid posterior-fossa views. Direct preview retrieval was unavailable; no complete-document review is claimed.
- [AIUM 2020 parameter, examination section](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jum.15264): accessible publisher text corroborates these orientation facts. AIUM lists a 2024 revision, but that publisher endpoint was inaccessible; this lesson does not reproduce a 2024 protocol or claim that 2020 is the current edition.
- [Steggerda et al., European Radiology 2015](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00330-015-3665-8), [PMID 25899415](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25899415/): primary retrospective comparison in 113 high-risk term infants. Mastoid views improved detection, yet MRI detected additional abnormalities. The draft does not generalise cohort performance to screening populations or all ages.

Only original short factual prose and evidence links are added. No source images,
screenshots, tables, extended passages, scan files or guideline PDF are bundled.
Reading access is not a redistribution licence. No new font, dependency, API,
fee or service. Existing model attribution remains required and unchanged.

## Clinical boundaries

The lessons concern neonatal/infant acoustic windows, not an adult transcranial
protocol. The underlying adult reference shapes are not neonatal normal-size
templates, age-specific proportions, echogenicity, fontanelles, CSF flow or
patient registration. No measurement cut-offs or patient diagnostic advice are
supplied. Model gaps are not obstruction and visible anatomy does not exclude
posterior-fossa injury. Revision-bound radiologist review remains required.

The 29 remaining geometry-only nested entries are all unnamed cranial-artery
source partitions, not 29 newly identified structures awaiting ordinary lessons.
Their parent FMA does not name a child branch. Keep them unnamed until a supported
identity mapping is reviewed; do not manufacture clinical teaching to fill a count.
