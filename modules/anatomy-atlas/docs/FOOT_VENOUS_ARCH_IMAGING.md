## Source scope and rights

- [Uhl, *Phlebology* (2012), PMID 23155152](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23155152/) is a review of direct CT venography for lower-limb venous mapping. Its abstract does not establish routine CT visibility of each foot arch or validate this atlas mesh.
- [Anastasi et al., *Journal of Anatomy* (2009), PMCID PMC2780576](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2780576/) is an original ankle MRI rendering study comparing images from 17 healthy volunteers and one patient. Its results depict a dorsal venous arch; they do not define routine MRI performance or a diagnostic threshold.
- [Becciolini et al., *Journal of Ultrasound* (online 2020; issue 2021), PMID 31919813, PMCID PMC8572232](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31919813/) reports one medial marginal vein/dorsal arch thrombosis case and the arch's relation to extensor hallucis longus. The reported compression and colour-flow findings, including comparison with the unaffected side, are case observations, not sensitivity or specificity evidence.
- [UAMS selected lower-limb veins table](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/vein-tables/selected-veins-of-the-lower-limb/) supports dorsal-arch continuity towards the medial and lateral saphenous pathways.

Links are for reading and attribution. No article text, figure, scan, video,
protocol or other media is redistributed. The teaching prose is newly written;
article access is not image or commercial asset clearance. No acquired scans,
identifiers or uncleared derivatives enter the repository or hosted product.
