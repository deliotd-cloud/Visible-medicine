# Abdominal-organ imaging orientation

Select liver, pancreas, gallbladder, spleen, either kidney or either adrenal gland in Abdomen or Whole body, then open the existing Imaging group. The four modality tabs now hold introductory, source-linked drafts. No new control, permanent panel, image asset or geometry is added.

## Coverage and useful distinctions

Eight existing selections, six organ concepts, 32 previously pending placements. There are 20 distinct modality texts, not 32 independent lessons: the general plain-film limitation is shared across five concepts, and sided kidneys/adrenals share appropriate concept teaching. Six short landmark notes and six representation cautions retain organ-specific context.

- Liver: phase-aware CT comparison; complementary MRI sequences; ultrasound/Doppler versus a surface model.
- Pancreas: regional continuity, MRCP duct-versus-tissue distinction and incomplete sonographic coverage.
- Gallbladder: CT stone-detection limitations, MRCP context and ultrasound assessment.
- Spleen: early enhancement heterogeneity, sequence/phase comparison and the accessory-spleen pitfall.
- Kidney: tissue versus excretory-phase information, renal tissue versus collecting system, and plain radiography versus urography.
- Adrenal: unenhanced attenuation versus enhanced data, chemical-shift caveats and adult ultrasound visibility limits.

These explain imaging concepts, not acquisition protocols or patient management. No dose, timing prescription, lesion threshold, follow-up algorithm, procedural advice or diagnosis is implemented. Draft coverage does not establish clinical completeness.
