# Abdominal wall and back source-guided dissection

Two optional, initially collapsed Guided learning sequences reuse the independent
specimen workbench. Six stops each, existing source memberships, no new mesh.
They are draft teaching, not complete anatomy or a clinical exposure.

| Sequence | Ordered steps | Existing source coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abdominal wall | Supplied context → external obliques aside → both oblique pairs aside → rectus pair → right wall → left wall | 29 surfaces; eight muscles and 21 skeletal context surfaces; seven original studies retained |
| Back layers | Supplied context → six trapezius parts aside → rhomboids → latissimus pair → multifidus groups → supplied context restored | 48 surfaces; 14 muscles and 34 bones; eight original studies retained |

Each guide resolves only against its exact admitted definition and reference
frame `bodyparts3d-v3-20110915:source`. No mirroring, sculpting, source admission
or fitting into the newer body. Sequences do not increase unique anatomy coverage.
Existing female-pelvis guidance and regional/shoulder tours remain unchanged.

## Interaction and review

The shared optional player gates Start/progression on loaded required bundles
and renderer readiness; failure or hidden document pauses progression. Exit stays
available. Existing 1.8-second quintic camera motion stops on manual orbit and
respects reduced motion. Finish/Exit restores pre-session selection, visibility,
Undo/Redo, search, display and captured valid camera. The final back stage restores
supplied context before Finish restores the learner's own prior state.

Full captions, order, views and surrounding IDs enter teaching fingerprints and
the actual-viewer checklist for all 77 involved selections. All 356 independent
source packets stay exact. Other 279 teaching packets, including the pelvic
guide, retain their previous teaching revision. Global renderer revisions can
separately invalidate geometry renderer approval. No approval is inherited.
Some skeletal context teaching remains pending: adding guidance does not remove
those approval blockers or enable imaging approval. The review test requires 231
stale material/revision/foreign-frame requests to return 409 before storage.

## Rights and clinical limits

Already admitted BodyParts3D/DBCLS version-3 assets retain **CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan**,
not the v4 CC BY 4.0 grant. Source-study adaptations in the two guide modules
retain ShareAlike/credit. Existing source notices/downloads and commercial reuse
rights remain available; subscriptions cannot override them. No dependency,
font, texture, external image, scan, paid service or mandatory fee added. See
[abdominal source](ABDOMINAL_WALL_SPECIMEN.md), [back source](BACK_LAYERS_SPECIMEN.md)
and [notices](../LICENSES/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Captions describe supplied surfaces and visibility changes. No certification of
sheath, aponeurotic, inguinal or neurovascular planes; complete back muscle stack,
fascia, cord, discs and nerves remain absent. Source fragments are not identified
fascicles. No attachment-map certification, patient registration, acquired
CT/MRI/X-ray/US series or paid lecture entitlement is implied.

Radiologist acceptance must inspect captions, visible sets, selected sources,
laterality, camera sweep/occlusion and limitations in the actual viewer. Browser,
keyboard, enlarged-text and physical-device checks remain separate from controlled
UI/source tests. No clinical approval or deployment claimed.
