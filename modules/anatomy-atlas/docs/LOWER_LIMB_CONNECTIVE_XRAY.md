# Achilles tendon and iliotibial tract: X-ray orientation drafts

Four source-bound draft placements: the left/right calcaneal tendons in Leg and
left/right iliotibial tracts in Thigh. Other topics, anatomy meshes, source holds,
dissection recipes and independent specimen curricula are unchanged. Select the
structure and open X-ray; no patient image is supplied by this teaching batch.

## Evidence checked on 14 October 2026

- [AAOS Achilles tendon rupture](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/achilles-tendon-rupture-tear):
  heel insertion and possible bony avulsion clue; US/MRI soft-tissue assessment.
  Original paraphrase only. No reproduced clinical images or examination advice.
- [Kokulu et al., Diagnostic Value of Lateral Ankle Radiography in Achilles
  Tendon Rupture](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36036526/), DOI
  10.1177/19386400221118496: accessible primary abstract, retrospective
  case-control design (154 confirmed ruptures, 308 sprain controls), imperfect
  fat-pad-sign sensitivity/specificity. Teaching does not transport those
  estimates into a validated diagnostic score or claim population screening.
- [Campos et al., Segond fracture anatomy/MR study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11323461/),
  DOI 10.1148/radiology.219.2.r01ma23381: primary abstract; six cadaveric knees
  and 17 acute clinical cases, broad tibial insertion with posterior fibres and
  ITT attachment to some Segond fragments. No universal fibre boundary inferred.
- [Khetan et al., isolated ITB avulsion case](https://jassm.org/an-unusual-case-of-isolated-iliotibial-band-avulsion-fracture/),
  DOI 10.25259/JASSM_59_2020: primary case text; radiographic Gerdy donor-site
  clue, CT confirmation and MRI evaluation. A single unusual isolated case is
  explicitly not generalized. Its discussion is not used to impose an absolute
  exclusion of ITB fibres from Segond fragments; Campos provides the caveat.
- [ACR/RSNA musculoskeletal ultrasound](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/musculous):
  official modality properties, limited X-ray depiction of soft tissues and
  potential US assessment. The ITB-specific normal-film limitation is a cautious
  teaching inference from these properties, not a measured diagnostic result.
