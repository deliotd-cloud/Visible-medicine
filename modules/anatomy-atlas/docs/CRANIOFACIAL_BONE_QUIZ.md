# Craniofacial bone questions

Nine original formative questions cover 15 retained head-and-neck bone selections:
paired inferior nasal conchae, lacrimal bones, maxillae, nasal bones, palatine bones
and zygomatic bones, plus the hyoid, mandible and vomer. They replace identification-only
Quiz notes using existing collapsed controls, explanations and answer feedback.
No new navigation, dental question bank or model is introduced.

## Factual references and reuse boundary

- [TTUHSC head-and-neck bones](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_head_neck.html): underlying anatomical facts about facial bones, their locations and articulations.
- [OpenStax skull chapter](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-2-the-skull): independent factual cross-check of facial bones and hyoid relationships.

The questions are newly written from general anatomical facts; publisher exercises
