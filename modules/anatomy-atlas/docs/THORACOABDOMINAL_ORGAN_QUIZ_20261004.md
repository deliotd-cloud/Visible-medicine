# Thoracoabdominal organ structure checks — draft

Eight existing selections replace generic identification prompts with original
four-choice checks in the existing Quiz notes: heart, oesophagus, thymus,
stomach, small intestine, large intestine, appendix and ileocaecal junction.
The authored lung/airway, pancreatic, renal, adrenal and splenic checks are not
replaced. Practice questions remain a separate section. No new navigation,
geometry, source dataset, image, dependency or clinical approval is introduced.

## Teaching scope and references

- [Cardiac MRI](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/cardiacmr): acquired motion
  information versus changing the viewpoint of a static surface. This does not
  claim that every cardiac functional assessment requires the same sequence.
- [Upper GI](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/uppergi): swallowed contrast,
  oesophageal lumen and the oesophagus–stomach–duodenum examination scope.
- [Small bowel follow-through](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/small-bowel-follow-thru)
  and [lower GI](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/lowergi): examination scope,
  not test-selection recommendations, preparation or contrast protocols.
- [Age-related thymic CT changes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9371532/):
  variation and involution, without a universal age/size/attenuation threshold.
- [Appendicitis ultrasound review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4805616/):
  nonvisualisation alone is not proof of absence/normality or exclusion of disease;
  no subsequent patient-management decision is prescribed by the quiz.
- [TTUHSC El Paso peritoneal/intestine tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/gastrointestinal_system/peritoneum_tables.html):
  terminal ileum–caecum continuity and a distinct appendix. The source selection
  remains a junction surface, not clinically accepted functional valve leaflets.

These links support independently worded factual teaching. No article prose,
figures, tables, photographs, scans, meshes, fonts or supplemental dataset is
copied. A reading link is not an asset licence grant or endorsement. Existing
model licences/notices still apply; there is no new third-party asset licence
or mandatory fee. The original questions remain under the repository's existing
application/content licence, subject to clinical review before release.
