# Coronary venous nested study

This source-only Atlas study adds two selectable, source-defined parts inside the guarded Heart action: coronary sinus FMA4706 and small cardiac vein FMA4714. Run `npm run coronary-venous:test` from `outputs` against the pinned BodyParts3D v4 source cache. Nothing here constitutes website publication or clinical approval.

The official BodyParts3D v4 PART-OF and IS-A indices both map coronary sinus **FMA4706** to **FJ2655**, and small cardiac vein **FMA4714** to the *two-file group* **FJ2724 + FJ2731**. The two latter files are not identified as separate named branches. The three PART-OF OBJ hashes match the existing heart FMA7088 source pins. Their respective triangle counts are 624, 570 and 892; each has one closed, oriented component and no exact-coordinate duplicate, collapsed, degenerate, boundary or nonmanifold faces or edges. The corresponding IS-A OBJs have different byte hashes but identical exact-coordinate unoriented triangle multisets and bounds. The common pinned source-to-scene matrix places every transformed vertex within the heart aggregate's bounds. These checks do not establish anatomical fidelity, a joined lumen, an ostium, a flow direction or a dissection plane.

Each of the three files has one root display owner: the heart aggregate. The new nested scene contains only the two child selections in its separate 40,996-byte GLB (SHA256 `4dbd938c5cde865a0f7f66957ec3304965530a5b7744d93a85e95531ce827b12`); the parent aggregate is absent under every visibility, Undo/Redo and reassembly state. The existing chamber catalog remains six records (four selectable spaces and two context walls) and its GLB is unchanged. The new mode retains the original 2,086 indexed source triangles under the common transform, recomputing only shading normals.

## Navigation and teaching

Selecting Heart offers an **Explore coronary venous parts** action alongside the existing chamber action. The compact heart study menu can switch between the two. Search and source-bound v2 links identify either child; a changed parent or child bundle hash rejects a stale link. Small-vein FJ2724 and FJ2731 remain one selection and are never named separately. No optional great or middle vein context is added to this scene, so no surface is presented as a connected junction.

Two original anatomy/function teaching drafts are added with source-pinned, extend-only bindings. Clinical, pathology and imaging topics remain pending. The review queue receives two new unsigned contexts with independent source tokens; existing review bindings remain byte-equivalent. The identification-practice whitelist remains unchanged, so these veins are excluded from that exercise.

The source gate, exporter and focused validator check official concept rows, active holds, raw hashes, topology, transformed vertices and indices, full-record parent identity, one visible owner, layer history and stale-link rejection. Relevant shared navigation, learning, teaching, review and cardiac regressions must pass before integration. Local desktop browser QA at `/regions/thorax` confirmed Heart launch, a posterior scene with exactly the two labelled venous surfaces and no heart aggregate, sinus hide/Undo/Redo/reassembly, small-vein selection, switching between chamber and venous studies, and Back restoring Heart selection and launcher focus. This bounded visual/interaction check does not establish anatomical continuity, mobile/device behavior, clinical accuracy or radiologist approval.
