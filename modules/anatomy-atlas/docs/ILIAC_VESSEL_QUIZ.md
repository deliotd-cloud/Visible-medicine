# Iliac vessel questions

Six original formative questions replace identification-only placeholders for
12 retained bilateral common, external and internal iliac artery/vein selections.
Existing Quiz controls show one question for the selected structure, with answer
feedback, explanation and reading link. No additional navigation is introduced.

## Factual references

- [TTUHSC pelvic arteries](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_pelvis_perineum.html): usual common-iliac branching, external-iliac/femoral naming boundary and internal-iliac supply.
- [TTUHSC abdominal veins](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/veins_abdomen.html): paired common-iliac union into the inferior vena cava.
- [TTUHSC pelvic veins](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/veins_pelvis_perineum.html): vesical-plexus drainage toward internal iliac tributaries.
- [UCSF anatomy core](https://orthosurgery.ucsf.edu/igot/ucsf-ortho-anatomy-core): external-iliac/femoral continuity at the inguinal ligament, in the anterior acetabular vascular-anatomy discussion.

These are factual reading citations, not permission to redistribute the sources'
text, illustrations or question banks. Wording is original. Model licences and
holds are unchanged; no new media, packages or paid service are added.
