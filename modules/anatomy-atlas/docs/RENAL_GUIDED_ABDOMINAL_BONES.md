## Abdominal skeletal context

21 exact existing skeletal selections now have original draft teaching: paired
hip bones, sacrum, L1–L5, xiphoid and paired ribs7–12. Seven factual families are
qualified by side/level. Rib7's direct cartilage connection, rib8–10's usual
indirect connection and rib11–12's floating/costotransverse distinction are
explicit; cartilage and missing thoracic context are not fabricated.

Anatomy, Function and six extended topics (Clinical, Pathology, CT, MRI, X-ray,
Ultrasound) plus model-scope self-checks use the existing collapsed Learn panel.
These are introductory drafts, not comprehensive disease curricula or exams.
Imaging notes distinguish modality capabilities from an unregistered surface;
they provide no patient pixels, acquired findings or automatic approved links.
Bones receive no muscle Origin/Insertion labels. All eight muscle lessons and
their review title maps remain unchanged. The skeletal reference map is scoped
to skeletal review records, so unused new references do not invalidate muscles.

Exact full source definitions and selected surfaces are required. Mutated names,
IDs, FMA, side, tissue, bundle/node, source hashes, recipes and frames fail closed.
All 356 independent source packets remain unchanged. 103 teaching contexts
change (82 renal guides and 21 bone lessons); the other253 remain unchanged.
Renderer revisions are separate; old approvals are not inherited. Imaging
approval remains blocked without cleared registered image evidence.

## References and rights

New anatomy facts are independently verified with Texas Tech/UAMS anatomy tables
and University of the West Indies thoracic teaching; imaging facts with ACR
primary publication abstracts, ACR/RSNA RadiologyInfo and AAOS. Reference URLs
and titles live in `content/abdominal-bone-teaching.ts`. No source prose, table,
image, PDF, scan or diagram is redistributed. A citation is not a content licence
or endorsement; copyrighted reading is linked only. Current OpenStax pages
show non-commercial/AI restrictions and are not used in this new teaching slice.
This does not retrospectively adjudicate older source grants or clear unrelated
material. Clinical correctness and rights clearance remain distinct gates.

Existing HRA meshes retain their admitted CC BY4.0 notices. Existing abdominal
v3 assets remain CC BY-SA2.1 Japan with source downloads and recipients' rights.
No new font, texture, dataset, dependency, external API, payment or mandatory fee.

## Required acceptance

`npm run renal-guided-abdominal-bones:test` covers exact definitions, source guards,
all356 source/teaching comparisons, 309 stale/foreign submissions before storage,
controlled actual supplements and real Learn markup. Historical pelvic and
wall/back review tests stay pinned to their delivered milestones; the new test
checks the actual combined state, rather than weakening original deltas.
Check renderer, requirements, existing source/teaching/navigation/review tests,
types and regional build before saving/exporting. Tests do not certify actual GPU,
browser, device, anatomical fidelity or clinical approval.

Radiologist review must inspect every guide step and selected skeletal lesson:
source limits, laterality/numbering, visible anatomy, captions, modality guidance,
question/answer quality and evidence. Browser checks include camera sweep,
occlusion, reduced motion, failures, Exit restoration, keyboard and enlarged text.
CT-head accepted masks and desktop clinical PACS remain outside this work.
