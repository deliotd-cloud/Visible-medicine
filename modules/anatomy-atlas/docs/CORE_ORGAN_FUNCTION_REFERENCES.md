## Permissions and attribution

[NHLBI policy](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/about/contact/trademark-branding-and-logo)
describes most site information as public domain with explicit exceptions. It
asks that formatted publications and media not be modified and that material
not imply endorsement or serve as advertising. This Atlas supplies independently
worded factual teaching, not modified NHLBI publications or branded multimedia.
The requested institutional source credit is included with the reading links.
No NIH/NHLBI logo, program mark or implied recommendation of Visible Medicine.

The two NHLBI topic pages visibly label embedded Nucleus Medical Media animations
and, on the lungs page, the medical illustration as copyrighted. Those assets
are neither downloaded nor reused. The text permission is not an image licence.

[NCI reuse policy](https://www.cancer.gov/policies/copyright-reuse), reviewed
12 March 2025, permits general text reuse unless a specific exception is stated,
with NCI credit and an original-page link. The Atlas gives its own short factual
summary, credits NCI and links the source. NCI graphics may have different owners;
no graphics, logos, PDQ marks or figures are included.

[MedlinePlus reuse terms](https://medlineplus.gov/about/using/usingcontent/)
explicitly classify medical-test information as public domain and request NLM
credit. The liver note retains that credit and links the test article as a
factual reference for albumin synthesis. No A.D.A.M. encyclopedia material,
licensed image, drug monograph, lab-result advice or test protocol is imported.
The summary does not frame or impersonate a MedlinePlus page.

[NIDDK copyright policy](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/copyright), reviewed May 2024,
allows most site text to be reused, distinguishing third-party documents/graphics
and requiring logos to be removed from edited content. The nutrient-processing
summary is original, credits NIDDK and does not imply endorsement or recommend
medical advice. No branded publication or third-party media is reused.

These are content-specific checks, not a blanket .gov licence assumption or a
guarantee about future third-party materials. Original code/text follows the
project's existing licence. No added paid service or runtime reference fetching.

## Clinical and geometric limits

These notes describe general physiology. They do not validate root geometry,
existing nested components, a physiological simulation or a patient's function.
Heart/liver aggregate exclusions and root source identities must remain explicit.
Pulmonary/cardiac nested views remain available; the root selection must not be
described as a complete segmented microscopic or functional system. No CT, MRI,
ultrasound, X-ray or lecture resource is unlocked by these notes. The owner must
review the actual source-bound revision before any clinical acceptance claim.
