# Abdominal vessel and connective X-ray teaching drafts

Sixteen retained abdominal selections have original X-ray orientation lessons:
twelve vascular selections and four connective surfaces. They supplement the
four gastric venous lessons in this website integration batch. All remain
drafts for the owner's revision-bound radiologist review.

## Anatomy and modality scope

Vascular lessons distinguish arterial supply from venous drainage, conditional
contrast demonstration from plain-film visibility, and an observed patient
junction from a reference mesh. Pancreaticoduodenal and hepatic tributary groups
keep their original components without invented child names. The source-labelled
ileocolic ascending branch remains an unvalidated source label, not independently
confirmed by its territory. The ileal selection is interpreted within regional
small-bowel drainage, not claimed to be a complete tributary tree.

Connective lessons distinguish the linea alba from a measured abdominal-wall
defect, and three mesenteric folds from the bowel lumen. Plain-film and contrast
limits are educational inferences from these different imaging targets, not
results of a visibility study on the retained surfaces. Model contact, explosion
and screen position do not establish continuity, flow, operative planes,
patient-side markers or spatial registration.

[ACR/RSNA abdominal radiography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/abdominrad),
[contrast](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/safety-),
[angiography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiocath) and

[An epigastric cadaver study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3830133/)
supports course variability, while a [selected right-colon cancer surgical series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8922185/)
supports pancreaticoduodenal venous variability, not routine X-ray visibility.

These are reading links only. No publisher prose, figures, tables, scans or
datasets are imported, adapted or relicensed. Original code and teaching retain
project MIT terms; existing BodyParts3D credits remain in place. No new asset,
font, dependency or paid service is introduced.

## Review and verification

`abdominal-detail-xray:test` binds each complete source identity, including
ordered source files and existing coverage limits. It checks the sixteen
pending-to-draft transitions, preservation of other teaching and geometry,
identity mutations and refusal of stale or forged review submissions before
storage. Clinical review must check terminology, anatomical relationships,
modality wording and each exact displayed teaching revision. No lesson approves
an acquired image, model geometry, clinical use or publication. Atlas, Education
case and paid-lecture access remain independent.
