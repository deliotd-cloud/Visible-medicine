## Evidence and limits

- [Wilson et al., respiratory effects in humans](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2278403/)
  supports regional variation in external-intercostal mechanics. The question
  deliberately avoids assigning one action to every slip.
- [TTUHSC El Paso muscle table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_thorax.html)
  supports layer/neurovascular relationships and transversus attachments.
- [NHLBI breathing overview](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/lungs/breathing-benefits)
  supports diaphragm descent and increased chest volume during inspiration.
