(sartorius, gracilis, semitendinosus) with the same-side tibia. Search for
`pes anserinus` in Whole body, confirm the sided view, then select, remove,
restore or separate a surface using existing controls.

This is a cross-region relationship study: muscles remain in Thigh and tibiae
remain in Leg. No source identity or region membership is rewritten, and no
nonfunctional regional entry is added. The study does not replace broader thigh,
popliteus or genicular views. No new permanent controls or geometry are added.
