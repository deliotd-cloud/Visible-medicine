8 September 2026: 84 original draft sections across 42 existing muscle/head representations. Sixteen definitions cover ECU, FDS, APL/EPB, brachioradialis, ECRB, ECRL, ED/EDM/EI, EPL, FCR, FDP, FPL, palmaris longus, pronator quadratus, supinator, pronator-teres heads and FCU heads.

## Learning scope

Distinguish tendon injury from neurological weakness; de Quervain's APL/EPB compartment from EPL rupture; proximal ECRB disease from other extensor disorders; FDS middle-joint action from FDP fingertip action; AIN motor-only patterns from broader median neuropathy; and PIN motor palsy from pain-dominant radial tunnel presentations. Palmaris absence remains a normal variant. ECU excursion in asymptomatic wrists and the small FCR case series are not universal diagnostic or management thresholds.

Grouped heads, digital tendons, compartments and nerve territories are not independently segmented. Each ECU/FDS selection retains two official components: 46 components overall. No abnormal geometry, scan, clinical examination protocol, treatment plan or approval is added.
