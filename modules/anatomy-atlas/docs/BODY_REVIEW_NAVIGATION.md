# Sequential body review navigation

The selected worksheet offers **Previous structure** and **Next structure**
within the current filtered body-review queue. The position is a place in that
queue, not a count of reviewed or approved structures. Region, system and search
remain unchanged. Crossing a 20-item page boundary keeps the queue on the selected
structure's page; either boundary disables the unavailable direction.

Navigation never saves or approves. Unsaved edits use the existing discard
confirmation: Cancel retains the structure and draft; confirmation discards the
unsaved draft and loads the requested structure. Selecting the already-active
structure is a no-op. Save or export needed edits before leaving. Saved review
history, revision checks, personal access and approval requirements are unchanged.

The controls are confined to the private body review workspace. They add no
learner toolbar, model, teaching text, paid dependency or patient correspondence.
Shoulder, nested and independent specimen reviews remain distinct scopes.
Source and website checkpoint evidence determine delivery status; this document
does not establish hosted publication, clinical approval or device certification.
