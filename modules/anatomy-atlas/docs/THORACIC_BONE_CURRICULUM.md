8 September 2026: 54 original Anatomy/Function drafts for all 27 existing thorax-primary skeletal entries: twelve bilateral rib pairs and three sternal components. Fifteen lesson definitions distinguish each rib level and each sternal part. The existing panels, navigation and geometry are unchanged; all teaching remains draft.

## Source scope and limits

The independent validator pins 27 exact FMA/file pairs against the cached official BodyParts3D v4 ISA index. Each entry has one source component. Rib laterality is retained; manubrium, sternal body and xiphoid are midline. The resolver checks exact FMA, bone/system, primary and secondary thorax membership, expected laterality and tab. The right/left order of paired lesson IDs is explicit and tested. No vertebral lesson, costal-cartilage entry or shoulder record is reclassified.

Ribs 3–9 have level-specific posterior articulation notes rather than an identical generic paragraph. The first/second rib relationships, lower-rib tip variation and absent costotransverse joints at ribs 11/12 are distinguished. The tenth rib is not asserted to have a universal anterior attachment. Sternal selections are components, not three separate adult bones or freely mobile dissection planes. Xiphoid shape/ossification are variable, not a fixed-age rule.

These are exterior bone surfaces. Named facets, grooves, marrow, cortical thickness, cartilage and joint cavities are not newly segmented or independently validated. Bone does not actively contract; translation is not breathing mechanics. No rib excursion, neurovascular clearance, pleural/kidney boundary, compression target, surgical plane or clinical diagnosis is certified. Source warnings and the two earlier Function holds remain intact.

## Factual references and rights

References read on 8 September 2026, with the thoracic vertebra reference also read in the preceding spinal milestone:

- [Rib anatomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK538328/): bounded skeletal features and broad respiratory role. Its introductory claim about all posterior articulations is not generalized to atypical ribs, and its incorrect rotator-cuff description of pectoralis minor is not adopted.
- [Thoracic vertebral facets](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459153/): typical shared rib-head articulations and atypical lower levels.
- [Sternum](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK541141/): three components, relationships, support and variable xiphoid morphology.
- [Sternal angle](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459336/): second-cartilage/manubriosternal relationship; no universal fusion age or exact patient-level plane is adopted.
- [2023 costal-margin cadaver study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37982795/): indexed abstract read; direct page returned no text. Findings support qualifying anterior attachment variability, not transferring study frequencies or diagnoses to this model. [2022 pilot abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36121266/) and [2024 interchondral study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39238097/) were also visible in search; no full text, figures or data tables were imported.

The consulted [OpenStax thoracic-cage page](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-4-the-thoracic-cage) currently displays NC-SA and AI-use restrictions and did not supply chapter text in the accessible response. It is excluded from the new runtime references/assets. A scoped source search found only the pre-existing citation links in `lib/neuroanatomy.ts`/`docs/DEEP_BRAIN.md`, not an imported OpenStax asset. This is not a blanket rights determination about all earlier editions or grants.

Only brief original factual notes are authored. No publisher prose, diagram, scan, table or dataset is redistributed. StatPearls NC-ND and publisher copyrights are not commercial asset licences. Original application code/text retain MIT terms; source-index evidence retains separate DBCLS BodyParts3D CC BY4 attribution/change obligations.
