# Pulmonary venous guided learning

**Pulmonary veins: paired hilar relationships** is a four-stop draft in Thorax
and Whole body Guided learning. It highlights the existing right superior,
right inferior, left superior and left inferior pulmonary-vein selections,
with the ipsilateral main bronchus and pulmonary artery faded for context.
Both stops on each side share a camera frame and retain assembled geometry.
The existing 1.8-second eased transitions, pause, Previous/Next, step picker,
reduced-motion option and restoration on exit remain in use.

## Learning and source scope

The captions orient the supplied exterior segments, rather than reconstructing
a complete venous tree. Original wording cites
[TTUHSC El Paso hilar anatomy](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/lungs_ans.html).
The four existing pulmonary-venous quick checks and CT/MRI reading notes remain
available through the normal folded teaching controls; they are not duplicated.

Anatomical left and right do not refer to screen position. Grouped source files
are not validated tributaries or atrial ostia. The model does not establish a
visible left-atrial connection, full lobar drainage, blood flow or a procedural
route. CT/MRI notes are conceptual teaching, not an acquired thoracic scan or a
registered patient model. Didanix Education/light and independent Atlas, case
and lecture access remain unchanged.

Existing BodyParts3D geometry retains its source attribution and licence.
Only original code and captions are added; no new image, mesh, dependency or
paid service is required. The reading reference grants no right to copy its
prose, illustrations or dataset.

## Clinical review

In private Clinical Review, open any of the four veins or four context
structures and inspect its guided-learning evidence. The complete sequence,
eight exact source records, two bundle identities, camera frames and transition
policy belong to the new teaching revision. Prior decisions do not approve it.
Review identification, anatomical relationships, visibility, captions and
camera smoothness before signing off the exact revision.
