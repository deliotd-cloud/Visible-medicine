## Evidence and boundaries

- [Bidic et al., 2010](https://utsouthwestern.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/dorsal-hand-anatomy-relevant-to-volumetric-rejuvenation/): primary institutional abstract for dorsal-hand cadaver/Doppler research. Supports venous/tendon layer orientation; small sample, not comprehensive tributary mapping.
- [Elmegarhi et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28417223/): primary study of pattern variation, with surface/tourniquet or near-infrared assessment, not a sonographic visibility study.
