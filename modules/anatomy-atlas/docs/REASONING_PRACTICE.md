# Source-bound anatomical reasoning pilot

Open **Shoulder & arm, Forearm, Hand, Hip & thigh, Knee & leg or Foot → Practice → Practice options → Apply anatomy · draft**. Whole-body practice can also use these concepts when their muscles are in the loaded visible scope. Pelvis shares the two gluteal concepts when their alternatives are visible; this is not a complete pelvic question set. Other regions show an honest unavailable state with links to supported regions. No new top-level control or note tab was added.

Sixty original draft concepts cover ten shoulder/arm muscles/heads, six forearm muscles, ten hand muscle/head/group selections, twelve hip/thigh muscles or heads, fourteen lower-leg muscles/heads and eight foot selections. Each is bound to the two existing sided FMA/file identities (120 representations), checked against the retained official BodyParts3D element index. There is no label-based matching, inferred nerve geometry or new mesh. Forearm head/group entries outside the six exact muscles are not silently treated as whole muscles.

Shoulder/arm: supraspinatus, infraspinatus, subscapularis, teres minor/major, serratus anterior, brachialis, coracobrachialis and the long heads of biceps/triceps. Forearm: pronator quadratus, supinator, brachioradialis, extensor carpi radialis longus, flexor carpi radialis and flexor pollicis longus. These sixteen earlier question records are hash-pinned unchanged by the hand extension.

Hand: three hypothenar selections, abductor pollicis brevis, opponens pollicis, the two separate adductor heads, and the existing lumbrical/palmar-interosseous/dorsal-interosseous sets. The grouped meshes remain grouped: their presence neither validates an individual muscle count nor supplies per-digit segmentation. Missing intrinsic structures, such as an independently selectable flexor pollicis brevis, are not inferred. Each adductor-head prompt specifically distinguishes that head, not the entire muscle.

Hip/thigh: rectus femoris, vastus intermedius, sartorius, gracilis, adductor longus/magnus, semitendinosus, semimembranosus, both biceps femoris heads and gluteus maximus/medius. The two gluteal selections preserve exact ordered `thigh, pelvis` memberships, rather than changing the catalogue or accepting arbitrary regions. Their appearance in both regional scopes does not duplicate them in whole-body sessions. All 26 earlier question records are hash-pinned unchanged.

Lower leg: both tibialis muscles, all three supplied fibularis muscles, the four long toe flexors/extensors, soleus, popliteus, plantaris and the two gastrocnemius heads. These cover the 28 sided muscle/head records currently assigned primarily to this region; they do not establish complete leg anatomy. Foot: abductor hallucis, abductor digiti minimi, flexor digiti minimi brevis, flexor digitorum brevis, flexor accessorius, extensor hallucis brevis and the two adductor hallucis heads. The retained flexor accessorius label is explicit; toe slips and its individual heads are not invented. All 38 earlier question records are hash-pinned unchanged.

Questions test distinctions in attachments, joint actions or motor supply. Alternatives are curated, same-side and actually loaded/visible; at least one alternative must remain. Each session asks a concept once, not again on its opposite side, and retains the existing maximum of 20 questions even when more concepts are eligible. Focus selection and retry respect current eligibility, including lost distractors. Landmark size preference is not applied to authored concepts; the setup note explains this.

Choose an answer button or a displayed alternative on the model. Both are graded by the same answer-once session reducer. Names are offered as answer choices; there is no correct-answer hint in labels, landmarks, selected-structure highlighting or an isolated-only target. Long stems remain in the Practice panel; the model overlay stays short. Skip counts as missed. Explanations and sources appear after answer/skip, and can be reviewed in completed results. Questions and results remain in memory, not a learner record/database.

`lib/atlas-practice.ts` wraps the unchanged identification engine, so the dedicated shoulder's existing practice and source/review fingerprints are preserved. `app/reasoning-feedback.tsx` gates explanation/citation rendering on an actual response. `lib/reasoning-questions.ts` composes the original prompts/rationales and regional `*-reasoning.ts` modules, including forearm, hand, thigh, leg and foot. They contain exact bindings, explicit regions, revisions and factual source references. An identity cannot inherit a question after its primary region, ordered memberships, side or source component IDs change. This identity contract is not a fresh geometry-byte audit.

## Lower-leg and foot extension — 9 September 2026

Twenty-two new original questions distinguish attachments, movement combinations and selected motor-supply facts. Each stem was checked against its three exact, same-side alternatives; a similar name or shared action must not make a distractor satisfy the complete clue. The two gastrocnemius and two adductor hallucis heads remain separate questions about source heads, not generic whole-muscle substitutions. This is authoring review, not independent medical/educator approval.
