# Source-bound anatomical reasoning pilot

Open **Practice → Practice options → Apply anatomy · draft** in a body region. Spine & back, Thorax and Abdomen now join the existing head/neck and limb regions. Whole-body practice uses concepts whose targets and alternatives are loaded and visible. Pelvis shares two gluteal concepts; Abdomen currently offers external oblique and the shared lumbar rotator, not a complete abdominal-wall set. Narrow scopes with no valid alternative show an explicit unavailable state. No new top-level control or note tab was added.

One hundred original draft concepts bind to 196 existing representations: the previous 80 concepts/160 sided selections plus 20 trunk/back concepts/36 selections. The extension has sixteen paired concepts and four explicitly midline-tagged concepts (diaphragm and three bilateral intercostal groups). Nine representations have multiple files; complete official source membership and retained order are required. Pectoralis major alone explicitly uses the `partof` source tree; earlier bindings still default to `isa`. No label matching, relabelled sides, inferred nerves or new meshes are introduced. Existing 80 question records remain hash-pinned unchanged.

Shoulder/arm: supraspinatus, infraspinatus, subscapularis, teres minor/major, serratus anterior, brachialis, coracobrachialis and the long heads of biceps/triceps. Forearm: pronator quadratus, supinator, brachioradialis, extensor carpi radialis longus, flexor carpi radialis and flexor pollicis longus. These sixteen earlier question records are hash-pinned unchanged by the hand extension.

Hand: three hypothenar selections, abductor pollicis brevis, opponens pollicis, the two separate adductor heads, and the existing lumbrical/palmar-interosseous/dorsal-interosseous sets. The grouped meshes remain grouped: their presence neither validates an individual muscle count nor supplies per-digit segmentation. Missing intrinsic structures, such as an independently selectable flexor pollicis brevis, are not inferred. Each adductor-head prompt specifically distinguishes that head, not the entire muscle.

Hip/thigh: rectus femoris, vastus intermedius, sartorius, gracilis, adductor longus/magnus, semitendinosus, semimembranosus, both biceps femoris heads and gluteus maximus/medius. The two gluteal selections preserve exact ordered `thigh, pelvis` memberships, rather than changing the catalogue or accepting arbitrary regions. Their appearance in both regional scopes does not duplicate them in whole-body sessions. All 26 earlier question records are hash-pinned unchanged.

Lower leg: both tibialis muscles, all three supplied fibularis muscles, the four long toe flexors/extensors, soleus, popliteus, plantaris and the two gastrocnemius heads. These cover the 28 sided muscle/head records currently assigned primarily to this region; they do not establish complete leg anatomy. Foot: abductor hallucis, abductor digiti minimi, flexor digiti minimi brevis, flexor digitorum brevis, flexor accessorius, extensor hallucis brevis and the two adductor hallucis heads. The retained flexor accessorius label is explicit; toe slips and its individual heads are not invented. All 38 earlier question records are hash-pinned unchanged.

Head/neck: the six globe-moving muscles plus levator palpebrae; digastric, geniohyoid, mylohyoid, stylohyoid, omohyoid, sternohyoid, sternothyroid and thyrohyoid; posterior/lateral cricoarytenoid, oblique arytenoid, thyroarytenoid and vocalis. This is a source-bound subset, not complete head/neck, cranial-nerve, speech or swallowing teaching. All 60 earlier questions are hash-pinned unchanged.

Questions test distinctions in attachments, joint actions or motor supply. Alternatives are curated, have matching catalogue laterality and are actually loaded/visible; at least one must remain. The four midline-tagged selections compare only with each other, never an invented left/right subdivision. Each session asks a concept once, not again on its opposite side, and retains the maximum of 20 questions. Focus selection and retry respect current eligibility, including lost distractors. Landmark size preference is not applied to authored concepts; the setup note explains this.

Choose an answer button or a displayed alternative on the model. Both are graded by the same answer-once session reducer. Names are offered as answer choices; there is no correct-answer hint in labels, landmarks, selected-structure highlighting or an isolated-only target. Long stems remain in the Practice panel; the model overlay stays short. Skip counts as missed. Explanations and sources appear after answer/skip, and can be reviewed in completed results. Questions and results remain in memory, not a learner record/database.

`lib/atlas-practice.ts` wraps the unchanged identification engine, so the dedicated shoulder's existing practice and source/review fingerprints are preserved. `app/reasoning-feedback.tsx` gates explanation/citation rendering on an actual response. `lib/reasoning-questions.ts` composes the original prompts/rationales and regional `*-reasoning.ts` modules. They contain exact bindings, explicit regions, revisions and factual source references. A binding uses either one `file` or an ordered `files` tuple with two or more members, never both. An identity cannot inherit a question after its primary region, ordered memberships, side or complete ordered source component IDs change. This identity contract is not a fresh geometry-byte audit.
