# Infant cranial ultrasound orientation drafts

Ten exact existing root-body selections: paired caudate nuclei, thalami,
putamina and globi pallidi, plus corpus callosum and the source choroid-plexus
group. Only their Ultrasound teaching changes. Source meshes remain unchanged
and unvalidated; these are not neonatal geometries, acquired images or mappings
to the owner's adult CT/MRI studies. An open, suitable infant acoustic window
is essential to the evidence context. No normative dimensions or diagnostic
thresholds, pathological images, procedure instructions or Doppler protocols
are supplied. Clinical sign-off must identify the new teaching revisions.

## Evidence and reuse boundary

- [AIUM/ACR/SPR/SRU 2024 parameter](https://doi.org/10.1002/jum.16551): infant-window and standard coronal/parasagittal targets; exact putamen/pallidum and plexus context.
- [Shuman et al., 1981](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6974980/): original sector sonographic/neonatal anatomical correlation for caudate and thalamic landmarks. The author is Shuman, not Grant.
- [Pigadas et al., 1981](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6974979/): original in-vivo/in-vitro sonographic and specimen correlation, including basal ganglia and thalami; not a visibility rate for every subdivision.
- [Babcock, 1984](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6709917/): original callosal sonographic observations; no population reference ranges are transferred.
