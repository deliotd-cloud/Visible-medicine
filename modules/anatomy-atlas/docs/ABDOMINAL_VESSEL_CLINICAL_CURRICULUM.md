## Required clinical validation and next work

Vascular, gastrointestinal, hepatobiliary, renal and radiology reviewers must check source extent, actual anatomy/variant relationships, clinical relevance and emergency wording. In particular:

- Distinguish acute mesenteric ischaemia, colonic ischaemia and venous congestion.
- Distinguish portal/arterial inflow from hepatic venous outflow.
- Do not diagnose symptomatic celiac compression from appearance alone.
- Do not treat a native mesh as a transplant, endoscopic or angiographic targeting model.
- Do not infer continuity, a safe ligation point, resection margin, bowel viability or an embolisation route from exploded views.

FMA45097/FMA45098, FMA19728 and FMA61970 remain held. No blood-pressure targets, anticoagulant doses, aneurysm repair thresholds, shunt routes or device choices are supplied. Future real US/CT/MRI correlation needs separately licensed/de-identified imaging and validated registration.
