# Spinal-bone teaching

8 September 2026: 50 original Anatomy/Function drafts for the existing 25 spinal-bone representations: C1–C7, T1–T12, L1–L5 and the fused sacrum. ## Identity and presentation limits

The validator independently pins the exact FMA/file pairs against the cached official BodyParts3D v4 ISA index: 25 entries, 25 source components. Canonical region is spine and laterality is midline; secondary neck, thorax, abdomen and pelvic/thigh memberships remain unchanged. The resolver guards FMA, system, category, primary/secondary spine membership, laterality and tab. Existing shoulder teaching and all unrelated topics remain untouched.

Atlas/axis teaching distinguishes the upper-neck articulations and absent C1–C2 disc. C7 vascular entry and process shape are qualified; T9/T10 facet variation is not treated as an observed mesh diagnosis. T11/T12 do not inherit upper-thoracic transverse costal facets. Lumbar load-bearing and lumbosacral numbering remain reference anatomy, not patient measurements. The sacrum stays one fused-bone selection, not five separable vertebrae.

Named processes/facets are context, not newly segmented landmarks. No cortical thickness, marrow, interior bone architecture, facet cartilage or validated joint clearance is supplied. Explode is not physiological spinal movement. A bony canal is not a spinal cord, and a vertebral label does not establish the corresponding neural segment. The coccyx, canal contents and each sacral foramen are not certified independent components of the sacrum selection. The two earlier Function holds (FMA19728 and FMA61970) remain pending.
