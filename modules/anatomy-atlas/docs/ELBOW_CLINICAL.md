# Elbow collateral and recurrent artery clinical drafts

Fourteen existing bilateral elbow-artery selections now provide source-bound
Clinical and Pathology drafts through the existing teaching panel. Seven paired
families distinguish arm-side collateral routes from forearm-side recurrent
routes and keep medial, lateral and posterior comparisons separate. The notes
do not assert donor-specific junctions, continuous lumens, collateral adequacy,
perfusion, injury, diagnosis or treatment.

The same exact selections remain available in the Shoulder & arm and Forearm
workspaces. Their Anatomy, Function and Quiz teaching, source identities,
coordinates, arterial-connection guides, separation behavior and 353,176-byte
`elbow-arteries` bundle are unchanged. CT, MRI, X-ray and Ultrasound remain
pending: 56 fallback bodies now state that the Clinical and Pathology drafts are
separate from unreviewed modality teaching. No scan or modality lesson was added.

`npm run elbow-clinical:test` checks all 14 canonical source identities, 84
declared payload changes (28 new drafts and 56 pending-copy corrections), all 28
actual React note renders, changed-source and changed-topic rejection, the 1,102
record schema, exact before/after reconstruction, mixed-state and unrelated-drift
rejection, unchanged recipes and geometry, and the source bundle bytes/hash. The
validation report is `docs/elbow-clinical-validation.json`.

Reference and reuse evidence is recorded separately in
`docs/ELBOW_CLINICAL_REFERENCES_20260917.md` and the project notices. These are
introductory educational drafts, not anatomical validation, clinical approval,
browser/device acceptance or publication. Revision-bound radiologist review and
independent Atlas, case and paid-lecture access remain required.
