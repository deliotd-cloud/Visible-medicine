# Elbow arterial ultrasound evidence follow-up

Six retained bilateral selections gain original, source-bound orientation drafts:
recurrent interosseous, anterior ulnar recurrent and radial collateral arteries.
The existing eight elbow US lessons, quizzes, geometry and tours are unchanged.
Both middle collateral US selections remain pending: the screened anatomical
and surgical sources do not establish direct branch-specific ultrasound evidence.
That evidence gap is not proof that the branch cannot be visualised.

## Reading evidence and limits

- [Cheng et al. 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9509653/):
  abstract-only support for perforator Doppler mapping with operative correlation.
  Do not transfer it to complete recurrent-artery sonographic detection.
- [Wei et al. 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4229283/):
  indexed primary full text separates clinical perforator localisation from
  anatomical parent-origin findings; no branch-specific detection rate follows.
- [Sun et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.7863/ultra.15.01032):
  primary abstract supports radial collateral Doppler mapping in a selected
  volunteer/cadaver comparison, not universal clinical visibility.

These are reading references. No publication prose, figures, tables, scans,
models or datasets are imported, traced or adapted. Wei's article has a
CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 reuse restriction: its media/text are not admitted as commercial
assets. New project prose/code are original MIT work. Existing BodyParts3D
CC BY 4.0 credits are preserved; no asset, dependency, service or fee is added.
