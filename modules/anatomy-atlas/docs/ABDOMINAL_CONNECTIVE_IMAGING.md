## References and scope

- [Gueroult et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38177404/): CT-based
  linea-alba morphometry,117 examinations. Supports describing shape/width
  variability; does not establish disease from this Atlas surface. No cohort
  width is applied as a diagnostic threshold.
- [Beer et al., 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19637295/): ultrasound
  measurements at specified abdominal-wall levels in150 nulliparous women.
  The limited study population is explicit, not a universal normal reference.
- [Okino et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11706218/): CT/anatomy
  correlation for the mesenteric root and its connections. Root-oriented
  vessel landmarks do not validate every fold of the larger selected surface.
- [Dodds et al., 1993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8210588/): mesocolic
  vascular landmarks, including the middle colic vessels for transverse
  mesocolon. A vessel remains distinct from the supporting mesentery.

The latter two are radiology anatomy reviews/correlative descriptions, not
population-based segmentation validation. Short original explanatory text is
used; no copied abstract, figure, slide, table, image or patient data is bundled.
Access to a paper is not permission to reuse its media. Existing source-mesh
attribution remains unchanged; no new dependency, font, service or fee.

## Deliberate pending topics

MRI for all three, mesenteric ultrasound and all mesoappendix imaging remain
pending. Finding evidence about the appendix does not establish the visibility
or boundaries of the mesoappendix. The source's unspecified laterality is not
changed. No generic imaging filler is used to inflate coverage.

## Verification and review

`npm run abdominal-connective-imaging:test` checks exact identities, original
pending topics, immutable source-bundle hashes, the teaching transition, content
contracts, revision-bound review material and rejection of altered source fields.
The pre-edit application is independently rebuilt from its saved Git commit;
all other topics, shoulder content and dissection profiles must match it exactly.
The immediate preceding teaching history is reconstructed explicitly rather
than rewriting its saved evidence or migrating any approval.

Primary references support introductory draft orientation only. The owner
radiologist must review anatomical identity, modality wording and limitations
against the exact revision before clinical publication. Future patient-image
correlation additionally needs authorised cleared cases and verified spatial
registration in Didanix Education/light, with independent case/Atlas/lecture
entitlements. Local test success does not establish those external gates.

The coordinating checkpoint records actual checks, rendered viewport evidence,
GitHub/D recovery and deployment status. Source-only changes do not imply that
the separately generated website runtime is already updated.
