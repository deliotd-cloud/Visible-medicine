## Independent acceptance still needed

Specialists must check clinical wording, evidence applicability, urgent-care advice and real source extents, including:

- Pituitary hormone excess versus deficiency and pressure effects.
- Lens versus retinal disease, urgent visual symptoms and absent independent retinal-layer coverage.
- Oral lesion warnings without diagnosing cancer from a reference surface.
- Tear production versus canalicular/sac/duct disease; no inferred patent lumen, valve or infant membrane.
- Submandibular obstruction versus sublingual-region saliva collections and unvalidated floor-of-mouth planes.
- Epiglottitis urgency, adult/child applicability and the inability of a static model to establish airway safety.

No physiological motion, visual function, endocrine test, tumour stage, operative/device path, patient scan or imaging registration is provided. Browser/device testing and clinical approval remain outstanding.
