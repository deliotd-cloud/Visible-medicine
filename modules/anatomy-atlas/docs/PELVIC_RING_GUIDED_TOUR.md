# Pelvic ring & hips — draft guided orientation

Six stops reuse five exact whole-bone root-body selections: paired hip bones
(FMA16586/16587), sacrum (FMA16202) and paired femora (FMA24474/24475).
The existing Pelvis and Whole body Guided learning selectors expose the tour;
the original pelvic organ tour remains the default. No new toolbar, model,
source frame or separate landmark selection is introduced.

The first two stops retain a three-bone ring camera window. Lateral and inferior
hip views focus one existing bone; the final two windows compare each hip bone
with its same-sided whole femur. The right hip is revisited with a different
stop identity; overview framing uses the unique target union. Explicit duplicate,
missing or foreign frame IDs still fail closed. The femora remain entire meshes,
not cropped or separately segmented proximal-bone teaching.

All surfaces stay assembled. Existing 1800 ms quintic-orbit transitions provide
smooth starts/stops; reduced motion selects immediate transitions. Reading,
manual camera interaction, unavailable bundles and renderer failure preserve
the existing pause/no-implicit-resume behaviour. Five selections require all
three source bundles before playback; no hidden approximate replacement.
