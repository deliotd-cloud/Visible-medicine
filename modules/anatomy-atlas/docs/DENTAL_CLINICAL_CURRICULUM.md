# Permanent-tooth clinical teaching

## Scope — 9 September 2026

Fifty-six original Clinical/Pathology drafts cover the 28 existing permanent-tooth selections, with one official ISA component per tooth. Six teaching groups share common context without merging the selectable identities. These are introductory drafts, not exhaustive dental curricula or clinical approval.

| Teaching group | Selections | Main example |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Incisors | 8 | Crown damage versus avulsion; adult/baby-tooth distinction |
| Upper canines | 2 | Impaction and neighbouring teeth |
| Lower canines | 2 | Periodontal support and assessment |
| Premolars | 8 | Cracks and symptoms that can be difficult to localise |
| Upper molars | 4 | Caries, including asymptomatic early disease |
| Lower molars | 4 | Abscess and urgent/emergency warning signs |

Disease examples are not exclusive to those tooth groups. Both sides, arches, central/lateral incisors and first/second premolars/molars remain independently bound. No wisdom teeth, primary dentition, FDI/Universal numbering or new geometry is invented. External surfaces do not independently segment enamel, dentine, pulp, canals or periodontal attachment; no real fracture, lesion depth, mobility or tissue vitality is simulated.
