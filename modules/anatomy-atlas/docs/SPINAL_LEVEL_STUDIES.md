# Focused spinal-level dissection

Select a participating vertebra/disc and open **Imaging → CT / MRI / X-ray** for [source-bound orientation notes](SPINE_IMAGING_TEACHING.md). These extend to 47 spinal bone/disc selections, not only the 15 targets below. The same compact panel is reused; real scans, soft-tissue segmentation and independent level registration remain absent.

Six new windows in **Spine → Study views** expose small existing source groups. They use the same compact searchable cards, selection, hiding, Undo/Redo, fade, cutaway, separation and source-bound study links; no new permanent panel, dependency or geometry is added. Search for a level, for example `L4`, or open a related study from a selected participating vertebra/disc.

Fifteen existing unique representations are reused (eleven bones and four discs). The root catalogue, original positions, geometry, IDs and bundle hashes are unchanged. Source names are retained; the shorthand level titles are teaching navigation, not independently verified patient level numbering or scan registration. Numeric source-frame checks confirm the four chosen disc centres lie between the nominated bone centres along the superior direction, but do not validate tissue surfaces or normal spacing.

All groups are midline and remain available under Left/Right filters, consistent with the existing atlas. C1–C2 starts posteriorly; the other groups start from the right side. Users can freely rotate and switch view. Window/focus equivalents share one card, not duplicate controls. Any structure can be set aside and restored through existing history. Return separation to zero to inspect the unchanged source relationship; exploded positions never become imaging coordinates.

## Anatomy and rights

The brief original prompts reference [TTUHSC El Paso back joint teaching](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/joints_back.html) and [atlantoaxial anatomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK563271/). They are factual reference links, not copied tables, diagrams, scans, articles or licences to republish those sources. Existing BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International, continues to govern the reused source geometry. No additional model, font, texture, dependency or paid service is introduced.

## Verification and limits

Dedicated checks cover exact FMA/source names, matching window/focus visibility, left/right/midline behaviour, one-card search, related study links, hiding/Undo/Redo/reset, exact preservation of all previous recipes and rejection of unrelated edits. Existing dissection workbench/guidance checks exercise shared behaviour. This is not browser/GPU, clinical or device acceptance.

Whole discs do not expose independently segmented annulus/nucleus; vertebral facets remain bone-surface features, not cartilage meshes. Spinal ligaments, meninges, roots, cauda equina and a validated spinal cord are not added. Do not infer stability, compression, pathology, surgical corridors or normal measurements. In particular, the T12–L1 model gap must not be interpreted as anatomy or disease. Clinical review and actual modality-specific registrations remain required.
