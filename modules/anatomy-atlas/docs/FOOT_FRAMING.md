# Foot framing — 13 September 2026

The Ankle & foot viewer now frames its actual foot-primary tissues instead of
letting long calf vessel/tendon extents dominate every viewing direction. Its
plantar starting direction and Both sides default are retained; single-side
views remain explicit choices. The existing View menu offers **Fit all sources**
and **Frame foot**, with a short close-up caption and no new permanent toolbar.

All 122 source selections remain available (61 per side): 106 foot-primary
selections define the regional preset; 16 proximal selections retain their
original full meshes and coordinates. Selecting a calf source, including the
calcaneal/Achilles tendon, automatically uses full-source framing. Camera
close-up is not cropping, hiding, remeshing, registration or anatomical repair.

`lib/regional-framing.ts` delegates hand behavior to the already verified hand
helper and adds a bilateral-capable foot preset. Other regions and landing-side
preferences are unchanged. Existing inspection, focus/isolation, removed-tissue
ghosting, source-origin, exam, non-spatial layouts and nonzero separation
disable the regional preset. The menu omits an unavailable regional fit when
there are no visible primary structures to frame. Source-bounds calculation
ignores invalid bounds rather than inventing a location.

The original full-source camera reference remains the basis of capture, restore,
pan and zoom. A restored saved pose takes precedence over the regional preset;
no saved-view format or private review approval is migrated. Reset restores
the regional preset when eligible; a retained proximal selection still requires
its full extent. Choosing Frame foot clears that selection explicitly.
