## Scope and source limits

Coverage: bilateral costal cartilages one to seven; septal, major alar and lateral nasal cartilages; thyroid, cricoid, paired arytenoid/corniculate/cuneiform cartilages; forearm/leg interosseous membranes; medial/lateral rectus check ligaments; median/lateral thyrohyoid, hyo-epiglottic, thyro-epiglottic, median cricothyroid and stylohyoid ligaments.

Exact source identities, laterality, categories and file memberships are independently pinned in `scripts/validate-connective-curriculum.mjs`: 38 ISA entries and five PART-OF nasal entries. The 43 representations use 44 source components because cricoid FMA9615 contains both FJ2440 and FJ2769. These official labels do not independently validate their union or physical boundaries. Hyo-epiglottic FMA55227 retains unspecified laterality; no midline value is invented.

The first and second sternocostal relationships are distinguished; adult fusion/variation is not ruled out. Forearm load sharing is position/contact dependent, not a fixed percentage. A tibiofibular membrane is not the entire ankle syndesmosis. Check ligaments are not certified complete pulleys or precise eye-excursion limits. Nasal source boundaries do not prove natural cleavage planes or valve function. Laryngeal cartilage/ligaments are not contracting muscles, a working vocal fold or a validated airway-access window. Existing source warnings and geometry remain unchanged.
