# Hand muscle teaching drafts

## Delivered scope

Ten definitions provide **40 draft Anatomy/Function sections for 20 existing bilateral muscle, head and group entries**. Thirty-four sections are newly drafted; six previous lumbrical/interosseous Function drafts are enriched. The existing notes panel, model and controls are unchanged.

Explicit IDs cover abductor/flexor/opponens digiti minimi, abductor pollicis brevis, opponens pollicis, the oblique/transverse adductor heads and the lumbrical/palmar-interosseous/dorsal-interosseous source groups. All names, sides, coverage notes and source component counts remain. Groups are not newly numbered muscles or separate tendon slips. Flexor pollicis brevis and other held/absent sources are not inferred from adjacent anatomy.

Notes describe typical attachments, actions and motor supply, not measured attachment footprints or validated nerve courses. They distinguish the first/fifth metacarpal attachments of opposition muscles from phalangeal attachments; retain separate adductor origins; explain the lumbricals' median/ulnar split; and use the middle-finger axis for interosseous actions. Palmar-interosseous thumb conventions vary and no individual count is assigned to the grouped mesh. All medical content remains draft; clinical, pathology, imaging and quiz topics are unchanged.

Current body totals: Anatomy 289 draft / 733 identity-only; Function 345 draft / 146 identity-only / 531 pending. Among the hand's 124 entries: Anatomy 62 draft / 62 identity-only; Function 62 draft / 8 generic / 54 pending. Every represented hand muscle has basic drafts, not a complete clinical curriculum or a complete hand model.

## Factual references and rights

Checked 7 September 2026; exact links also appear per lesson:

- [Thenar eminence](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK580533/): selected APB/opponens facts.
- [Hypothenar eminence](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK546622/): selected abductor/flexor facts, not its inconsistent opponens insertion statement.
- [Intrinsic hand muscles](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539810/): lumbrical group, motor-supply and conventional finger-interosseous context.
- [Interossei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK534772/): dorsal attachments/actions and differing thumb interosseous conventions.
- [Texas Tech upper-limb anatomy](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_upperlimb.html): adductor-head origins, fifth-metacarpal opponens attachment and selected cross-checks.

These teaching references contain some inconsistent details; citation does not certify every claim on their pages. In particular, the Texas Tech table supports the transverse adductor's metacarpal shaft origin and the fifth-metacarpal opponens insertion. Precise attachment extents, variants and represented source fidelity still require an anatomist's adjudication.

Only original short factual explanations are authored. No third-party chapter, table dataset, illustration, scan, translation or question bank is imported. StatPearls chapters retain CC BY-NC-ND restrictions and are **not commercial redistribution assets**; Texas Tech retains its copyright. Citations do not license copying. Existing original code/text MIT and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0/DBCLS notices remain separate. No dependency, mesh, font, texture, paid API or runtime reference-fetching service is added. This is a bounded reuse policy, not blanket legal clearance of external sources.
