## Teaching and limits

The two existing tabs distinguish tendon disruption, muscle weakness and nerve dysfunction. Topics include scapular winging, partial/full-thickness cuff tears, suprascapular localisation, subscapularis examination associations, axillary relationships, proximal/distal biceps injury and the shared triceps distal apparatus.

These are short overviews, not an exhaustive disease catalogue, treatment recommendations, self-examination instructions or diagnostic algorithms. A muscle head/portion is not a separately segmented tendon. No pathological deformation, nerve course, acquired scan, registration, layout or control is added.
