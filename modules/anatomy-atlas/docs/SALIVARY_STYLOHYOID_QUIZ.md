# Salivary-gland and stylohyoid formative questions

Six exact-source head/neck placements replace generated identification prompts
with three original four-choice questions: bilateral submandibular glands
(FMA59802/59803), sublingual glands (FMA59804/59805) and stylohyoid ligaments
(FMA72309/72311). Only Quiz changes; anatomy, clinical/imaging lessons, tours,
geometry, source labels, boundaries and validation states remain unchanged.

The small batch follows the owner's preference not to spend disproportionate
effort on oral anatomy. Dental prompts and the ambiguous superficial-perineal
group remain separate; no specificity is invented to remove a pending count.

## Evidence and rights

- [Texas Tech oral-cavity teaching](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/nervous_system/infratemp.html): describes the submandibular duct against the medial sublingual-gland surface, opening on the sublingual caruncle. These are typical anatomical relationships, not reconstructed ducts.
- [Texas Tech joints/ligaments table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/joints_alpha.html): stylohyoid ligament between the temporal styloid and lesser hyoid horn. It does not validate either model's attachment geometry.
