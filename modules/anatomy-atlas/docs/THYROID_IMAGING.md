# Inferior thyroid artery: CT/CTA and ultrasound drafts

Four draft placements cover the two existing inferior thyroid arterial selections:
FMA10697/right/FJ2209 and FMA10680/left/FJ2210. CT and Ultrasound are available in
the existing Imaging panel wherever those sources are supplied: head/neck,
shoulder/arm, thorax and whole body. No new panel, geometry or scan is added.

The CT note distinguishes patient-specific proximal origin-mapping from assuming
the reference model's parent. Ultrasound distinguishes acquired Doppler
measurements from surface colour. A single complication example distinguishes
US/CT observations from subsequent angiographic identification. No protocol,
normal threshold, disease frequency, treatment or operative target is provided.
MRI and X-ray remain pending; thyroid-gland MRI cannot simply be reassigned to
this artery. Earlier Anatomy, Function, Clinical, Pathology and Quiz are unchanged.

## Binding and review

The runtime helper accepts only complete pinned source identities, on the exact
two supported topics. Changed side, tree, bundle, source members or coordinates
fail closed. Fresh arrays prevent callers mutating shared notes. Immutable
before/after snapshots preserve all9,914 other topics, shoulder teaching and
dissection recipes; predecessor history removes only this recorded transition.
`thyroid-imaging:test` also exercises altered identities, mixed/unrecorded
history, full schema records, original bundle bytes and pending MRI/X-ray.

Owner radiologist sign-off must review each note/source/revision, the scope of
both cohorts and the single case, variant origins and source-boundary limitations.
Anatomical source labels do not prove a patent junction, complete thyroid supply,
recurrent nerve location or clinical truth. No acquired-image or registration
approval is implied; Atlas, Didanix Education imaging cases and paid lectures
retain independent entitlements. Real-case privacy/release clearance and device
acceptance remain separate. Coordinating checkpoint records actual browser QA.
