## Claim/reference mapping and reuse

Only original concise factual prose and outbound reading links are added.
No publication prose, figure, table, classification, scan, cadaver image, model
or dataset is redistributed. Access to a publication is not commercial-asset
permission. Existing BodyParts3D CC BY4.0 credit remains; original notes/code
retain project MIT terms. No new fee-bearing service, dependency, font or texture.

- [TTUHSC upper-limb arteries](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html): branch origins and predominant arch contributions; copyrighted table used for factual checking only, not copied or imported. These norms do not certify the source donor's connections.
- [Altinsoy et al.,2017 CTA study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29089685/), DOI10.4103/ijri.IJRI_365_16: radial/ulnar depiction, variants and calcification in patients assessed for coronary bypass conduits. Dense calcified segments were not fully evaluable in the illustrated study. No diagnostic rates, harvesting advice, scan parameters or publisher images reused.
- [Nakatsuka et al.,1991 forearm MRA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1945792/): phase-contrast depiction of radial/ulnar vessels in ten volunteers, with three dialysis-shunt patients also studied. This historical feasibility evidence is not a modern protocol, routine-MRI equivalence or universal accuracy claim. The English abstract was read; the full Japanese article was not translated or imported.
- [Trager et al.,1993 Doppler study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8349968/), DOI10.1016/0363-5023(93)90304-L: measured calibre/flow and inter-individual radial/ulnar dominance in20 volunteers. No measured values, examination manoeuvres or normal thresholds are assigned to the atlas donor.
- [Kodaira et al.,2026 anterior-interosseous CTA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41952244/), DOI10.1002/micr.70221: artery measurements at two forearm levels in22 patients with traumatic hand/finger defects, with a separate six-case reconstruction series. Only the imaging observation is summarized; no size thresholds, flap dimensions, operative advice or outcome prediction is carried into the atlas.
- [Forearm ultrasound after radial occlusion,2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40605325/): acquired radial/ulnar/anterior-interosseous measurements and distal flow direction in40 patients after coronary angiography/intervention. This altered-circulation population does not validate normal donor flow or prove complete source collaterals.

For PubMed pages that returned empty/challenge text, the indexed primary abstract
was used. No authentication/challenge bypass or secondary summary substitutes.
The references do not justify filling the other interosseous modality slots.
