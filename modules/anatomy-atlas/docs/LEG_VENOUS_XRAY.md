# Leg venous X-ray teaching draft

Four topic drafts cover the eight existing bilateral popliteal, small saphenous,
anterior tibial and posterior tibial selections in
`content/lower-venous-imaging-pins.json`. The atlas has source-labelled 3D
surfaces, not acquired contrast images. X-ray venography describes contrast-filled
channels; a plain radiograph or model surface cannot establish a venous lumen,
confluence, patency, reflux or flow. Projected overlap does not prove connection.
The right and left anterior tibial selections each retain two source parts; those
parts are not separately validated companion veins. Other source groups do not
validate complete companions, valves or exact junctions. Source laterality is not
an acquired image's patient side, and no patient-space registration is implied.

## Evidence and limits

- [ACR/RSNA venography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/venography) describes the contrast X-ray modality, not plain-film vein visibility.
- [Quinlan et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12821771/) reviewed 808 thrombus-free venograms from medical trial participants. Two vessels in the popliteal fossa were not synonymous with true popliteal duplication; frequencies are cohort observations, not population prevalence.
- [Cross et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10982994/) studied 52 cadaveric fossae and 63 venograms, finding variable tibial/fibular union and popliteal formation. It does not map these model junctions.
- [Sauerbrei et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7217162/) reported observer uncertainty, especially for anterior tibial veins, in 34 older lower-limb venograms. Its examination comparison is not a present-day technique recommendation.
- [Short-saphenous termination comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2679965/) examined 64 limbs of 46 selected patients, many with incompetence. [Corcos et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8916464/) used several imaging methods in separate nonrandom groups of 528 limbs. Their junction findings cannot establish a normal-population rate or a junction on these surfaces.
- [Schweighofer et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20022210/) dissected cadaveric small-saphenous junctions and valves; this is anatomy evidence, not current contrast demonstration. [UAMS lower-limb vein table](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/vein-tables/selected-veins-of-the-lower-limb/) provides general drainage orientation.
