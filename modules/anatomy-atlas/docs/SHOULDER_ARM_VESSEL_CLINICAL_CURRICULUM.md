The thoracoacromial CTA paper initially examined 110 sides from 55 patients but retained only 49 trunks after exclusions. No initial-sample or variant percentage is imported as population prevalence. The perforator study identifies several sources of chest skin supply; pectoral muscle perfusion is not synonymous with all overlying skin perfusion. The notch study describes multiple vessel/nerve arrangements; anatomical crowding does not itself diagnose entrapment.

Some PMC opens returned browser-check pages and some PubMed opens were empty; accessible original-source indexed abstracts and available official information were used only for the bounded claims above, without bypassing access controls. The correct Cleveland Clinic TOS URL ends in -tos. No inaccessible article was claimed to have been fully reviewed.
