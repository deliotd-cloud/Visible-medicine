# Lower-leg Clinical / Pathology drafts

8 September 2026: 56 original short drafts across 28 existing bilateral muscle/head entries. Thirteen definitions cover the long extensors, fibularis muscles, long flexors, plantaris, popliteus, soleus, tibialis muscles and both gastrocnemius heads.

## Scope and limitations

Teaching distinguishes tendon injury from neurological weakness; tertius from the lateral-compartment fibularis muscles; peroneal tendon disease from sprain; FHL symptoms in dancers and nondancers; tennis leg from assumed plantaris rupture; calf muscle injury from thrombosis; acute compartment syndrome from routine exertional pain; posterior tibial dysfunction from multi-structure arch collapse; and gastrocnemius strain from shared Achilles injury.

No abnormal mesh, individual digital slip or Achilles subtendon, os-peroneum variant, nerve territory, pressure measurement, patient scan, management protocol or clinical approval is supplied. Explode is not a decompression or diagnostic examination.
