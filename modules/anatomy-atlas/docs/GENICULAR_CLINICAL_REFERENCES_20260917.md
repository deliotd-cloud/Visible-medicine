## Limits and exclusions

NC/ND papers, including the2016 middle-genicular cadaver study and several
pseudoaneurysm reports, are not admitted as adapted text or assets. StatPearls
figures, OpenStax mixed-notice candidate and held Clarke reference are excluded.
Worker also suggested a popliteal-trauma paper; it was not needed/admitted here.

Each middle source retains two disconnected components, not two newly named
arteries or invented bridging geometry. All source faces/coordinates and arterial
connection guides are unchanged. No measured lumen, flow, collateral sufficiency,
injury, viable tissue, angiographic blush or embolisation target is supplied.
CT/MRI/X-ray/US remain pending, with imaging-only fallback wording clarified.
Clinical validation must assess all five family distinctions, side/source mapping
and educational suitability at the exact reviewed revision. Atlas/case/paid
lecture access and privacy gates remain independent.
