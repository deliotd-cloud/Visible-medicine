# Major limb bone quick checks

Ten original four-choice question groups replace generated identification text
for 17 retained selections: bilateral hip bone, femur, fibula, patella, tibia,
radius and ulna, plus left clavicle, humerus and scapula. Existing dedicated
shoulder/right-side questions are preserved. The questions cover normal
landmarks, articulations and orientation, not fracture diagnosis or image-based
exams. Bilateral selections share the appropriate question; 17 placements are
not 17 unique questions.

Factual references are the Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center El Paso
[upper-limb bone table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_upperlimb.html)
and [lower-limb bone table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_lowerlimb.html).
Question, choice and explanation text total 116 and 121 words respectively.
These are factual reading links, not licences to redistribute the tables or
media. Original wording uses project MIT terms; no table, publisher question,
image, scan, mesh, font, dependency, service or fee is added. Existing mesh
licences and attribution remain unchanged.

All lessons remain drafts pending revision-bound radiologist review. Correct
answers about usual anatomy do not validate these static source surfaces,
landmarks or spatial relationships. No patient-space registration, diagnosis,
clinical approval or paid-resource access is inferred.
