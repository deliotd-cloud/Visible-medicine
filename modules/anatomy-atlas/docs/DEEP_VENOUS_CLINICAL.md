# Source-bound deep lower-limb venous teaching

This increment replaces only the pending Clinical and Pathology payloads for
six exact admitted selections: bilateral anterior tibial veins, bilateral
posterior tibial veins, and bilateral deep femoral (profunda femoris) veins.
Anterior calf, posterior calf, and deep-thigh teaching remain distinct. Every
match requires the complete catalog identity, not an FMA identifier alone.

The calf lessons introduce why deep venous context and same-side comparison
matter without turning a static source surface into an ultrasound examination.
The thigh lessons distinguish the profunda femoris vein from the femoral vein.
They are short educational drafts, not a diagnostic pathway, treatment or
anticoagulation algorithm, prescribed scanning protocol, or claim of disease.

The source geometry and bundle, existing Anatomy/Function and imaging lessons,
study recipes, guided navigation, review state, and the other 9,906 topic
payloads remain unchanged. Companion channels may exist; the anterior tibial
selections contain two recorded source parts. Fibular veins, exact collecting
junctions, valves and lumen are not supplied. No flow, compressibility, clot,
reflux, Doppler response, or scan registration can be inferred from the static
surfaces or an exploded view.

The factual text is original and accompanied by the project’s visible reference
and credit fields. No article figure, table, patient scan, identifier, logo, or
uncleared derivative is imported. Article- or publisher-specific reuse evidence
does not extend to third-party media or establish project-wide clearance.

`npm run deep-venous-clinical:test` checks the immutable baseline and transition,
all 12 replacements and 12 server-rendered notes, all 1,102 schema records,
identity/source/topic rejection, unchanged bundle bytes and recipes, and strict
historical before/after/mixed/unrelated-drift states. Its machine-readable report
is `docs/deep-venous-clinical-validation.json`.

Clinical approval remains false. Revision-bound radiologist review, physical-
device acceptance, authenticated model staging, and independent Atlas, case,
and paid-lecture access remain separate release gates.
