# Girdle and major limb-bone teaching

8 September 2026: 34 original Anatomy/Function drafts for 17 existing bone entries through 10 definitions. No geometry, interface controls or dependencies added. The three right-shoulder bone entries already have pilot teaching and are preserved, not overwritten by mirrored notes.

## Scope and source identity

New teaching covers left clavicle/scapula/humerus, both radii/ulnae, both hip bones/femora/tibiae/fibulae/patellae. Exact FMA, laterality, category, source component and regional membership were independently checked against the cached official BodyParts3D v4 ISA index. Each of these entries has one source component. Shared regional membership is not duplicate anatomy: humerus belongs to shoulder-arm and forearm, hip bone to pelvis and thigh, and femur to thigh, pelvis and leg.

Dispatch checks each exact identity, side, bone category, system, primary region and every required secondary region. It preserves coverage warnings and returns detached arrays. A hip bone remains one adult surface, not independently dissectible ilium/ischium/pubis. Bone labels do not certify marrow, cartilage, growth plates, foraminal clearance, tendon continuity, ligament integrity or attachment footprints.

Teaching distinguishes scapulothoracic gliding from a synovial joint, elbow from radioulnar motion, radius from ulna at the wrist, tibia from fibula in load sharing, and patellofemoral from tibiofemoral articulation. Explode translations are not joint rotation, patellar tracking or operative separation. No quantitative load fractions, maturity assumptions or patient measurements are inferred.

Reference pages were not treated as error-free. Unrelated vascular, embryological, fixed-age, attachment and procedural assertions were not adopted. In particular, the radius has a radial-head articular surface, not an imported claim of a disc inside the radial head; the proximal ulnar radial notch is not the distal radial ulnar notch. Bone silhouettes do not establish physiological movement or reliable neurovascular clearance.

Only brief original factual teaching was written. No publisher prose, illustrations, images, tables, scans or datasets were imported. NC-ND/copyright reference terms are not commercial asset grants. Original code/text retain MIT terms; separate DBCLS BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 attribution and modification obligations remain. No new paid API, font, texture, mesh, dependency or private data. Citations are not endorsement or clinical approval.
