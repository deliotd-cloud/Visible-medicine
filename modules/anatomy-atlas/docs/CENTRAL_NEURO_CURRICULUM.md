## Evidence that changes the teaching

The [2022 human forniceal-commissure study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9314404/) found much subtler fibres than some earlier descriptions and cautioned about distinguishing them from neighbouring callosal fibres. The draft does not assign a proven hippocampus-to-hippocampus route, memory function or patient tractography result. Its Function section remains pending specialist adjudication. Research uncertainty is not resolved by the source label or a green software test.

The posterior commissure contributes to distributed eye-movement and pupillary circuitry. Its surface is not the neighbouring nucleus or every constituent connection. References: [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441892/) and [Stanford neuro-ophthalmology teaching](https://neuro-ophthalmology.stanford.edu/2020/02/neuro-ophthalmology-illustrated-chapter-13-diplopia-11-vertical-eye-movements/). No diagnostic or treatment rule is added.

The [adult human spinal ependymal-region study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4614133/) cautions against assuming an open central canal throughout an adult cord or directly transferring rodent regenerative findings to humans. Notes distinguish a lumen/space from spinal cord tissue and qualify CSF content by patency. No continuous fluid conduit, nerve tract, syrinx diagnosis or complete cord is implied.
