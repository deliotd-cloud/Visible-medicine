# Heart, lungs and main bronchi — applied anatomy

Four original draft questions cover five exact existing root selections in
Whole body and Thorax through the existing **Apply anatomy** mode. The heart
question distinguishes pulmonary venous return/systemic pumping from gas
exchange; the lung question distinguishes gas-exchange tissue from conducting
airways. Separate main-bronchus questions relate each proximal airway to its
ipsilateral lung, without treating a source gap as obstruction or patency.

The usual right/left lobar count is taught, not inferred from these surfaces.
The lung roots consist of supplied bronchovascular source groups: they are not
complete lung parenchyma, fissures, alveoli or ventilation/perfusion models.
The heart root keeps its56 admitted sources, not all83 components in the official
aggregate; all27 previously excluded components remain excluded.
No diagnosis, procedure, acquired scan or patient-specific registration is added.

The engine remains unchanged: only loaded, eligible same-side authored choices
are shown. Heart has four unpaired choices; paired lung/main-bronchus questions
have two choices per hemithorax. Contralateral structures never become answers
or distractors. Removing a target or its last eligible alternative prevents
that question. Correct answers score correctly; answer-once, missed-question
retry and feedback-after-response semantics are retained.

## Exact scope and review

identities, ordered source file/hash pairs, bundles and coordinate provenance
from baseline `24d023f471d39d7d1e4660fb4f20264528bdd6fe`. The right main bronchus
uses PART-OF/FJ2539; the left uses ISA/FJ2450. Neither FMA nor a plausible label
alone admits replacement geometry. New records reject changed IDs, names,
side, source hash/order, region, tree, bundle, node and bounds.

All previous 166 reasoning concepts remain ordered and unchanged. The total is
170 concepts over 313 root representations. No geometry, source catalogue,
topic prose, extra panel, dependency, font or licensed image is added.
Five review teaching fingerprints advance because the actual interactive
question becomes part of their existing worksheet. Source and all nine topic
slots stay exact; the other 1,099 root teaching materials remain exact. These
drafts require revision-bound radiologist/educator review. No private decision
is created, read or carried forward; geometry and imaging approval are separate.
