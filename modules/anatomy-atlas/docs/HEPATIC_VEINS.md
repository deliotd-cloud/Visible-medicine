# Hepatic venous source addition

11 September 2026. Three source-bound selections are available in Abdomen and Whole body. Search “hepatic vein”, select a surface and expand the existing **Venous drainage** panel. **Show available veins & bones** reveals immediate drainage neighbours with bone context; hide, Undo, extract and reset use the existing controls. No additional permanent toolbar is introduced.

The singular “tributary” labels come from the official source; these multipart groups are not 12 newly named individual veins. All 13 components remain in their original positions and move together within their selection. No gaps are bridged, surfaces mirrored, branch identities invented or territories inferred.

The source audit pins ten original SHA-256 hashes, verifies the complete ISA definitions and checks known holds. It screens the bounds of 1,036 earlier root selections, then compares whole groups and individual components with nearby existing sources, including the compound liver with its nested branch surfaces. There are 378 candidate/reference and 81 internal comparisons. No exact shared faces or qualifying translated duplicate were found. All components pass the implemented closed/oriented combinatorial checks. Some source surfaces approach liver/branch surfaces closely; this is not proof of correct junctions, collision clearance or self-intersection freedom.

Original OBJ bytes are retained in content/sources/hepatic-veins. The 256,984-byte GLB has SHA-256 **1f8922b93ec4c92bf6d695de9b75a00fb6f1552522125ca25861b1ed04f403cf**. The established transform, exact-coordinate indexing, recomputed normals and Float32 storage retain all ordered faces and winding. No earlier source mesh, raw catalogue or recipe is replaced. Display: 1,039 selections; archival ingestion: 1,022.

## Functionality and teaching

The existing systemic map grows to 47 source selections, 26 groups and 49 directed teaching relationships. Middle hepatic outflow points towards the inferior cava; right/left tributary groups point only towards their respective hepatic vein. Portal inflow remains a distinct map without a direct edge into hepatic outflow. No lumen, common trunk, sinusoid, valve, Doppler signal or measured flow is generated.

Full-record and bundle admission checks prevent partial, modified, duplicate or mismatched sources from acquiring these bindings. Existing study links, remove/Undo, side filters, source-position labels, separation and imaging-ready reference coordinates apply. No patient frame ID, clinical approval or lecture entitlement is inherited.

Six original Anatomy/Function drafts explain the new groups and their limits. CT/MRI/X-ray/US content for these additions remains pending. The separate seven-group liver-branches dissection retains its existing source identities, including FMA15800 (anterior inferior tributary of middle hepatic vein); it is not merged or relabelled as these root selections.

## Reproduction and review

Run npm run hepatic-veins:test and npm run systemic-venous:test. The addition test loads the generated GLB, checks 14 side-aware structure links, exact records/anchors and 111 changed-source rejection cases. Systemic tests use an independent 49-edge oracle, reciprocal/side checks, visibility history, 296 view plans, 47 real component SSR renders and 94 actual parent-handler cases. Source comparison reproduction: node scripts/audit-hepatic-veins.mjs --check. Offline tests do not establish browser/device/GPU acceptance.

Clinical review must verify vessel naming/course/calibre, tributary identities and separation, liver relationships, confluences and teaching wording. Couinaud territories remain withheld; pre-existing tissue/segment source conflicts are unchanged. Acquired imaging requires separately licensed, de-identified data, modality review, validated correspondence and independent access control.
