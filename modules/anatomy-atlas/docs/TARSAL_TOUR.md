# Right tarsal bone orientation tour

Draft source-bound teaching sequence for the seven original right-foot tarsal surfaces in `foot-skeleton`. The selections and camera frames use the display catalog identities exactly; no geometry, source coordinates or patient data are added. Every stop frames the same assembled seven bones. The existing guided-tour player supplies the quintic orbit transition and fades other surfaces.

The anatomical order and neighbour descriptions follow the [TTUHSC El Paso lower-limb bone table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/bones_lowerlimb.html), rows for the talus through cuboid. The captions are original short orientation copy, not a reproduced table. The table is descriptive anatomy; it does not validate this model's joint spacing or anatomical accuracy.

Tibia, fibula and metatarsals are omitted from this focused frame. Cartilage, ligaments and tendons are absent. The tour does not show weight bearing, measured joint spaces, acquired projection or CT/MRI registration. It remains a draft pending revision-bound radiologist sign-off; it is not clinical approval or a published teaching asset.

The focused test checks all seven catalog IDs and FMA identities, bundle bytes and hash against the `d35fab9730ca5420d7a65f0ba321d8cbfff03571` source baseline, unchanged existing tours and per-structure evidence, stable frames, source mismatch rejection and review-packet tampering rejection. Registry and package integration are coordinated in the main task.
