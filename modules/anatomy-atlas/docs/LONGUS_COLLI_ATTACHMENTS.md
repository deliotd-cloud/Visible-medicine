# Longus colli: part-specific attachment navigation

17 September 2026. The existing Dissect → Muscle attachment relationships panel
now supports the three admitted left longus colli parts. Select a part in Head &
neck, Spine or Whole body, inspect its grouped origin/insertion bones and choose
Show. Bone buttons open their existing information. No extra permanent panel.

Superior oblique, vertical intermediate and inferior oblique parts retain their
separate source identities. Typical partners are C3–C5 / C1, C5–T3 / C2–C4 and
T1–T3 / C5–C6 respectively. No occipital or longus-capitis substitution is made.
Head & neck lacks thoracic bones: those entries are readable but not clickable;
the existing source-bound whole-body link offers the complete available set.
Spine and Whole body contain every mapped bone. No right counterpart is added.

Show retains only the chosen part and its available bony partners; it preserves
whole source surfaces. The existing side filter remains authoritative. One
dissection action supports Undo/Redo; camera/system switches are not history
snapshots. Separation/cutaway/inspection reset through the existing parent action.
These anterior muscles open from the front, avoiding the Spine default rear
view obscuring them behind vertebrae. Other attachment studies keep their view.
The panel is collapsed by default and absent in exam mode.

## Source and anatomical limits

The extension uses the existing longus-colli admission contract, checking every
complete muscle and context record, bundle, coordinate frame, licence and source
version. Missing, duplicate or changed entries fail closed. No new pin catalogue,
mesh, mirrored geometry or source modifications are introduced. The 18 paired
neck relationships and their original 31-record pins remain unchanged.

These are typical whole-bone relationships, not level-specific donor footprints,
segmented entheses, measured slips, simulated motion or a safe surgical plane.
The original muscle-part surfaces remain clinically unvalidated. Three source
parts do not prove that they are separately identifiable on acquired imaging.
Existing clinical/imaging drafts and all other teaching are unchanged. User
radiologist sign-off must remain bound to the actual revised display/source.

supports the part-specific typical attachment levels, consistent with the atlas's
existing longus-colli anatomy draft. Consulted 17 September 2026. Only concise
original factual labels and a reading link are included; no table, diagram, scan,
publisher text or media is redistributed. No new dependency, paid service, asset
licence or font. Existing BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 attribution remains required.

## Verification

`node scripts/validate-neck-attachments.mjs` covers all 21 supported muscle
selections / 12 relationships, all applicable regions and sides, exact bone sets,
source mutations, independent whole-body links, actual panel/parent handlers,
exam denial and reversible dissection. Longus-colli source failures are tested
separately from the original paired-neck source contract.

The coordinating checkpoint records actual browser, build and recovery evidence.
These tests do not certify clinical accuracy, device acceptance, patient
registration or publication. No private scans, CT-head masks, clinical PACS or
independent Atlas/case/lecture entitlements change.
