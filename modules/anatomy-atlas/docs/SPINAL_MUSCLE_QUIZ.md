# Spinal muscle Quiz drafts

Six original formative questions replace generated identification for bilateral
psoas major and serratus posterior superior/inferior. The source baseline is
`027f29d54045256c7b4d359eaed45a3dee2c5b25`. Complete retained display identities,
previous lessons, catalog/coordinate identity and verified bundle digests are
recorded in `content/spinal-muscle-quiz-pins.json`. Matching a name or FMA identifier
alone is insufficient: altered source records are refused.

The questions concern usual anatomical relationships, attachments and nerve
supply, not a claim that the static surfaces show every attachment or nerve.
They do not establish respiratory function, complete muscle boundaries, fibre
paths, patient anatomy or clinical suitability. University references are factual
reading links; no publisher table, diagram, question or dataset is imported.
Exact-source radiologist review remains required, independently of model review.

Factual reading: Texas Tech University HSC [kidney/retroperitoneum tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/gastrointestinal_system/kidney_tables.html)
and [back tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/back_tables.html).
Original questions, choices and explanations total 55 derived words for the
kidney page and 118 for the back page. No superior-serratus fixed rib/root-level
question is used because the reviewed table's levels need corroboration.

Use `npm run spinal-muscle-quiz:test` after shared dispatch and Review generation.
The focused check compares all topic placements against the pinned parent,
checks altered-source refusal and current/stale Review material, and exercises
correct/wrong feedback, retry/reset, focus and exam concealment with the actual
question component. A source-only mode supports bounded worker validation before
integration; it does not prove final dispatch or website delivery.

The separate website must receive matching generated learner and protected
Clinical Review outputs together. Never edit generated website modules by hand.
No masks, scans, source meshes, approvals, entitlements, dependencies or paid
services are changed by this batch.
