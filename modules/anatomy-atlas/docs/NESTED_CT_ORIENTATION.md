# Named nested CT orientation drafts

Five original short orientation exercises add nine CT placements to admitted
source selections: inferior-collicular brachia (two), anterior superior temporal
gyrus (two), posterior superior temporal gyrus (two), optic chiasm (one), and
optic tracts (two). Existing anatomy, function, clinical, pathology, MRI, quizzes,
models, source identities and teaching bindings remain unchanged.

These are teaching drafts, not CT segmentation, a scan viewer, a diagnostic
protocol, patient registration or evidence that small tracts/functional regions
are fully delineated by routine CT. The auditory and visual research references
study MRI; the new CT orientation exercises are our educational interpretation,
not results tested by those articles. They explicitly distinguish source surfaces
from patient findings. Source-defined STG parts are not validated auditory or
language territories. The 29 unnamed cranial partitions and two deep-femoral
remainders retain pending imaging; no parent text is borrowed to clear them.

## Review and checks

Radiologist review must validate each note's anatomy, laterality, source limits,
CT visibility and appropriateness of suggested MRI correlation on the exact
teaching revision. Actual anonymised paired CT/MRI examples and a validated
patient-to-model registration are separate future work. No clinical approval
or acquired-image approval is created by this change.

`npm run nested-ct-orientation:test` compares the live corpus to saved
`31a6ae7d`, checks all108 review contexts (nine changed/99 unchanged), rejects
27 stale/foreign teaching requests before storage and renders all nine exact CT
lessons. The regenerated renderer fingerprint also revises all108 geometry
review revisions, despite unchanged mesh bytes; 108 stale geometry submissions
are rejected before storage. No old review is silently migrated.
The history helper verifies hardcoded hashes before projecting out only the five
new CT fields/four references; altered content cannot be silently erased.
Legacy teaching remains checked live; the eye milestone validator replays its
immutable saved content, while the new validator proves its preservation in the
live corpus. Full build/export and physical-input checks are recorded separately.
