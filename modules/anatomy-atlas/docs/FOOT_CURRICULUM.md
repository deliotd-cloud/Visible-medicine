## Scope

Eighteen definitions supply 72 new Anatomy/Function drafts for 36 admitted bilateral muscle/head/slip entries through the existing panel. Exact FMA identities, names, component counts and warnings are retained. No source mesh, UI control, dependency, scan, nerve course or clinical approval is added.

Flexor accessorius remains the source name for quadratus plantae. Short toe tendons are not confused with their long counterparts.
