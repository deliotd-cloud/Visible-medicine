# Renal and urethral reference revision — 17 September 2026

This is a scoped provenance and teaching revision, not project-wide commercial
or clinical clearance. It replaces the EAU-dependent reference pathways in
`hra-renal-clinical.ts`, two ureteric-artery nested notes in `renal-teaching.ts`,
and the four root urethral imaging notes in `pelvic-organ-imaging.ts`.
No images, tables, staging scales, guideline algorithms, patient scans or
publisher wording are imported. Source identity, geometry, access and draft
review status are unchanged. Historical citations remain in immutable offline
baseline records as evidence of what changed, not current teaching sources.

## Independently checked sources

1. Bonatti M, Lombardo F, Vezzali N, et al. *MDCT of blunt renal trauma:
   imaging findings and therapeutic implications.* Insights Imaging 6,
   261–272 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1007/s13244-015-0385-1
   Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13244-015-0385-1
   Supports the capsule/parenchyma versus perirenal-fat compartments and
   CT assessment of parenchymal, vascular and collecting-system injury.
   Its own rights notice permits any use with author/source credit under
   Creative Commons Attribution, but supplies **no version or licence URL**.
   PMC rights metadata independently has the same limitation. Do not label
   it CC BY 4.0. The publisher rights section is the permission evidence.
   No treatment recommendations or older injury grading are reproduced.
2. Coccolini F, Moore EE, Kluger Y, et al. *Kidney and uro-trauma: WSES-AAST
   guidelines.* World J Emerg Surg 14, 54 (2019).
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13017-019-0274-x
   Article-level [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
   Supports CT phase distinctions, FAST limitations, selected MRI use and
   delayed urographic evaluation. MRI teaching specifies problem-solving/
   follow-up rather than an unsourced universal claim about practicality.
3. Withey SJ, Otero-Garcia M, Aymerich M, et al. *Multimodality staging of
   renal cell carcinoma—pearls & pitfalls.* Abdom Radiol (2026).
   https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00261-026-05660-5
   Article-level CC BY 4.0. Independently supports vascular versus collecting-
   system extension and MRI clarification of venous thrombus extent when CT
   is uncertain. We do not reproduce its third-party staging tables or
   quoted society recommendations, scan protocols or images.
4. de'Angelis N, Schena CA, Marchegiani F, et al. *2023 WSES guidelines for
   the prevention, detection, and management of iatrogenic urinary tract
   injuries during emergency digestive surgery.* World J Emerg Surg 18,
   45 (2023). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13017-023-00513-8
   Article-level CC BY 4.0. Supports preservation of ureteric vascular supply
   and nephrographic/excretory CT evaluation after surgical injury. The
   relationship between disrupted supply and ischaemia is explanatory
   physiological context, not a claim that this partial arterial mesh
   establishes viability. No operative steps or acquisition recipe included.
5. Frankiewicz M, Vetterlein MW, Markiet K, et al. *Ultrasound imaging of
   male urethral stricture disease: a narrative review of the available
   evidence, focusing on selected prospective studies.* World J Urol 42,
   32 (2024). https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00345-023-04760-x
   Article-level CC BY 4.0. Supports specialised sonourethrography, surrounding
   tissue assessment, posterior limitations, contrast urethrography and
   supplementary MRI. Studies are small and technique/operator dependent;
   no comparative-performance guarantee or treatment advice is made here.

These are original short, adapted summaries with source attribution and
licence links in the existing teaching presentation. No author/agency endorses
Visible Medicine. Repeated display of the same short source-bound note does
not represent independently authored clinical evidence for every mesh.

## Audit corrections and exclusions

EAU reading links alone do not establish infringement. Nonetheless their
commercial/derived-use restrictions do not satisfy this product's preferred
source policy, so replacement content is grounded independently in the sources
above. No noncommercial/no-derivatives article is used as the replacement.

Khan et al. (SAJR 2004, DOI 10.4102/sajr.v8i4.101) was initially proposed from
its journal footer. Main review found an article-level copyright-status warning
despite that footer; it is **not cleared and not used**. A generic open-access
label is insufficient. A proposed paediatric transplant case was also not used
to support general ureteric injury teaching. NCI PDQ's third-party AJCC tables
were not imported. Unavailable PMC pages were not accessed around CAPTCHA;
accessible publisher pages supplied the evidence instead.

## Verification and review

New baseline/transition records must prove exactly which body, independent HRA
specimen and nested teaching projections changed. Prior accepted hashes are
retained, with explicit offline historical reconstruction rather than broad
rebaselining. Live rendering, source binding, citation presence and limits are
checked separately. Dated checkpoint records the actual checks and backups.
No test, licence or model-source audit grants clinical sign-off. The user
radiologist must review the revised content and its actual renderer revision.
