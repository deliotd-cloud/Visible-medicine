# Regional muscle quiz coverage

Six original four-option questions cover twelve exact bilateral selections:
longus capitis, rectus capitis anterior, rectus capitis lateralis, coccygeus,
and the oblique and transverse heads of adductor hallucis. These replace
identification-only Quiz notes; all other teaching is retained. Bilateral pairs
share general anatomy wording but have separate immutable source bindings.
Drafts require revision-bound radiologist review, not inferred clinical approval.

## Factual references checked 10 October 2026

- [Texas Tech head/neck muscle table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_head_neck.html): longus-capitis cranial attachment and the two rectus-capitis actions.
- [Texas Tech pelvic wall table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/reproductive_system/pelvicwall_tables.html): coccygeus and levator ani contribution to the pelvic diaphragm.
- [Texas Tech lower-limb muscle table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_lowerlimb.html): adductor-hallucis anatomy, function and innervation.
- [Elsevier transverse-head anatomy reference](https://www.elsevier.com/resources/anatomy/muscular-system/muscles-of-lower-limb/transverse-head-of-adductor-hallucis/22943): capsular/ligamentous origin, without implying direct attachment to metatarsal heads.

All question wording is original. No publisher figures, tables, prose, exam
items, meshes or datasets were imported. Citations establish factual support,
not permission to redistribute reference material. The test conservatively
counts all stem/choice/explanation words against every cited page, capped at
180 attributed words per page for this batch. No paid service or dependency.
