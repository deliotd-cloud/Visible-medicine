# Deep back and suboccipital quick checks

This batch fills generated-identification Quiz entries for retained deep-back
and suboccipital muscle selections. It excludes the psoas, serratus posterior,
trapezius and anterior/lateral prevertebral groups and preserves prior drafts.
Repeated bilateral and segmental questions are shared groups, not unique exams
for every representation. All remain exact-revision radiologist-review drafts.

validate depicted attachment sites, fascicles, innervation territories, surgical
planes or complete muscle boundaries in static meshes. No pathology, patient
findings or CT/MRI spatial registration is inferred.

The coordinator checks the actual cited university sources rather than assuming
that all named parts share a general muscle-table summary. Longissimus capitis
must not inherit a vertebra-to-vertebra attachment question. Iliocostalis group
questions must distinguish the column's collective attachments from each part.
No blanket intertransversarii innervation claim is made. Lumbar medial/lateral
variants share a conservative lateral-bending function question, rather than
an oversimplified transverse-process attachment question.

Original project wording cites factual references; no publisher question bank,
source table, prose, illustration or dataset is imported. Mirrored university
tables count together for the derivation limit. Current OpenStax non-commercial
