## Evidence and limits

- [TTUHSC original lower-limb arterial teaching table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_lowerlimb.html):
  parent/branch orientation for medial and lateral plantar selections. The
  superficial branch name comes from the existing catalogue, not a claim that
  the reference establishes its visibility on CT.
- [Gabrielli et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11694970/): cadaveric
  evidence for arch constitution and variability; not patient CTA performance.
- [Papon et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9787393/): deep plantar
  course and non-universal presence in its small dissection series. This is not
  a population prevalence estimate or clearance for a bypass procedure.
- [Field and Sun, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21611062/): single-case
  reference, not diagnostic-accuracy evidence. Main read the PubMed
  abstract/captions; the PMC full-text request returned a browser
  challenge and was not used as evidence of a successful full-text review.

All references were read on 26 September 2026. Original short factual summaries
and links only: no paper, abstract, figure, scan or extracted dataset is bundled.
Public availability is not a media-reuse licence; this change imports no asset
or dependency and adds no fees or new asset licence obligations.

Source-labelled surfaces are not a complete circulation, perfused lumen,
verified anastomosis or scan registration. The superficial branch note explicitly
withholds a routine visibility claim. No patency, stenosis, perfusion, surgical
route or intervention suitability is established. Exploded gaps are artificial.
