## Evidence and commercial-use boundary

- Kye et al., *Psoas sign: a reevaluation*, J Korean Radiol Soc (1987), original
  study comparing plain radiographs and CT in72 patients:
  https://jksronline.org/DOIx.php?id=10.3348/jkrs.1987.23.3.462
  Supports projection/fat/overlap limitations and the unreliability of the sign
  as a stand-alone disease indicator. No population-wide detection rate claimed.
- Moskowitz, *The psoas sign, hepatic angle, normal patients, and everyday
  practice*, Gut (1973), primary normal-patient study:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4706913/
  Supports normal nonvisualisation and the need for clinical context. Historical
  cohort findings are not presented as universal current sensitivity/specificity.
