# Hepatobiliary quick checks

Five original source-bound formative questions replace generic identification
copy for the liver, gallbladder, hepatic portal vein, cystic duct and common
hepatic duct. They cover dual hepatic blood inflow, bile production versus
storage, portal inflow versus hepatic venous outflow, and the two different
biliary duct unions. Each has four distinct choices, one answer and an
explanation. They use the existing Quiz notes interaction; they do not add a new
mode or claim a certified examination.

## Exact source and review scope

`content/hepatobiliary-quiz-pins.json` records complete retained identities,
bundle bytes, coordinate frame, previous quiz content and baseline
`e3d71fe4f3ce60169029fa29d0561d81335bad53`. The resolver compares the entire
canonical identity, not a name, FMA match, guessed side or nearest geometry.
No source mesh, pose, dissection, tour, imaging lesson or original anatomical
catalogue record is modified. The gallbladder retains its existing legacy
`pelvis` ID/bundle assignment; that identifier is not a new anatomical-location
claim and this batch does not silently rename or reposition it.

Only five of 9,936 topic placements change. The new questions enter the same
source-bound Clinical Review material; earlier or altered question packets are
refused, not migrated or accepted as an approval. The existing quiz component
hides answers until checking, supports correct/incorrect feedback and retry,
and remains concealed in exam mode. Normal anatomy, source-surface continuity,
acquired imaging findings and patient-space registration are separate concepts.
No surface validates flow, perfusion, duct patency or an operative plane.
