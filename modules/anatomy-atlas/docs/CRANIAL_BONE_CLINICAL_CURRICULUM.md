# Skull, facial-bone and hyoid clinical teaching

9 September 2026: 46 short original Clinical/Pathology drafts for 23 existing selections (22 skull/facial bones and the hyoid), backed by 24 source components and fifteen lesson groups. These are introductory clinical distinctions, not comprehensive disease coverage, validated fractures or clinical clearance. Existing tabs, compact controls and geometry are unchanged.

## Structure-specific teaching

- Frontal: visible contour, posterior sinus wall and outflow injury are different questions.
- Ethmoid: cribriform/olfactory injury and suspected cerebrospinal-fluid leakage; clear discharge alone is not confirmation.
- Parietal: cranial-vault injury versus underlying brain injury; a reference skull cannot clear head trauma.
- Temporal: hearing, balance and facial movement; conductive and sensorineural injury are distinct.
- Occipital: skull–C1 relationship and bony versus ligamentous injury; normal alignment is not a stability test.
- Sphenoid: optic canal versus superior orbital fissure, without assigning a nerve lesion from a bone selection.
- Maxilla: orbital-floor bone and orbital soft-tissue findings are separate; a fracture does not prove muscle entrapment.
- Zygomatic: cheek and orbit considered together; one bone is not the whole arch or fracture complex.
- Mandible: bite, sensation and potentially multiple fracture sites; one mandible has two sides and two joint relationships.
- Nasal: external bridge versus internal septal injury; a straight-looking nose does not exclude a septal haematoma.
- Vomer: one bony septal contribution, not the complete septum or cartilaginous partition.
- Inferior concha: bony support versus mucosal swelling and normal nasal-cycle variation; no airflow is measured.
- Lacrimal: bone versus gland and drainage pathway; a watery eye is not automatically a bone disorder.
- Palatine: hard versus soft palate and submucous cleft; an intact-looking bony roof is not a speech-function test.
- Hyoid: grouped two-component neck bone, not skull proper; swallowing/airway context without inferring an injury mechanism or dividing source parts into unvalidated body/horn anatomy.

## Evidence and reuse boundaries

The exact links are attached to each runtime lesson. Relevant direct text, indexed excerpts and abstracts were inspected on 9 September 2026; full-text systematic review of all references is not claimed.

- [NICE NG232](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG232/chapter/recommendations): limited head-trauma warning and imaging limitations, not a copied triage checklist or scan-timing algorithm.
- AO Surgery Reference: frontal sinus, orbital floor, skull-base and occipitocervical pages support anatomical injury distinctions. No plates, scans, drawings, tables, classification posters, surgical techniques or algorithms are shipped. Older advice about steroids, leak provocation/testing and operative management was deliberately not adopted.
- NHS trust and Right Decisions ENT/maxillofacial information: nasal/septal, orbital, mandibular, tear-drainage and palate distinctions. Local referral pathways, medication doses, operative details, outcome percentages and procedural instructions are not reproduced.
- [Temporal-bone review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3052678/): complication categories only; no historical treatment or prognosis recommendations.
- Hyoid [systematic review abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22691692/) and [blunt-trauma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26564301/) / [bicycle-trauma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33962266/) case reports: limited injury/symptom context. Small studies do not establish population risks, treatment success or a forensic conclusion.

An AAO-HNS clinical-indicator page surfaced during research with explicit non-commercial/no-transfer terms. Its content, tables and materials are excluded; nasal teaching uses independently retrieved NHS sources instead. No source graphics or article files are downloaded or included. Citation links do not grant commercial asset rights, and public NCBI hosting does not relicense an article. All added prose is brief original factual synthesis, not copied publisher expression. Existing original MIT code/text and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 obligations remain; no new mesh, font, texture, dependency, paid API or patient data.

## Software verification

Run `npm run cranial-bone-clinical-curriculum:test -- --source`. The test independently checks the 23 identities and all 24 components against the captured official ISA index, including both ordered hyoid files. Runtime resolution requires exact FMA, side, source tree, ordered files, primary/ordered region membership, skeleton system and bone category.

Checks cover resolver/display/export parity, detached arrays, retained warnings, non-target topics, draft and approval boundaries, and 22 corrupted-section cases. They do not prove medical correctness. The new offline history projection precedes axial-bone history: 42 projections / 2,506 pinned edits. Runtime and exports use current lessons. Original baseline and older captures stay fixed; previous axial-bone counts are explicitly historical while current direct/export checks remain active.

Clinical and Pathology each reach 463 draft / 559 pending body entries. Anatomy remains 1,020 draft / two identity-only; Function 1,018 draft / four pending. No CT, MRI, Ultrasound or Quiz content was added. The remaining 108 skeletal Clinical/Pathology gaps are the hand and foot bones (54 representations each).

## Clinical release gates

Independent anatomical, maxillofacial, ENT, ophthalmic, neurosurgical and radiological review is still required. Review symptom wording and urgency, side/source mapping, orbital and skull-base relationships, nasal/tear/palatal distinctions, normal variation and limits of the cited evidence. Explicitly review paediatric applicability before child teaching; the adult reference is not a paediatric anatomy or trapdoor-fracture model.

No patient fracture, intracranial haemorrhage, duct patency, soft-tissue injury, nerve damage, airway function, speech performance, instability or scan registration is demonstrated. No browser/device/visual or specialist acceptance is supplied by this content-only batch. Actual imaging, comprehensive referenced questions and full-body editorial persistence remain future work.
