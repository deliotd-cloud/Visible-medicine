# Elbow clinical teaching: reference and reuse scope

Original, concise drafts for 14 existing bilateral selections; no clinical
acceptance implied. The seven families retain their existing source anatomy.

  *Injury of the brachial artery accompanying simple closed elbow dislocation:

## Review requirements

Owner radiologist review must check the seven family distinctions, bilateral
identity, wording of vascular versus neurological evidence, and suitability for
learners. Approvals bind the exact revision. Source meshes cannot establish flow,
injury, patency, collateral reserve or viability. CT/MRI/X-ray/US remain pending;
future modality links require their own image/privacy/access checks. Paid lecture,
case and Atlas rights remain independent. No new service, package, font or fee.
