## References and scope

- [Zhou et al., 2017](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ofaj/94/3/94_119/_pdf),
  *Simple ways to dissect ciliary ganglion for orbital anatomical education*:
  introduction supports parasympathetic synapse and pupil/accommodation context;
  discussion provides pathway-level clinical context. Human cadaveric education
  study, not validation of our mesh, a diagnostic test or a procedural plan.
- [Hamel et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23076729/),
  *Ciliary ganglion afferents and efferents variations*: primary abstract reports
  20 dissected orbits, mixed fibre classes and variable roots; its proposed
  explanation of postganglionic mydriasis is not a proven universal mechanism.
