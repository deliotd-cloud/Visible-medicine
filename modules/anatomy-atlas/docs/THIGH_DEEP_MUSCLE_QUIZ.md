# Hip and thigh question coverage

Fifteen original formative questions cover thirty retained bilateral selections:
adductor brevis, longus, magnus and minimus; superior and inferior gemellus;
gracilis; iliacus; obturator externus and internus; pectineus; piriformis;
quadratus femoris; sartorius; and tensor fasciae latae. The existing 22 thigh
muscle quiz placements are preserved. This completes draft question coverage
for the current 52 root thigh-muscle representations, not all possible anatomy.

Questions distinguish actions, tendon destinations and anatomical relationships.
The adductor minimus question explicitly treats it as a described portion of
adductor magnus. The obturator-nerve question tests the relationship of its
divisions to adductor brevis, not a fixed nerve bifurcation location. Distractor
positions vary (4/4/4/3). These are normal-anatomy formative questions, not
fracture exams, procedural instruction, scan interpretation or clinical approval.
