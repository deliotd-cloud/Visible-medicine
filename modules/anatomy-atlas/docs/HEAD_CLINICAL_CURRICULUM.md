# Eye, swallowing and voice Clinical / Pathology drafts

9 September 2026: 120 brief original sections for 60 existing head-neck muscle selections, using 29 shared definitions and 65 exact source components. The existing two tabs are reused; no extra controls or geometry.

## Scope and important distinctions

- Eye: central internuclear dysfunction versus a medial-rectus lesion; VI/lateral rectus and IV/superior oblique; III/levator/superior rectus; orbital restriction versus weakness; Brown syndrome versus inferior-oblique weakness; ptosis mechanisms and urgent neurological warning signs.
- Hyoid region: digastric dual motor supply, C1 fibres carried with XII, floor-of-mouth infection spaces, stylohyoid muscle versus Eagle-syndrome ligament/bone pathology, and strap muscles versus thyroid gland/cartilage.
- Tongue: peripheral/nuclear XII patterns are not universal rules for supranuclear disease or resting position.
- Palate/pharynx: structural versus motor velopharyngeal dysfunction, tensor-veli V3 supply, stylopharyngeus IX exception, and Zenker mucosa/submucosa versus muscle.
- Voice: abduction versus adduction, airway risk versus voice symptoms, and vocalis/thyroarytenoid versus ligament and mucosal layers.

These are introductory teaching drafts, not a comprehensive pathology database or a patient diagnostic tool. Emergency wording must receive local clinical review. Source grouping is not an independent muscle count.

The checked cached ISA element-parts index also has no matching names; this is a scoped inventory observation, not proof that no usable dataset exists elsewhere. Facial-expression and masticatory coverage still needs licensed source discovery, identity/component review, spatial validation and mesh ingestion. No lesson was attached to a substitute surface.

Existing holds on middle constrictors, pharyngeal raphe and other withheld laryngeal structures remain. No complete pharyngeal tube, mucosal seal, tendon pulley, swallowing/voice motion or disease surface was inferred. Left digastric has three source components versus two right; thyroarytenoid has two per side. Those counts do not validate bellies or tissue layers. Uvular and transverse-arytenoid midline entries remain grouped.
