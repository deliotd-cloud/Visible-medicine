# Hand-bone X-ray orientation drafts

38 existing pinned selections gain X-ray orientation in the current Imaging
panel: ten metacarpals, ten proximal, eight middle and ten distal phalanges,
covering both sides. Carpals already have separate teaching and are not changed.
CT, MRI, ultrasound, Anatomy, Function, Clinical, Pathology and Quiz are preserved.

The notes distinguish CMC/MCP ends, finger PIP/DIP levels and the thumb's single
IP joint. Ten groups provide ray/level context. Projection overlap and comparison
across views are explained without claiming that an orbit/explode view is a
radiograph. Each returned lesson is draft and bound to the complete retained
source identity, geometry and provenance, not merely a matching FMA number.

## Sources and reuse boundaries

- [RadiologyInfo: Bone X-ray](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/bonerad): projection formation, multiple views and soft-tissue limits.
- [AAOS: Hand fractures](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/hand-fractures): metacarpal/phalangeal organisation and fifth-metacarpal neck context.
- [AAOS: Finger fractures](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/finger-fractures/): joint naming and localising bony findings.
- [AAOS: Thumb fractures](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/thumb-fractures): thumb metacarpal/phalanges and CMC/MCP/IP distinction.

Checked 25 September 2026. These are factual references, not licences to reuse
the sites' text, images or radiographs. New teaching is original concise prose;
no external figure, scan, model, font or dependency was copied or added. Existing
mesh provenance/notices remain unchanged. Projection-overlap cues are educational
applications of projection geometry, not findings measured on a patient study.
