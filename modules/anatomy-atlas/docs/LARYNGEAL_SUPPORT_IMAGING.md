# Laryngeal-support CT/MRI orientation

Twenty original draft topic placements cover ten existing head-neck selections:
bilateral thyrohyoid membranes, lateral thyrohyoid and vocal ligaments, and
median thyrohyoid, hyoepiglottic, thyroepiglottic and median cricothyroid ligaments.
Seven shared teaching groups retain the complete individual source identities;
the hyoepiglottic source's unspecified laterality is not silently relabelled.

The notes distinguish anatomical search locations from resolved image findings.
They compare neighbouring hyoid/cartilage landmarks and pre-epiglottic boundaries,
without promising that routine CT or MRI resolves each thin ligament. In particular,
the cited MR study did not separately identify vocal ligaments or conus elasticus.
That finding is acquisition-specific, not a claim that no future sequence can
resolve them. No scan acquisition, airway procedure or cancer-staging instruction
is added. These neck selections are not bound to the approved head scans.

## Factual reading references, not imported assets

- [TTUHSC larynx tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/larynx_tables.html): hyoid/thyroid/arytenoid relationships.
- [Joshi et al., Imaging in laryngeal cancers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3624744/): normal cross-sectional orientation and pre-epiglottic boundaries only.
- [Sakai et al., MR imaging of the larynx at 1.5 T](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2298998/): explicit limits of separate ligament identification in the reported acquisition.
- [Elsevier, cricothyroid membrane](https://www.elsevier.com/resources/anatomy/respiratory-system/upper-respiratory-system/cricothyroid-membrane/24990): median-band attachment and distinction from lateral conus elasticus.
