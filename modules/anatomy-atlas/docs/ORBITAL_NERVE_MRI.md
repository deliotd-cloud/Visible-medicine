# Orbital nerve MRI — draft extension

Fourteen MRI draft placements cover the existing bilateral superior and inferior
oculomotor divisions, trochlear, frontal, lacrimal and nasociliary nerves, and
ciliary ganglia. The complete pinned display identity and MRI allowlist control
admission; matching a name or FMA identifier alone is insufficient. Each callback
returns fresh lesson arrays.

## References and scope

Original short factual summaries link to primary abstracts checked on
26 September 2026:

- [Li et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33811598/): superior oculomotor division and trochlear drafts.
- [Arizono et al., 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42034568/): inferior oculomotor division, frontal, lacrimal, nasociliary and ciliary-ganglion drafts.

The nasociliary draft explicitly distinguishes poor reader agreement from
detectability. Grouped results remain grouped; no individual nerve percentage is
inferred. Li is not used to support V1 or ganglion teaching.

Distal V1, ethmoidal and ciliary nerve branches are excluded from this extension.
Cisternal and intraorbital evidence are not interchangeable. No additional source
identity, geometry, anatomy lesson, clinical topic, control or asset is supplied.

The static reference mesh is not a patient scan, a validated registration or
evidence of nerve function/pathology. Visibility does not establish diagnostic
sensitivity. The cited cohorts do not validate disease-cohort performance.
Revision-bound radiologist sign-off remains pending. Atlas, imaging-case and
paid-lecture access remain independent.

No article text, figures, images, tables, scans, identifiers, contours or other
derivatives are imported or redistributed. Source accessibility grants no
relicensing permission. CT-head masks, accepted boundaries and clinical PACS
remain outside this work.

## Verification boundary

`npm run orbital-nerve-mri:test` verifies complete source identity and bundle
bytes, compares the current teaching with the actual parent Git source, checks
all 9,936 topic placements (14 additions / 9,922 unchanged), exercises mutated
and opposite-side identities, and renders the real information-panel callback.
It checks exported content and unsigned review material, source-summary budgets,
and both negative AC1 endpoints. The exact offline transition rejects mixed or
unrecorded history; it cannot transfer an approval.

The existing laryngeal/oesophageal history suites project only this recorded
extension before testing their original immutable snapshots. No historical
expected hash is replaced. These code/component checks are not browser/GPU
acceptance, publication, external backup or clinical approval. Existing release
and privacy gates remain in force.
