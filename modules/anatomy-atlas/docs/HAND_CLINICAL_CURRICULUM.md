## Scope and distinctions

The teaching connects intrinsic muscle actions with median/ulnar dysfunction, thenar wasting, thumb-base joint pain, compensatory pinch and intrinsic imbalance. It distinguishes active little-finger abduction from resting outward drift, thumb opposition from adduction, long-flexor compensation from recovered adductor function, and the usual mixed nerve supply of lumbricals.

The adductor heads share whole-muscle context. Lumbricals and interossei remain grouped source surfaces, not individually numbered muscles or segmented nerve territories. No clinical sign alone localises a lesion. No disease mesh, patient scan, strength measurement or treatment instructions are supplied.
