## Limits that must survive sign-off

The lens note distinguishes specialised UBM biometry from routine B-scan, and
diameter from thickness. It does not generalise to traumatic displacement or
treat the reported method as a gold standard. The scleral note refers to an
illustrated affected eye, not a sensitivity/specificity result or universal
diagnostic threshold. The combined ultrasound coats are not this isolated mesh.
No acquisition instructions, treatment recommendations or imported images.

Both are drafts for radiologist review. No anatomy approval, validated dimensions,
probe correspondence, scan access or imaging entitlement is inferred. Existing
CT/MRI teaching, other eye topics, model bytes and independent product access
are preserved. Didanix desktop and CT-head masks are outside this change.
