# Source-bound portal tributary and hepatic outflow teaching

This increment replaces only the pending Clinical and Pathology payloads for
eight exact admitted selections: the splenic vein, bilateral gastroepiploic
(gastro-omental) veins, bilateral gastric veins, the middle hepatic vein, and
the source-labelled right and left hepatic tributary groups. The eight family
keys remain distinct. Every match requires the complete catalog identity, not
an FMA identifier alone.

The lessons distinguish portal venous inflow from hepatic venous outflow and
give structure-specific educational context for portal obstruction, gastric
collaterals, and hepatic outflow obstruction. They are short drafts, not a
diagnostic pathway, treatment protocol, procedural plan, or claim of disease.
Static surfaces do not establish CT, MRI, ultrasound or Doppler findings.

The source geometry and bundle bytes, existing Anatomy/Function and imaging
lessons, study recipes, navigation, review state, and the other 9,902 topic
payloads remain unchanged. The right hepatic tributary selection retains four
source files and seven disconnected components; the left retains five files
and five components. Each remains one source-labelled group, not newly named
veins or a validated drainage territory. No bridging, continuity, lumen, flow,
pressure, exact confluence, or Couinaud territory is inferred. Root selections
and nested liver-branches selections remain distinct.

The factual text is original and accompanied by visible reference and credit
fields. No article figure, table, patient scan, identifier, logo, or uncleared
derivative is imported. Article- or publisher-specific reuse terms do not
extend to third-party media or establish project-wide clearance.

`npm run portal-hepatic-clinical:test` checks the immutable baseline and
transition, all 16 replacements and 16 server-rendered notes, all 1,102 schema
records, complete identity/source/topic rejection, unchanged bundle bytes and
recipes, and strict historical before/after/mixed/unrelated-drift states. Its
machine-readable report is `docs/portal-hepatic-clinical-validation.json`.

Clinical approval remains false. Revision-bound radiologist review, physical-
device acceptance, authenticated model staging, and independent Atlas, case,
and paid-lecture access remain separate release gates.
