- Five entries retain unspecified laterality: descending thoracic aorta, superior vena cava, azygos, great cardiac vein and middle cardiac vein. Hemiazygos remains source-midline.
- The brachiocephalic/subclavian inlet entries retain thorax, shoulder-arm and head-neck memberships in their existing order. Thoracoabdominal wall branches retain thorax/abdomen memberships.

- No missing left internal thoracic vein, accessory hemiazygos, perfusion territory, coronary dominance or spinal arterial connection is reconstructed.
- Red/blue is artery/vein classification, not oxygen content. Explode and cut views do not display disease, angiographic contrast, pressure, Doppler or physiological flow.
