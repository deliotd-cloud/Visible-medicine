# Shoulder and upper-arm artery questions

Seven original formative question families replace identification-only Quiz
prompts for fourteen existing bilateral arterial selections. The axillary,
brachial, deep brachial, anterior/posterior circumflex humeral, circumflex scapular
and thoracodorsal surfaces retain their complete source records and model bytes.
Source baseline: `39a454bbbaa6966b801b72d580077bf12b073aed`.

Questions use the established four-choice panel, correct/wrong feedback, retry,
reset and exam concealment. The seven shared concepts are not fourteen distinct
lessons. All other anatomy and teaching topics remain outside this batch.

references do not grant permission to redistribute their media.

The static source surfaces do not prove complete lumens, patency, perfusion,
continuous courses or safe procedural routes. No acquired scan, patient-space
registration or clinical approval is added. Atlas, imaging-case and paid-lecture
entitlements remain independent.
