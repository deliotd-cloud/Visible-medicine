## Scope and primary evidence

- The [AIUM 2023 practice parameter](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jum.16234)
  supports usual ipsilateral transtemporal MCA/ACA and P1/P2 flow orientations
  and method-specific interpretation. These are acquired Doppler relationships,
  not properties or measurements of static meshes.
- The [primary practice standards](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17238867/)
  support segment/context-based identification. Whole source selections are not
  separately validated A1/A2, M1/M2 or P1/P2 surfaces.
- [Baumgartner et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9010531/) studied communicating
  cross-flow in occlusive disease with adequate windows; PCom inference had false
  positives and negatives. No diagnostic performance is generalized to normal
  subjects, a different technique or the Atlas donor.
- [Klotzsch et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8610318/) support variable PCom
  detection and flow interpretation, not a fixed model arrow.
- [Marinoni et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9372576/) document inadequate
  acoustic windows; missing signal alone is not proof of absent anatomy.

References are factual reading links, not permission to copy publisher prose,
figures, video, protocols or datasets. All added copy is original and brief. No
media or new dependency/font/texture/model is distributed, and no mandatory-fee
service is introduced. No numeric diagnostic thresholds, compression manoeuvres,
