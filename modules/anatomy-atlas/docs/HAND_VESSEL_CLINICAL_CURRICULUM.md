Case reports do not establish incidence. The one-/two-artery comparison concerns selected well-perfused fingers, not permission to omit repair in every injury. Cadaver variations and different arterial nomenclatures do not identify a variant in these meshes. Venous findings cannot diagnose isolated arch thrombosis. The historical congestion report supplies the concept of congestion before obvious colour change only, not a validated test or management recommendation; its compression manoeuvre is not reproduced.

Some full-text opens returned browser checks and some PubMed opens were empty. Accessible official-source pages and indexed original abstracts supported only the stated facts. No access controls were bypassed and no inaccessible full article was claimed to have been reviewed. Clinical acceptance requires full evidence appraisal, not abstract-only treatment decisions.

## Independent acceptance remains

- Anatomist and hand/vascular specialists: every identity, side, extent, component grouping, branching/venous plane, numbered-source interpretation and clinical statement.
- Reconstructive review: thumb/finger perfusion, venous return, limited study populations and rare-case framing. No safe incision, pin trajectory, vascular sacrifice rule, flap boundary or procedure is validated.
- Emergency advice, NHS 111 localization, patient-facing wording and accessibility require review.
- Spatial, visual, mobile and screen-reader acceptance are separate. Sites background rules prevented browser QA/handoff; automated tests are not substitutes.
- Actual licensed/de-identified CT/MRI/US studies, radiology annotation and registration validation remain absent.
- Preserve unresolved FMA45097/FMA45098, FMA19728 and FMA61970 without invented identity or approval.
