## Evidence and editorial limits

Each displayed/exported lesson includes its own reference links. Sources were consulted on 9 September 2026:

- NHS stroke guidance supports the aggregate-brain context and UK emergency wording; NINDS and Weill Cornell support syringomyelia context.
- GeneReviews supports Huntington disease; NINDS and the NCBI-hosted neuroscience chapter support Parkinson disease and striatal input distinctions.
- A published carbon-monoxide imaging report and CDC guidance support pallidal/systemic context, without making pallidal abnormalities diagnostic.
- ILAE supports focal emotional-seizure distinctions. Thalamic stroke and pain studies support variable location-dependent consequences.
- University of Utah neuro-ophthalmic teaching supports lateral-geniculate field distinctions. Human medial-geniculate case reports illustrate possible central auditory effects, not a general prediction.
- Fornix infarction reports support acute-memory context. An anterior-commissure malformation report does not establish an isolated syndrome or universal compensation.
- Stanford supports dorsal-midbrain gaze context. Nationwide Children's and a primary agenesis study support variable callosal outcomes and the persistence of other commissural connections.
- NCI supports choroid-plexus tumour distinctions; NIAAA and an MR clinical study support multiregional Wernicke-related context and the emergency caveat.

Rare reports and selected cohorts are not population incidence, deterministic localization, prognosis or treatment evidence. Geometry statements describe software/source limits, not patient findings. No procedural target, drug dose, treatment sequence, outcome percentage, diagnostic test or publisher question bank is reproduced.

Only brief original factual synthesis and links are shipped. No article, illustration, table, screenshot, texture, scan, case image or third-party prose is imported. Public access and citation do not license commercial asset reuse, including on NCBI/PMC. Existing original MIT code/text and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 attribution remain unchanged; no additional asset, font, dependency, paid API or mandatory service is introduced. This is not a systematic review, legal clearance or specialist validation.

## Remaining acceptance and next work

Independent anatomy, neurology and neuroradiology review must check source extents, side/group metadata, central versus peripheral distinctions, disease associations and evidence strength. Neuro-ophthalmology, audiology and paediatric review apply to relevant lessons. Emergency wording needs local-service review outside the UK. Geometry/tract completeness, device accessibility, actual patient-image licensing and registration, content revision review and clinical approval remain independent gates. Automated content checks do not establish any of them.

Next authoring: 80 pending organ-system entries, then 89 connective and 227 vessel entries; two bone, one muscle and one neural identity remain held. Fuller referenced questions, full-body editorial persistence and user-supplied US/CT/MRI integration remain part of the whole-atlas goal.
