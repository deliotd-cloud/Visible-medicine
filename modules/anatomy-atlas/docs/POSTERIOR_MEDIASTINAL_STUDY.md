# Posterior mediastinal source study

Thorax and Whole body Dissect offer a compact comparison of four existing source
surfaces: oesophagus, descending thoracic aorta, azygos vein and hemiazygos vein.
The study starts from behind and uses existing search, rotation, selection,
Remove, Undo/Redo and separation controls. Return separation to 0% before
examining the source-coordinate arrangement.

These entire supplied surfaces are not a segmented posterior-mediastinal
compartment. Their catalogue laterality labels are unpaired, unspecified or
midline, so they remain available with either side filter. That behaviour does
not mean that each vessel has bilateral anatomical symmetry. No source is
mirrored, trimmed or relabelled for this view.

## Teaching reference and limitations

The grouping follows the posterior-mediastinal contents described in TTUHSC
El Paso's [Lungs and Mediastina dissector explanations](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/lungs_ans.html),
objectives 10–11, accessed 24 September 2026. This is a factual reference for
original short guidance; no illustration, table, mesh or copied prose is imported.
The normal azygos course is right-sided and the hemiazygos crosses towards it;
these teaching relationships still need assessment against the supplied surfaces.

The view does not establish joined lumina, drainage junctions, vertebral levels,
complete tributaries, compartment boundaries or a surgical approach. Missing
thoracic duct, nodes and autonomic nerves are not reconstructed. Existing whole
surfaces can extend beyond the named compartment. No CT/MRI registration,
acquired image, clinical approval or patient-specific application is supplied.

The four existing BodyParts3D 4.0 records retain their full IDs, component hashes,
CC BY 4.0 credits and unvalidated status. No asset, dependency, font, texture,
subscription or other service is added. Clinical sign-off must bind the actual
source/renderer revision and reviewed scope.

## Verification boundary

The focused source test covers identity and component corruption, duplicate or
missing sources, region/side scope, discovery and reversible dissection. Exact
offline history reversal preserves prior recipe hashes; it never removes the
new view from the live runtime. Browser/device, backup and actual publication
outcomes are recorded in the dated main-task checkpoint rather than inferred
from a test or source commit.

On 24 September 2026, the local Thorax desktop sample at 1087 × 854 opened
the study through search and its reset confirmation, selected the oesophagus,
and verified four enabled selections → Remove → three → Undo → four. The
posterior render was visually inspected at 0% separation. Whole-body and all
side-filter combinations passed source tests, not a fresh browser/device sample.
The full regional structure browser still includes removed structures by default;
its existing enabled-only checkbox limits the list to this study's four targets.
No physical-device, screen-reader or clinical acceptance is implied.
