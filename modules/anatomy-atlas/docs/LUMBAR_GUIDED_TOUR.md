# Lower lumbar spine and sacrum — draft guided tour

Five source-bound stops reuse L4, L5, the sacrum and the existing discs labelled
for the fourth and fifth lumbar vertebrae. No mesh, transform, source identifier,
licence or held anatomy is changed. This adds a second Spine option to the
existing Guided learning selector and the whole-body tour library; cervical
orientation remains the default. No extra top-level tab or panel is added.

## Teaching and source boundaries

Original brief captions cover the body/posterior arch, whole-disc surface,
posterior elements, lower disc/sacral transition and anterior sacrum. References
checked29 September2026:

- [TTUHSC El Paso back anatomy tables](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/back_tables.html):
  vertebral components, lumbar/sacral anatomy and facet-joint relationships.
- [UAMS back joints and ligaments](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/joint-tables/joints-and-ligaments-of-the-back-region/):
  intervertebral discs and their gross components.

These are factual references for original prose only. No source diagrams,
tables, clinical images, textbook extracts or examination questions are copied.
Existing BodyParts3D4.0/CC BY4.0 and dependency notices remain unchanged.
No paid service, asset, font, texture or new dependency is introduced.

Disc names deliberately preserve source labels rather than asserting a
validated L4–L5/L5–S1 patient interval. The whole-disc meshes do not independently
segment annulus, nucleus or endplates. Roots, cauda equina, ligaments, joint
capsules and canal contents remain absent. No stenosis, herniation, mechanical
load, procedural route or acquired-image registration is inferred.
