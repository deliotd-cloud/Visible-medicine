## Factual references and limits

References checked 15 October 2026. Some direct web fetches failed/returned a
challenge; the identified primary PubMed/PMC and ACR indexed text was inspected
instead. This is not a claim to have purchased or reviewed unavailable full text.

- [ACR Acute Hip Pain, 2024 update](https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/3082587/narrative): variant5 separately considers suspected tendon/muscle/ligament injury after negative or indeterminate radiographs. This supports the soft-tissue limitation, not a patient-specific examination order or diagnostic conclusion.
- [ACR/RSNA Bone X-ray](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/bonerad): bone/projection context and soft-tissue limitations. No illustrative image is imported.
- [Texas Tech lower-limb anatomy table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_lowerlimb.html): attachment orientation only; copyrighted table and images are not copied.
- [Kruse et al., adductor magnus origin](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38733350/), DOI10.1002/jum.16480: original sonographic study supports distinguishing ischiocondylar attachment context from neighbouring hamstrings, not plain-film muscle visibility.
- [Zhong et al., pes anatomy and ultrasound](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5908566/), DOI10.1097/MD.0000000000010352: gracilis is part of a shared pes region; the source does not validate a radiographic tendon outline or this model.
- [Olewnik et al., quadratus anatomy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38830558/), DOI10.1016/j.aanat.2024.152284: cadaveric variation does not establish a patient muscle diagnosis from a projected bone interval, or validate this specimen's belly count.

Keep bony orientation separate from individual muscle/tendon visibility. A
normal bony film does not establish intact fibres or tendon continuity; projected
ischiofemoral geometry does not diagnose quadratus oedema or symptomatic
impingement. No acquired image, procedure guidance, treatment threshold or
source-to-patient registration is added. Static meshes and explode views do not
simulate X-ray attenuation. Atlas, cases and paid lectures remain independent.
