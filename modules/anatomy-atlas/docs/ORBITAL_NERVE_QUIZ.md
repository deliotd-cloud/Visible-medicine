# Orbital nerve Quiz drafts

Fifteen original formative questions cover thirty retained bilateral orbital
nerve/ganglion representations. Paired structures share a question about usual
anatomy; these are fifteen concepts, not thirty different lessons. They replace
identification-only Quiz notes using the existing radio-answer, feedback and
retry controls. No new navigation or visual clutter is introduced.

The exact source baseline is
`b436df6fd03170d27c08d20bf6b29c7d27e0c320`. The pins record complete display
identities, previous lessons, coordinate/catalogue identity and verified bundle
bytes. Names or FMA identifiers alone cannot authorize a lesson: changed source
records are refused. The separately held short-ciliary source group is untouched.

Questions distinguish the ophthalmic division and its orbital branches,
oculomotor divisions, trochlear motor supply and ciliary ganglion pathways.
They describe usual anatomy, not verified continuous fibres, complete source
surfaces, patient-specific reflexes or procedural guidance. Clinical review
remains pending at the exact source and teaching revision.

Factual reading links are listed with each original question in
`content/orbital-nerve-quiz.ts`. No publisher prose, tables, illustrations,
question banks, imaging or new anatomical geometry are imported. References
do not grant rights to the publishers' media. Existing model licences and
credits remain unchanged.

respectively. Mirrored TTUHSC nerve tables are counted together, not treated as
independent sources. Only the cited anatomical facts support these questions;
unrelated clinical statements or illustrations on those pages are not imported.

The focused suite checks the thirty transitions, all other topic placements,
source mutation refusal, current/stale/tampered Review packets and actual
question interactions including correct/wrong feedback, retry/reset, focus and
exam concealment. Source-only checks do not establish integrated delivery.
