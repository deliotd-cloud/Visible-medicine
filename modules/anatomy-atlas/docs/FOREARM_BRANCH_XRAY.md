# Forearm branch contrast-X-ray teaching

Twelve exact bilateral existing forearm selections, six arterial families,
15 October 2026. Original teaching drafts; revision-bound radiologist review is
required. No acquired angiograms, new geometry or clinical approval is supplied.

The batch covers common, anterior and recurrent interosseous arteries, radial
recurrent arteries, and anterior/posterior ulnar recurrent arteries. Existing
source pins establish identity only. No parent vessel, connected lumen, complete
arcade, measured calibre, flow, perfusion or safe procedural route is inferred.
The unsupplied posterior interosseous trunk remains absent. Median antebrachial
vein X-ray teaching remains pending; the arterial evidence does not cover it.

Contrast catheter angiography/DSA and plain radiography are distinguished from
each other and from CTA/MRA/Doppler. Cadaver dissection is anatomical evidence,
not a visibility test. A selected diseased-wrist cohort is not a normal-donor
detection study. Reassembling the Atlas gives reference orientation, not image
registration. Side markers must come from the actual acquisition.

## Evidence and deliberate limits

- [Texas Tech upper-limb artery table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html): usual named origins and branch distinctions only. Its prose, tables, drawings and images are not reproduced.
- [Goelz et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36980506/), [publisher record](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4418/13/6/1198): 17 selected Kienbock-disease patients; superselective wrist angiography, common interosseous access context and anterior interosseous/radiocarpal assessment. Not normal proximal-branch detectability, donor measurements or a protocol for this product.
- [Hayashi and Maruyama, 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2350635/): dissection of recurrent arteries in 16 cadavers; variable communications. Not universal arcade continuity, acquired X-ray detection or a treatment recommendation.
- [Adult elbow arterial study, 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9384425/): injected anatomy of 22 upper extremities; medial/lateral arcade context. Not a living angiographic accuracy study or validation of these meshes.
- [Radial recurrent variation study, 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23440497/): variable radial recurrent morphology. Not the actual origin or safe catheter route in either Atlas side.

Only original factual summaries and reading links are added. No article prose,
figure, table, scan, measurement dataset or media is copied, adapted or imported.
Existing BodyParts3D CC BY4.0 attribution and source restrictions remain intact.
No new dependency, font, texture, paid service or mandatory fee.

## Verification and review

The helper matches complete existing source identity, not names or FMA alone.
Tests compare all root topics and Review packets against the immutable baseline,
then separately verify current selected content and refusal of mutated identity,
forged packets and stale/forged decisions before synthetic storage. A passing
test is not approval of the anatomy, clinical facts or imaging visibility.

The original transition must remain pinned to its introduction commit when later
teaching changes are authored. Current selected bindings and security checks
continue independently. Website delivery uses the normal generated-module and
private Review pipelines; source completion alone is not website integration.
