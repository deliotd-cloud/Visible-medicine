# Nested guided learning in the shared library

The existing Guided learning Tour picker includes **Right eye layers** and
**Left eye layers** in Head & neck and Whole body. Selecting one replaces the
regional tour with the existing eye workbench inline, not a dialog, second viewer,
iframe or separate window. Its Guided learning disclosure starts expanded; the
learner must explicitly press **Start eye walkthrough** after the model is ready.
The existing 24 regional tours, ordering and defaults remain unchanged.

Each eye is independently admitted against the exact current parent, coordinate
frame, parent-bundle record and complete pinned child set. Missing, duplicate or
modified source records are not repaired, mirrored or substituted. Invalidated
selections unmount the stale view and fall back to an available regional tour.
Switching study/source/guide contents remounts its camera, readiness and session;
reselecting the same guide retains the current session. Region changes cannot
retain an out-of-region eye guide. Existing asset prefixes and education source
bindings are passed unchanged, without granting imaging/lecture access.

Only the entry point changes. The [eye guide](EYE_LAYER_GUIDED_LEARNING.md)
still uses four original draft stops, smooth/reduced-motion camera behavior,
visibility rather than tissue translation, loading/hidden-page/renderer pauses,
and prior manual-view/camera restoration. Ending guidance returns to manual eye
exploration; the Tour picker changes study and Exit tour returns to the atlas.
No automatic start, surgical-plane, clinical-approval or scan-registration claim.

The requirement inventory separately records two nested eye guides/eight stops
over 15 existing children, with source provenance and fingerprints. These overlap
the 108 nested selections; they add no root anatomy, meshes or regional tours.
Malformed stop/child counts are rejected instead of generating misleading totals.
No new assets, fonts, textures, dependencies or fees; existing notices remain.

Teaching packets/checklists and source identities remain unchanged from the
saved eye guide. Normal renderer regeneration requires geometry re-review but
does not invalidate otherwise unchanged teaching histories or migrate approval.
Controlled UI/source/API tests and builds do not prove actual keyboard/GPU/zoom
behavior. Signed-in browser/readability and revision-bound radiologist acceptance
remain required; no deployment is implied by source availability.
