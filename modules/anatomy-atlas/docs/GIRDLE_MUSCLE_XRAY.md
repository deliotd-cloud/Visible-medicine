# Shoulder-girdle muscle X-ray orientation

Sixteen retained root-body selections gain X-ray drafts in Shoulder/arm and
Whole body: bilateral anconeus, brachialis, coracobrachialis, levator scapulae,
rhomboid major/minor, serratus anterior and teres major. Projected bony orientation
is distinguished from seeing an individual muscle, proving tendon integrity or
assessing movement. This does not expand the separate nine-structure shoulder pilot.

`girdleMuscleXrayLesson` binds complete exact identities from the existing
`content/shoulder-arm-muscle-imaging-pins.json`, with independently checked
IDs, sides, source files, bundle and unvalidated status. Other topics or altered
source records are refused. No new pin, mirrored surface, geometry, acquired
image, mask or spatial registration is introduced. Continuous tours and their
caption/camera definitions are unchanged.

## Factual references and limits

- [Texas Tech upper-limb muscle anatomy](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_upperlimb.html): bony attachment facts only, not copied table or illustrations.
- [Mozes et al., 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10580821/): selected snapping-scapula cohort; projected bony incongruity counts are not population sensitivity or muscle/nerve function tests.
- [Winblad et al., brachialis case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4897327/): one adolescent injury with negative radiographic bony findings and MRI-demonstrated muscle injury, not a detection rate. Its CC BY-NC-ND licence does not permit commercial image/adaptation reuse; no prose or asset is imported.
- [Fitzpatrick et al., teres-major case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4861626/): one injury with normal standard radiographs and MRI-demonstrated injury, not a general visibility claim or acquisition prescription.
- [ACR acute elbow and forearm pain](https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/3195154/Narrative), [abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39488347/): adult guideline context for suspected soft-tissue injury despite normal/indeterminate radiographs. No guideline table or patient imaging pathway is reproduced.
- [ACR chronic shoulder pain update](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37236752/): contextual distinction, not a coracobrachialis integrity test or individual examination recommendation.

All teaching prose is original project work; references support factual synthesis
only. No third-party figure, table, scan, measurement dataset, prose or media is
copied, adapted or relicensed. Existing BodyParts3D attribution and commercial
licence obligations remain. No new asset, package, font, service or mandatory fee.
