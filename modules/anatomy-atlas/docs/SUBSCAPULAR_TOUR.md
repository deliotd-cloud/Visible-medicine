# Right shoulder: subscapular branches

29 September 2026. New draft in Guided learning, available from Shoulder & arm
and the whole-body library. The existing muscle tour remains the default;
the same compact Tour selector adds this option without another toolbar.

Four stops highlight the right axillary, subscapular, circumflex scapular and
thoracodorsal arteries, with the existing right scapula as faded context. The
camera moves from an overview into source-bound branch frames, with the shared
1.8-second smooth orbit and reduced-motion alternative. Playback waits for the
required anatomy and explicit Start. Existing imaging notes and self-checks
remain available where authored; no acquired image or spatial link is invented.

## Source and content

Original 123-word captions describe typical parent/branch relationships using
the [UAMS upper-limb artery table](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/artery-tables/arteries-of-the-upper-limb/),
checked 29 September. No source table, diagram or article text is redistributed.
The four arterial identities are FMA22655, FMA22678, FMA23180 and FMA66321.
Existing BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 sources, model bytes, positions and notices are
unchanged. No new asset, dependency, font, texture or paid service.

Selected surfaces do not establish joined lumina, measured branch junctions,
complete collateral anatomy, patient variants, perfusion or clinical validity.
Muscles and nerves are not shown. Source limitations are visible in the player.
The dedicated nine-structure shoulder pilot is unchanged.
