## Scope

Distinguish muscle/tendon injury from neurological weakness, proximal injury from distal shared attachment problems, and common syndromes from a single presumed tissue diagnosis. Examples include lateral hip pain with or without bursitis, supported-side abductor dysfunction, possible deep gluteal causes of sciatica-like pain, asymptomatic ischiofemoral MRI changes, internal/external snapping, pes-region pain, hamstring avulsion and the knee extensor mechanism.

Magnus/minimus separation is variable. Psoas/iliacus and the biceps heads have distinct motor supplies. Sartorius and gracilis are not hamstrings despite sharing the pes insertion with semitendinosus. Vasti do not cross the hip. Shared clinical notes do not validate a tendon compartment, nerve territory or injury in each selected head.

No disease geometry, patient scan, measurement threshold, management protocol, rehabilitation timetable or clinical approval is supplied.
