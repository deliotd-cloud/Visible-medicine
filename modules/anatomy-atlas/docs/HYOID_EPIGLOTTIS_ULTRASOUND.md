## Evidence and applicability

Parmar et al., 2014, [primary airway ultrasound study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4126113/),
examined 100 healthy adults; short neck, morbid obesity and abnormal airway were
excluded. The factual summaries distinguish superficial hyoid echoes/shadow
and epiglottic tissue from fat and air-interface echoes. This selected population
does not establish universal visibility. No measurements, diagnostic thresholds,
probe-placement procedure, swallowing assessment or patient interpretation is
provided. Original captions use 133 source-derived words in total.
