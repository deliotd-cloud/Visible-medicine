## Primary evidence and commercial boundary

- [Yu et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36512153/): CTA study of
  35 patients, variable origins and muscular/septal perforator courses.
- [Jiang et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27446322/): retrospective
  CE-MRA series, 68 patients / 136 limbs; parent vessels seen but tertiary branch
  identification incomplete. This is not a routine-MRI sensitivity estimate.
- [Debelmas et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29481406/): prospective
  colour-Doppler comparison of 30 perforators with operative findings; useful
  localisation, not exact correspondence in all cases.
