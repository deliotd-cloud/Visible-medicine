# Coronary arterial guided learning

The draft **Coronary arteries: surface orientation** sequence is available under
Guided learning in Thorax and the Whole body tour library. It uses the existing
four artery selections and heart context, not new anatomy or a separate viewer.
The earlier airway/vessel and chest-wall tours retain their definitions and order.

## Six stops

1. Right coronary trunk: anterior orientation against the faded heart.
2. Left coronary trunk: left-side comparison with its two named branch selections.
3. Anterior interventricular branch (LAD): anterior close-up.
4. Circumflex: left-side relationship with the trunk and heart.
5. Circumflex again: posterior comparison of the same selection.
6. Return to the complete four-artery overview and distinguish reference anatomy
   from future acquired coronary CTA.

The source IDs/FMA identities remain FMA3802, FMA3855, FMA3862 and FMA3895, with
FMA7088 as heart context. The LAD aggregate contains 18 original source files;
the circumflex contains six. These are file memberships, not validated coronary
segments, vessel calibre, clinical branch counts or continuity. The heart aggregate
is exterior context, not a myocardial supply territory or segmented wall map.

## Playback and review

Each stop requests the established 1,800 ms quintic-orbit camera transition and
14-second reading interval. The repeated circumflex selection has a distinct step,
view and camera frame; changing only the camera does not invent a second structure.
Play/Pause, step selection, reduced motion, renderer readiness, missing-source
refusal, imaging-link suspension and Exit use the existing player. Separation is
zero throughout. Opening imaging notes or a quick check pauses playback.

The exact five-record source, frame, licence, credits and bundle gate used by the
coronary dissection also guards the tour. Altered sequences, captions, source hashes
or camera-frame definitions are refused; exported-object mutation cannot redefine
the admitted sequence. All five participants receive the complete tour in their
teaching review evidence. Prior approvals are not inherited. Geometry and teaching
remain separate review tracks; no imaging approval is available without a validated
mapping. Atlas access does not grant access to a separate imaging case or paid lecture.

References are factual reading links: [TTUHSC thoracic artery table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_thorax.html)
and [ACR/RSNA coronary CTA overview](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiocoroct).
Captions are original drafts; no reference illustration, scan, texture or model was
copied. Existing BodyParts3D credits and commercial-compatible reuse notices remain.

## Acceptance still required

The owner-radiologist must review exact arterial extent, grouped-file identity,
typical course wording, labels, fading, tiny-trunk visibility and all six camera
frames. Check smooth transitions, pause/resume and selection on actual devices.
Source fixtures and component harnesses do not prove rendered or clinical accuracy.
This sequence does not validate a complete coronary tree, ostia, connected lumens,
nodal/marginal/posterior interventricular branches, dominance, flow, stenosis,
patient correspondence or calibrated CT/MRI projections.

Reproduce the focused source/player checks with `npm run coronary-arterial-tour:test`.
The 30-tour historical Review suite retains its exact pre-addition epoch; the new
suite checks current 31-tour/183-stop material, unchanged earlier sequences, all
9,936 existing topic placements, five changed teaching identities and 1,099
unchanged teaching identities. These counts are coverage evidence, not approval.
