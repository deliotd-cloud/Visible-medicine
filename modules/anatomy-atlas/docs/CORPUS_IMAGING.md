## Evidence and limits

- [Satragno, Martinoli and Cittadini (1989), Magnetic resonance imaging of the
  penis: normal anatomy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2918824/) supports the
  limited MRI demonstration of compartments, coverings and urethral lumen with
  combined T1/T2 and different planes. This historical technical study is not
  evidence of universal routine visibility, modern diagnostic accuracy or model
  validation. No fixed MR signal, sequence parameters or accuracy statistics added.
- [AIUM Practice Parameter for the Performance of Penile Ultrasound (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1002/jum.16235)
  supports longitudinal/transverse evaluation and the distinction between
  anatomical and colour/spectral Doppler assessment. The primary professional
  document was read directly, including its examination specification. No
  instrumentation, pharmacostimulation, dosage or treatment instructions copied.
- [NCI SEER Training: Penis](https://training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/reproductive/male/penis.html)
  supports the urethra-associated spongiosal column versus paired cavernosal bodies.
