# Shoulder vessels: X-ray orientation and evidence limits

Fourteen retained source selections receive original X-ray limitation drafts:
bilateral thyrocervical/costocervical trunks, dorsal scapular/suprascapular
arteries, axillary/suprascapular veins and medial brachial veins. These notes
do not add vessels, radiographs, connected lumens, flow or patient registration.

`shoulderVesselXrayLesson` requires the complete existing canonical identity
from four unchanged pin sets: shoulder-girdle artery quiz, systemic venous,
suprascapular vessel ultrasound and upper venous clinical. Name matching,
proximity, mirrored sides and altered nested source records are refused.
The medial brachial pair remains in its separately recovered bundle; it is
not relabelled as the complete brachial venous system.

## Factual reading

- [Texas Tech arteries](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html) and [axilla teaching](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/musculoskeletal_system/axilla_ans.html): anatomical orientation, not copied tables, prose or illustrations.
- [Thyrocervical CTA study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36350499/): 55 patients screened, 82 trunks in 41 patients aged 15–82 analyzed. Not 55 analyzed adults or a plain-film detection estimate.
- [Ikka et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26920558/): 50 fresh-cadaver dissections and, separately, 93 arteriographies in 47 patients; branching variability, not a pooled normal radiographic cohort. Here the paper's abbreviation DSA denotes the dorsal scapular artery, not proof of digital-subtraction acquisition.
- [Carotid collateral study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31429719/): 16 selected unilateral common-carotid occlusions with patent ipsilateral internal/external carotids. DSA/CTA collateral observations do not establish ordinary costocervical flow.
- [Suprascapular aneurysm case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21686592/): one variant on CTA and selective arteriography, not a normal-visibility or variant-frequency study.
- [Andrews et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3304791/): 74 digital-subtraction venograms spanning upper-extremity and mediastinal examinations in 69 selected patients; not 69 normal arms or proof that every named tributary was seen.
- [Dynamic axillary series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17398059/): six selected patients, not a normal dynamic reference for either atlas donor side.
- [Imanishi et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11428774/): radiopaque arterial/venous injection of five fresh cadavers, studying scapular cutaneous networks. Not living venography of this named suprascapular vein or proof of its drainage recipient.
- [Suprascapular-notch Doppler](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm7120491): a different modality and healthy-volunteer cohort; no X-ray visibility inferred.

Unenhanced radiography, contrast arteriography/venography, CTA/MRA/CTV/MRV,
ultrasound and injected cadaver radiography remain distinct. A crossing does
not establish continuity, patency, supply/drainage territory or a procedural
route. Acquired side/projection must be checked independently. No treatment
or acquisition protocol is supplied; Atlas/case/paid-lecture access is separate.

14-topic/Review transition, unrelated teaching, tours and geometry, complete
source-identity mutations and stale/forged refusal before storage. Its
coordinator-only `--working-transition` mode applies before the source commit.

deliver changes; generated website modules must not be hand-edited.

All prose is original factual synthesis. Reading references are not imported
assets or redistribution licences. No source prose, table, figure, scan, mask,
dataset, font, dependency or service is added. Existing model licences and holds
remain. Revision-bound radiologist sign-off is pending; engineering checks do
not establish browser, clinical, privacy, spatial or public-release acceptance.
