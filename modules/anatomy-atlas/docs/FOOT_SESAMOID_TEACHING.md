# Generic foot-sesamoid teaching draft

This shared draft covers the existing side-specific source groups FMA45097 and FMA45098 in nine tabs: Anatomy, Function, CT, MRI, X-ray, Ultrasound, Pathology, Clinical and Quiz. The source inventory identifies generic foot-sesamoid groups; it does not assign their components to medial/tibial or lateral/fibular hallux sesamoids. The first-MTP pair is a conditional teaching example, not an identification of either mesh or a patient shape. The single quiz item tests that distinction.

`content/foot-sesamoid-teaching.ts` contains 352 unique body/bullet words, shared by both selections. No individual reference contributes 200 words of fact-derived prose: the AAOS material supports the first-MTP mechanics and clinical correlation; Nwawka et al. supports the sesamoid/ossicle distinction, variants, CT/MRI and pathology; Chen et al. supports targeted ultrasound and MRI context. The remaining wording expresses source-identity, mesh and diagnostic limits or the original quiz. No figures, scans, tables, meshes, geometry, identifiers or other assets are added; no new asset licence is required. Citation links are references, not imported content.

References consulted:

- [AAOS, *Sesamoiditis and Sesamoid Fracture*](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/sesamoiditis/) — common first-MTP example, mechanical role, symptoms and radiographic bipartition.
- [Nwawka et al., *Sesamoids and accessory ossicles of the foot*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3781258/) — variability and CT/MRI/radiographic distinctions. The [publisher's full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13244-013-0277-1) was also inspected.
- [Chen et al., *High-Resolution Ultrasound of the Forefoot and Common Pathologies*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9322853/) — targeted ultrasound and MRI context and limitations. The PMC full text returned a browser challenge and the publisher page returned a rate limit; the article's indexed primary-source excerpts were inspected. No claim relies on uninspected figures or tables.

These are introductory draft lessons. Revision-bound foot-and-ankle anatomical, MSK radiology and clinical sign-off is required before acceptance, including source-group/component identity and all imaging and clinical wording. No acquired imaging, registration, patient finding, diagnostic rule or treatment recommendation is supplied. The broader Atlas teaching, source, imaging and access goals remain open.
