## Editorial distinctions

The notes separate the ophthalmic and optic nerves, facial sensation and forehead movement, the two oculomotor divisions and their distinct muscle targets, sensory fibres passing through the ciliary ganglion from parasympathetic synapses, and long versus short ciliary pathways. Lacrimal secretomotor traffic is distinguished from V1 sensory origin. Nasal general sensation is not olfaction. The singular long-ciliary source label is not evidence of a complete branch count.

These are typical teaching relationships, not measured source endpoints or verified connections. Selecting a parent does not imply that all its downstream anatomy is included. Explode/cut controls do not model conduction, reflexes or safe surgical/nerve-block routes. No individual clinical signs, diagnostic rules or treatment recommendations are added in this batch.
