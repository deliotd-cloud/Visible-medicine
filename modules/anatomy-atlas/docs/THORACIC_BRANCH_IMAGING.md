## Teaching and modality limits

- Inlet: actual arch/jugular connections, side-specific course, scalene and
  costoclavicular relationships, contrast timing, incomplete central ultrasound
  coverage and positional versus clinically significant compression.
- Coronary arteries: ostia, LAD/circumflex/RCA grooves, source versus projected
  images, motion/blooming and dedicated coronary MRA versus cine/perfusion MRI.
- Cardiac veins: great versus middle cardiac venous course, separate venous
  enhancement and coronary sinus connections, not inferred dominance or flow.
- Chest wall: internal thoracic, superior epigastric and musculophrenic courses,
  layers/Doppler where supported, source gaps and incomplete collateral identity.
- Bronchial/oesophageal: small arterial courses and variable origins on CT;
  distinction from pulmonary arterial supply and venous varices. The explicitly
  variant bronchial source and grouped oesophageal identity remain unchanged.
