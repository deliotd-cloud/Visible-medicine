# Hand arterial X-ray orientation

Six retained source selections gain original X-ray/angiographic draft teaching:
left/right superficial palmar arterial arch, deep palmar arch and princeps
pollicis. Existing exact identities are reused from
`content/hand-arterial-ultrasound-pins.json`; its geometry bytes and ultrasound
topic permissions are unchanged. No new mesh, branch or image is supplied.

## Evidence and scope

- [Braun et al., 1977](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/614070/): radiopaque
  injection/dissection of cadaver hands with multiple X-ray projections;
  projection ambiguity, not routine unenhanced-film arterial visibility.
- [ACRA, 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29127118/): palmar arch angiography
  in cardiac-catheterization patients; patterns in that cohort, not universal
  prevalence, collateral adequacy or a procedural eligibility rule.
- [Rauch et al., 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10520330/): selected clinical
  palm angiograms, with detection differences from anatomical/sonographic work;
  interpretation limit: its abstract does not establish why an individual segment
  is unseen or whether it is anatomically absent or occluded.
- [Ikeda et al., 1988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3418051/): cadaver
  stereoscopic arteriography and variable combinations contributing to thumb
  supply; not routine clinical detection of a named princeps pollicis.

The other 22 retained arterial selections (radialis indicis, metacarpal and
common/proper digital branches) remain pending for X-ray teaching: these sources
do not establish each named branch's modality-specific depiction. Existing
numbering, incomplete bilateral inventory and source grouping are not revised.
Ordinary radiographs, contrast angiograms and injected cadaver preparations are
distinguished. Reference geometry supplies neither a lumen nor measured flow,
patency, patient-space registration, diagnosis or a procedural protocol.

Only original factual prose/code and reading links are included. No publisher
asset, patient image, new dependency, font, texture or fee is introduced.
Clinical/content accuracy, rights/privacy-cleared representative image links and
independent Atlas/case/lecture entitlements remain separate release requirements.
All six worksheets require radiologist review of the exact teaching revision;
no prior decision or approval transfers. Source tests are not browser acceptance.
