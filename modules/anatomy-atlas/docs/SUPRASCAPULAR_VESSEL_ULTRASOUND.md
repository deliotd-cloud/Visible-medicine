## Primary evidence and media rights

Jezierski et al., 2018, *Morphological Aspects in Ultrasound Visualisation of the
Suprascapular Notch Region: A Study Based on a New Four-Step Protocol*,
[DOI 10.3390/jcm7120491](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm7120491), examined 120 healthy
volunteers. The [publisher PDF](https://res.mdpi.com/d_attachment/jcm/jcm-07-00491/article_deploy/jcm-07-00491-v2.pdf)
reports arterial recognition in every **visualised notch**, not guaranteed
recognition in every shoulder. Some notches were obscured by the clavicle; the
vein was less consistently visualised. Vessel identification used flow-spectrum
assessment. The usual ligament relationships are described with variation.

The article declares CC BY 4.0, but this batch imports no figure, PDF, ultrasound
frame, scan, Doppler trace, measurement table, dataset or source prose. It adds
independently written factual teaching and a reading citation only. Existing
model/dependency notices and MIT code terms remain unchanged. No payment,
external API, image-generation service or mandatory licence fee is introduced.

## Deliberate clinical limits

- Non-visualisation does not establish absence; Doppler colour alone does not
  identify an artery or vein. An actual acquisition is required for assessment.
- The static vessel mesh does not supply a sonogram, Doppler measurement or
  validated relationship to a nerve or ligament. It is not a patient registration.
- No prevalence/diameter threshold, diagnostic performance, disease conclusion,
  needle trajectory or nerve-block instruction is provided.
- Atlas, imaging-case and paid-lecture entitlements remain independent; a reading
  citation grants no paid-content access. Didanix Education/light remains the
  intended learner viewer, not the clinical desktop PACS.
- Sign-off must identify these exact source and teaching revisions and check the
  wording against actual learner/Review presentation after website generation.
  Imaging approval remains unavailable without separately validated acquisitions
  and mappings. No private review record is read, migrated or approved.
