# Cricothyroid source-part imaging teaching

29 September 2026. Two original draft lessons fill CT/MRI topics for the four
existing named muscle-part selections: straight right/left FMA46611/FMA46612,
oblique right/left FMA46613/FMA46614. This is one shared concept and eight topic
placements, not eight unique lessons or new anatomy. Whole-body root teaching
and all geometry/bindings remain unchanged. Ultrasound and X-ray stay pending.

## Evidence and limits

- [Pickuth et al. (2000), spiral CT before/after cricothyroid approximation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10971539/): the accessed primary abstract
  describes29 patients, pre/postoperative cricothyroid distance and associated
  pitch changes. It does not validate separate straight/oblique muscle-belly
  segmentation. No surgical recommendations or numerical target are supplied.
- [Chen et al. (2012), laryngeal reconstruction using micro-MRI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21816571/): primary abstract, one excised human
  postmortem larynx at7T, manually reconstructed muscles/cartilages, limited
  cricothyroid-joint definition. This does not demonstrate routine in-vivo belly
  resolution, diagnostic sensitivity, normal thresholds or a patient mapping.
- Existing [TTUHSC El Paso anatomical reference](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/nervous_system/deepneck_tables.html)
  supports the cricoid/thyroid attachment relationship, not the imaging claim.

Only the publicly accessible abstracts were used for the two new study summaries.
They are short original factual teaching notes with citations; no abstract text,
figures, tables, scans, models or supplementary files are redistributed. Article
links do not grant media reuse rights. Existing geometry credits are unchanged;
there are no new dependencies, external runtime requests or mandatory fees.
