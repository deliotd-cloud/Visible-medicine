## Evidence read and rights

- [Casoli et al.,2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14730394/): primary abstract;
 69 upper limbs, distinction between MCA and posterior radial collateral and
 variable MCA origin. This is anatomical evidence, not ultrasound validation.
- [Sun et al.,2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26969598/): indexed primary
 PubMed abstract;15 volunteers and22 cadaveric specimens. It describes radial
 collateral/posterior radial collateral/perforator anatomy. Direct PubMed access
 initially returned only footer content; the indexed primary abstract was read.
- [Coskun et al.,2015](https://www.ajemjournal.com/article/S0735-67571500051-0/fulltext):
 primary case, MRA depiction of aneurysmal inferior thyroid arteries. Main read
 indexed primary text; direct opening returned a cache error. The bounded content
 worker also reported full-text access. Normal MRI claims are not generalized
 from that case.
