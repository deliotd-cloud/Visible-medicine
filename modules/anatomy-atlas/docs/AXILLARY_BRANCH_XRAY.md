# Axillary branches: X-ray and contrast orientation

Eighteen retained root-body selections receive X-ray drafts: bilateral anterior
and posterior circumflex humeral, circumflex scapular, thoracodorsal, subscapular,
thoracoacromial trunk and its pectoral/acromial/deltoid branches. These notes
orient existing donor surfaces; they do not create radiographs, new vessels,
connected branch junctions, flow or registered patient anatomy.

Exact complete source identities come from three existing pin sets:
`shoulder-arm-artery-quiz-pins.json` (eight selected humeral/scapular branches),
`shoulder-arterial-imaging-pins.json` (only the two subscapular selections), and
`thoracoacromial-quiz-pins.json` (eight trunk/branch selections). Their original
sides, files, bundles, source hashes and unvalidated status remain unchanged.
`axillaryBranchXrayLesson` refuses other topics and any complete-record drift.

## Factual reading and study limits

- [Texas Tech artery table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html), [axillary dissection answers](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/musculoskeletal_system/axilla_ans.html): anatomical orientation only; no table, prose or illustrations copied.
- [Hue et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9706673/): 40 injected cadaveric shoulders and 15 radiographic observations, not living unenhanced radiography or a branch-detection rate.
- [Reid and Taylor, 1984](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6201224/): dissection, ink injection and barium radiography in 60 fresh cadavers and 50 fixed specimens. Injected anatomy does not establish donor perfusion or live DSA visibility.
- [Bonczar et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36094609/): 55 patients undergoing neck/thoracic CTA; observed thoracoacromial branching variation is not a template for either donor side or a catheter-angiographic sensitivity estimate.
- [Subscapular–thoracodorsal CTA analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34998174/): 100 adults / 200 arterial systems, including independent branch origins when a common trunk was absent. Keep this cohort separate from injected cadaver and DSA evidence.
- [Pan et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39568746/): six selected refractory-pain cases in different body regions with DCE-MRA and procedural DSA. Not six normal shoulder studies, a routine-visibility estimate or a treatment recommendation.

All prose is original factual synthesis. No source figure, table, prose,
measurement dataset or media is imported or relicensed. Ordinary radiography,
contrast X-ray/DSA, CTA/MRA and injected cadaver studies remain distinct.
Projected crossings and an atlas surface cannot establish connected lumen,
patency, perfusion, nerve geometry or a safe procedural route. Verify acquired
side/projection independently. Atlas/case/paid-lecture access stays independent.

## Verification and delivery

`npm run axillary-branch-xray:test` replays the immutable introduction and exact
baseline; `--working-transition` is coordinator-only before that commit. It
checks eighteen topic/Review transitions, preserved teaching/tours/GLBs, complete
identity mutations and synthetic stale/forged refusals before storage. Normal
teaching, renderer/display binding, learner export and private Review import
pipelines must deliver changes; never hand-edit generated website copies.

Draft teaching requires the radiologist's exact source/revision sign-off.
Tests do not establish browser/GPU, signed-in, clinical, privacy or release
acceptance. No approval migrates; accepted CT/MRI masks, private scan data and
the owner's desktop clinical PACS remain untouched. No public deployment or
new asset, package, font, service or mandatory fee is introduced.
