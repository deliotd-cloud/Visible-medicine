## Evidence and commercial reuse boundary

Original concise factual synthesis and reading links only. No source prose,
publisher image, table, classification, scan or dataset is imported. Publicly
readable articles are not presumed to license their media for commercial use.

- [TTUHSC pelvis/perineum veins](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/veins_pelvis_perineum.html): common-iliac union into the IVC and pelvic visceral/perineal drainage. Copyrighted teaching table; no table or diagram redistributed.
- [Locating Femoral Vein by Anatomic Landmarks: A Cadaveric Study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12032564/): the femoral-to-external-iliac naming transition at the inguinal ligament. No cadaver image, measurements or procedural directions reused.
- [Shin et al., MDCT iliac variation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24874031/), DOI10.1007/s00276-014-1316-4: CT variation in internal-iliac drainage, interiliac communications and caval connections. No published classification table, figures or prevalence values copied; these data do not classify the atlas donor.
- [Ruehm et al., pelvic/lower-extremity MRV](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10796919/), DOI10.1148/radiology.215.2.r00ap27421: dedicated contrast MRV feasibility in patients without acute DVT. Original abstract checked; no routine-MRI equivalence, acute-thrombus exclusion or current protocol recommendation. No publisher media or acquisition parameters imported.
- [Assi et al., iliac ultrasound and flow modelling](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37353157/), DOI10.1016/j.jvsv.2023.05.017: acquired common/external iliac ultrasound measurements combined with patient-specific CT/US modelling. Proof-of-concept one subject/one control; not a validated atlas diagnostic or a measurement of this donor. Internal-iliac flows were calibrated, so this is not cited as direct ultrasound measurement of every internal tributary.
- [Bhatt et al., transvaginal duplex versus venography](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40537052/), DOI10.1177/02683555251353550: women with suspected pelvic vein incompetence, including internal iliac reflux. Position/criterion-dependent results are not an examination route or diagnostic threshold for the male-donor atlas. No numerical thresholds or publisher figures reused.
