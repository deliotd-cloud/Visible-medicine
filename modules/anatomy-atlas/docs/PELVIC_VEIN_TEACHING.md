# Pelvic-vein teaching — 12 September 2026

Extends the ten admitted root-body pelvic vein selections, not the separate
female-pelvic specimen. Use Pelvis / Whole body → Study → Pelvic venous
tributaries, select a vessel and open its existing Anatomy, Clinical or Imaging
tabs. No new panel, geometry, course, scan or permanent control is added.

Six groups distinguish iliolumbar, superior/inferior gluteal, obturator, internal
pudendal and lateral sacral teaching. Existing Anatomy, Quiz and the three
already-authored Function notes are preserved. Seven pending Function notes
now have drafts; all ten gain Clinical and Pathology; nine gain CT; five gain
MRI and Ultrasound. These 46 placements reuse 18 topic texts, not 46 independent
full lessons. X-ray remains pending, as do MRI/US for iliolumbar, obturator and
lateral sacral selections and CT for lateral sacral.

Teaching covers variable lumbar venous drainage, obturator/external-iliac
communications, gluteal and perineal venous disease, presacral relationships,
contrast-tracing and the difference between an anatomical map and dynamic
haemodynamic assessment. Small-branch visibility is not inferred from studies
of larger pelvic veins. Female-pelvic ultrasound/MRV or cadaveric evidence is
explicitly not transferred as validated performance or distances to this male
donor. No management protocol, numeric diagnostic cutoff, injury grade,
operative distance or classification table is reproduced.

The complete source-record check still precedes all teaching resolution.
Changed/foreign sources cannot acquire these notes by sharing a name/FMA ID.
The two left-sided held definitions, original meshes, catalogues, source
transforms and study recipes remain unchanged. Regression evidence compares
all non-target teaching and every old authored target topic with the saved
baseline, checks current content schema and source-mutation rejection, and
exercises rendered content without claiming WebGL/device acceptance.

Passed: 9,737 unchanged-topic comparisons, 90 actual teaching-panel renders,
720 altered-source/topic rejections and all 1,087 body schema records. The
existing pelvic geometry/study regression, current source-hold check, cranial
component regression, TypeScript and production build also pass. The generated
coverage inventory matches current source. These checks do not establish
anatomical accuracy, WebGL/device acceptance or clinical approval.

The inventory also corrects a pre-existing reporting mismatch: 29 deliberately
unnamed cranial-artery source partitions have no independent lesson identity.
It now explicitly records these as geometry-only with all topics pending,
instead of asserting that every nested source part has teaching. The other
75 nested representations retain their authored Anatomy drafts. Missing
bindings in other studies still fail the audit; no parent lesson, named
arterial segment or clinical identity has been invented for these pieces.

Radiologist review must check factual wording, laterality, source extents,
variants, clinical relevance and modality limitations at the exact revision.
