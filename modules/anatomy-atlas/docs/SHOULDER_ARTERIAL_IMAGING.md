## Educational distinctions

- Joint MRI is not dedicated arterial MRA; source acquisitions establish signal,
  branch identity, field of view and extent, not the coloured 3D surface.
- Grey-scale, colour localisation and spectral Doppler are distinct acquired
  observations. No flow direction, velocity, resistance index or patency is
  inferred from a mesh, and a non-visualised segment is not declared absent.
- The anterior-circumflex Doppler example concerns a selected rotator-cuff study;
  it supplies no diagnostic threshold for night pain.
- The thoracodorsal example concerns a small perforator-flap planning series;
  this donor surface is not a skin-perforator map or reconstructive suitability
  assessment. Cadaveric branching context is not a validated probe window.
- Return separation to zero before spatial comparison. Explode, clipping and
  camera sweeps do not create an acquisition plane or patient transform.

## Source and commercial-use boundary

Only original bounded factual teaching and citation links are authored. No
external diagram, photograph, ultrasound example, waveform, table, publisher
wording or protocol is imported. A link or abstract does not license its figures.
Existing authored MIT code and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 credit/change notices remain;
no asset, texture, font, dependency, API cost or mandatory fee is added.

- [TTUHSC El Paso upper-limb arterial anatomy](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/arteries_upperlimb.html): typical branching context, not scan visibility.
- [Rowsell et al., cadaveric subscapular–thoracodorsal anatomy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6498399/): cadaveric observations, not patient/perforator correspondence.
- [Terabayashi et al., anterior-circumflex Doppler study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25069807/): selected haemodynamic assessment, not a diagnostic rule.
- [Tashiro et al., colour-Doppler flap-planning series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26732295/): selected perforator work, not general visibility of all branches.
- [ACR/RSNA vascular ultrasound](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/vascularus): modality context, not an authored acquisition protocol.
- [ACR/RSNA MR angiography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiomr): dedicated acquisition context, not guaranteed visibility.
