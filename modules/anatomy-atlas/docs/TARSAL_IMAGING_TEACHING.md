# Ankle and midfoot bone imaging teaching

In **Ankle & foot** or **Whole body**, select a talus, calcaneus, navicular, cuboid or cuneiform bone. Expand the existing learning panel and choose **Imaging → X-ray, CT or MRI**. Fourteen exact source selections now receive 42 introductory draft sections, organised into five shared concepts with seven bone-specific orientation notes. The existing dissection, bone exposure studies, side filters, labels and separation controls remain unchanged; no new toolbar or scroll area.

The sections distinguish bone localisation, projection/cross-sectional interpretation and the limits of an outer surface. They do not provide a complete imaging curriculum, pathological mesh, patient image, measured loading state, normality judgement or clinical approval. Ultrasound remains pending for these bones. Earlier Anatomy/Function/Clinical/Pathology/Quiz content is retained.

## Required before clinical publication

1. Musculoskeletal radiologist/anatomist review of every bone binding, anatomical relation, projection term, clinical caveat and source currency.
2. Source geometry review for individual landmarks, articular contours, segmentation and alignment. No cartilage/ligament or loaded joint measurement is established here.
3. Real browser, mobile/touch, keyboard and enlarged-text acceptance. This background pass performed code and rendered-component checks only and left the browser untouched.
4. Separate consent/privacy/licensing, normality and registration validation for future actual scans, plus independent resource-entitlement checks.

This completes the introductory tarsal imaging pass, not all ankle/foot anatomy or imaging. Continue substantial wider-body anatomy/function work rather than repeat this pass or expand low-priority oral detail. Saving/deployment evidence belongs in the dated checkpoint.
