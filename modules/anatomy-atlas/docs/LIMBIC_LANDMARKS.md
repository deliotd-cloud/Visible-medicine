## Important source limits

- The lamina definition contains two individually manifold halves touching at one source-coordinate vertex. The combined surface is not a manifold continuous sheet. No bridge, opening or repair is generated.
- The septum-of-telencephalon definition has two components. Its broad name does not establish separately segmented septal nuclei, septum verum or complete septum-pellucidum anatomy.
- The left stria medullaris extends slightly across source zero-X (minimum −0.664756 mm); most source vertices are positive-X. Coordinate origin is not proof of an individual's midsagittal plane. Both source names and positions remain unchanged and require review. No automatic side-label swap, reflection or re-centring is performed.
- The complete stria terminalis source (FMA61974) is withheld: FJ1825 contains six components, four duplicate faces and non-manifold topology. The compound has seven components. Both files and their original notices remain offline; the shared hold policy rejects subset/alias admission, including a shortcut through the left-labelled component.
- No fibres, synapses, nuclear boundaries, complete limbic circuit, tractography or registered patient images are inferred.
