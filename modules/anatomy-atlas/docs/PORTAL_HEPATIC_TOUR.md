# Portal inflow and hepatic venous outflow

The Abdomen and Whole body Guided learning libraries offer an eight-stop draft
comparison: superior mesenteric vein, splenic vein, inferior mesenteric vein,
hepatic portal vein, right/middle/left hepatic veins, and inferior vena cava.
The existing liver, corrected pancreas and spleen provide faded landmarks.

Use Guided learning, choose **Portal inflow and hepatic venous outflow**, then
press Start. Playback is explicit, with existing pause, step navigation, quick
check, camera and reduced-motion controls. Each stop lasts14seconds and retains
the established1.8second quintic orbit transition. Portal-pair and hepatic-trio
frames share exact catalogue-derived bounds; surfaces remain assembled, without
fabricated bridges or new explode/dissection planes.

## Scope and evidence

This compares two anatomical systems, not one continuous blood-flow route. Liver
microcirculation, intrahepatic portal branches, complete tributaries/accessory
veins and atrial connections are not supplied. Parent organ surfaces are not
validated compartments or perfusion territories. Mesh gaps do not diagnose an
obstruction, and adjacency does not prove a confluence or collecting trunk.

Original concise captions refer to the [TTUHSC vein anatomy table](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/veins_abdomen.html),
the primary [916-case portal confluence CT study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21986867/)
and the primary [200-subject hepatic vein CT study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21976007/).
The IMV and hepatic collecting patterns vary; cohort findings do not validate
the donor surfaces or establish a universal anatomy, imaging measurement,
clinical diagnostic rule, procedural route or patient-space registration.
No publisher prose, image, diagram, model or dataset is imported. Original
code/captions use the project MIT licence; existing model attribution/licences
remain intact. No payment, dependency, font or new asset is required.
