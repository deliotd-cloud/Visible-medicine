# Hip and pelvic attachment studies

The compact attachment panel now covers the remaining 17 thigh-curriculum
concepts (34 sided selections) plus both coccygeus selections. Together with
the previous thigh studies, all 54 current thigh-curriculum selections have
attachment controls. This is not complete hip or pelvic-floor anatomy.

Select a covered muscle in Thigh, Pelvis, Spine or Whole body according to its
source membership, open Muscle attachment relationships and choose Show.
Five existing bones and two iliotibial-tract selections are the mapped partners.
The tract remains connective tissue, not bone. Show enables the required system;
partner buttons restore/select that structure. Missing regional partners have
the existing side-preserving whole-body continuation. No new toolbar or pop-out.

## Anatomical limits

- Tensor fasciae latae selects the iliotibial tract, not a direct muscle-to-tibia
  tendon. Gluteus maximus has separate femoral and fascial partners; its lumbar
  fascia, sacrotuberous ligament and coccygeal contributions are not mapped.
- Gemelli retain their shared obturator-internus tendon pathway. No separately
  segmented tendon or donor-specific attachment coordinates are claimed.
- Obturator externus reaches the trochanteric fossa; internus reaches the medial
  greater trochanter above it. Their membrane origins are described but not
  separately selectable. The hip bone represents the bony margin only.
- Adductor magnus retains its portion distinctions; minimus remains a variably
  separate portion. Source labels do not establish universal boundaries.
- Gluteal facets and iliopsoas apparatus are teaching relationships, not mapped
  functional compartments or nerve territories.
- Psoas-major vertebral levels/discs remain unassigned. Spine offers its
  whole-body continuation for the femoral partner; whole body does not resolve
  the missing origin map.
- Coccygeus links hip bone and sacrum with explicit unmapped coccyx/ligament
  limits. FMA19728 remains an uncertain category, not an assigned muscle;
  no pelvic-floor root-level exam fact or continence simulation is added.

Original Anatomy/Function topics and source geometry are unchanged. The panel
reuses brief factual curriculum drafts with mapping limits. Reading references
include [TTUHSC lower limb](https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/anatomytables/muscles_lowerlimb.html),
[iliopsoas](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK531508/),
[hip](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK526019/) and
[pelvic attachments](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482258/).
These are factual reading links, not licences to redistribute publisher prose,
figures, tables or scans. No new asset, dependency or paid service is added.
Existing BodyParts3D commercial-compatible attribution remains unchanged.

## Verification and review

Run `npm run hip-attachments:test`. Seven original bundles and 43 identities
are pinned. Tests independently verify endpoints and laterality, regional and
whole-body visibility, actual panel/button/parent handlers, mixed tissue systems,
source-corruption rejection, exam guards and Undo/Redo. The report records exact
counts; existing attachment, content and review suites also remain required.

These are draft relationships. Radiologist acceptance must inspect actual source
surfaces, hip landmarks, muscle portions, shared-tendon/fascial pathways, absent
membranes/coccyx and all partial maps. Whole structures are not measured donor
footprints. Renderer revisions require re-review, not inherited approval.
Hands-on browser/device acceptance is separate from automated tests.

Show resets separation, inspection and camera. Dissection Undo restores layers
and removals, not camera or system switches. No scan, CT-head mask, clinical PACS,
Didanix Education/light integration or independent entitlement is changed.
GitHub/D recovery and hosted rollout are recorded separately in the checkpoint.
