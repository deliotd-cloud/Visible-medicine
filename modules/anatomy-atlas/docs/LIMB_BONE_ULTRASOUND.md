These are linked primary educational references. ESSR's technical documents are
not a validated sensitivity study or a product-media licence. RadiologyInfo
retains copyright and encourages linking rather than copying. We incorporate no
publisher text, PDF, figure, photograph, video or scan. Notes are brief original
factual summaries, with app-specific limitations. Existing model attribution is
unchanged; no font, package, fee-bearing service or dependency is introduced.
