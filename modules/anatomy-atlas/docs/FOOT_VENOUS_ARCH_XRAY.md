## Evidence and commercial scope

[Engelke et al., RadioGraphics 2001; DOI 10.1148/radiographics.21.5.g01se281239](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/abs/10.1148/radiographics.21.5.g01se281239)
provides the arch-specific depiction example in its public Figure 1a caption.
Its selected critical-ischaemia population is not evidence of routine visibility
on normal plain films. [Lengua et al., 1988; PMID 3199035](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3199035/)
supports the distinction between contrast phlebography and an ordinary radiograph.
Only public bibliographic/abstract/caption information was read; no full-text
purchase, image, publisher prose or protocol was imported. Reading links are not
commercial redistribution licences for those articles or their figures.
New original code and teaching retain the project's MIT terms; existing model
credits and BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 terms are unchanged.
