# Hepatic arterial angiographic orientation drafts

The common hepatic artery, hepatic artery proper and left gastric artery now
have original X-ray orientation drafts in Abdomen and Whole body. They use the
existing exact abdominal arterial pins and leave every other topic, geometry,
source label and validation state unchanged. These are anatomical references,
not acquired images, procedural instructions or patient-space correspondence.

## Evidence and scope

[Covey et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12147854/),
DOI 10.1148/radiol.2242011283, reports a single-institution 600-patient digital
subtraction angiography series. Its public publisher Figure 1 caption identifies
the usual common-to-proper hepatic sequence around the gastroduodenal origin;
the abstract records common and proper hepatic origin variants, and Figures 3
and 5 identify replaced and accessory left hepatic origins from the left gastric
artery. Those patterns do not establish this source donor's complete supply or
population-wide visibility. The captions are evidence for factual orientation,
not assets imported into the product.

[Coşkun et al., 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15848625/),
DOI 10.1016/j.transproceed.2005.01.019, compared donor hepatic CTA with conventional
angiography. Of 48 candidates, 28 underwent both; one variant branch was not
identified on CT. The study also reported an accessory hepatic branch from the
left gastric artery. These observations retain their donor-cohort scope and do
not make the modalities interchangeable or prove universal branch depiction.

The [UAMS abdominal artery table](https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/artery-tables/arteries-of-the-abdomen/)
supports usual origins and gastric relationships. The
[ACR/RSNA catheter-angiography page](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/angiocath)
distinguishes catheter X-ray, CT and MRI examinations. Original prose separates
plain radiography, CTA, MRA and catheter/DSA; no examination is delivered by a
static coloured vessel surface.

## Source identity and review

Existing FMA14771 retains its catalogue `midline` laterality, FMA14772 retains
`unspecified`, and FMA14768 retains `left`. Those source values are not patient
side markers and are not silently corrected. Exact complete source signatures
include geometry hashes, node, bounds, metadata, provenance and validation.
Changed or incomplete signatures refuse the lesson.

Radiologist review must bind the actual teaching revision and evaluate the
usual-versus-variant wording, replaced/accessory distinction, source completeness
and modality limits. No source is newly approved. Keep Atlas, imaging-case and
paid-lecture access independent. No registration, lumen, perfusion or flow claim
follows from source surfaces, explode offsets, colours or passing tests.

## Commercial scope

Only independently written factual prose and reference links are included.
No publication text, caption, figure, table, patient image, scan or model is
copied or adapted. Reading access does not confer media-reuse rights. Existing
licensed geometry and credits remain unchanged; no dependency, asset, service
subscription or mandatory fee is added. Clinical and public-release clearance
remain separate from technical verification.
