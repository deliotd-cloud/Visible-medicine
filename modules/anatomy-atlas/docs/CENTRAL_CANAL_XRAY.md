# Central canal: X-ray limitation teaching

One original draft for the exact root-body selection
`vm:anatomy:body:spine:midline:space:central-canal-of-spinal-cord` (FMA78497).
It appears in the existing X-ray tab, with no new controls or geometry.

The lesson distinguishes the intramedullary central canal, the bony vertebral
canal and the subarachnoid contrast column of myelography. It does not claim
direct routine radiographic visibility, patency, a diagnosis, measured CSF flow
or patient-space registration. The assembled reference is anatomical context,
not a patient image. Exact-revision radiologist review remains required.

The unchanged `central-canal-imaging-pins.json` binds the full selected identity,
source hashes, bundle and representation. This extension does not alter its
historical CT/MRI/ultrasound lessons, pins or transition record. The new helper
accepts only X-ray and the complete pinned structure; near matches fail closed.

Original summaries cite [RadiologyInfo myelography](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/myelography)
and [spine MRI](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/spinemr), read 15 October 2026.
Links are factual reading references, not permission to import publisher text,
figures, pixels or scans. No third-party visual, font, dataset or dependency is
added; existing anatomy licences and holds remain unchanged.

Run `npm run central-canal-xray:test` for exact bindings, historical retention,
all-lesson/Review-packet scope and stale/forged Review rejection checks. These
are source/engineering checks, not clinical or acquired-image acceptance.
Atlas, imaging-case and paid-lecture entitlements remain independent.
