# Forearm muscle imaging drafts

13 September 2026. Original CT, MRI, ultrasound and X-ray orientation now
fills 168 previously pending topic placements for 42 existing bilateral
BodyParts3D selections: 21 source-labelled muscle/head concepts. This is
introductory imaging teaching, not comprehensive clinical completion.

## Content and use

Open `/regions/forearm`, select a muscle and use the existing imaging tabs.
The same source-bound notes appear in the root-body explorer. No permanent
control or additional panel is introduced. The notes distinguish individual
muscle heads, distal targets, wrist tendon compartments, relevant nerve
relationships and source-specific limitations. Grouped finger muscles are
not presented as individually validated tendon slips; source-labelled heads
are not silently promoted to complete muscles.

Every placement is `draft` and retains **No imaging study loaded**. CT and
X-ray landmarks do not establish muscle/tendon integrity. MRI surfaces have
no patient signal or quantitative measurements. Ultrasound descriptions do
not provide acquired echotexture, dynamic findings or intervention guidance.
Return separation to zero when comparing spatial relationships.

Existing Anatomy, Function, Pathology, Clinical and Quiz teaching is preserved.
This root-body content is not automatically copied into the main website's
separate shoulder, female-pelvis or UM lower-limb runtime modules.
