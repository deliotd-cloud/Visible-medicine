Whole body and regional library

The expanded library contains 924 selectable entries: 203 skeletal, 369 muscular, 72 organ (including 28 teeth), 54 nervous-system, 146 vascular and 80 connective-tissue entries. These are source representation counts, not a claim of complete anatomical coverage. Four additional candidate entries were withheld after left/right position discrepancies were detected; they were not automatically relabelled. Source geometry was converted from OBJ to GLB, grouped by anatomical identity, smoothed through normal recalculation, and partitioned into 73 regional/system bundles. One common transformation is used for the entire body library. Colours were adapted for illustration. Source hashes, exact bindings, exclusions, regional groupings and transformations are recorded in the full-body catalogue. Both official IS-A and PART-OF archive distributions were used under the same CC BY 4.0 grant.

Nervous coverage is limited to brain, selected cranial/orbital nerves and a central-canal representation. The source uses the same file for conflicting spinal-cord/central-canal identities; this viewer uses the narrower central-canal identity and does not represent it as a complete spinal cord. Brachial and lumbosacral plexuses and limb peripheral nerves are not included. These gaps were not filled with invented geometry.

Guided regional dissection

The September 2026 extension adds 60 named muscle components from the same official source archives. Original visibility recipes now provide 102 guided stages and 84 focused views, with ghosting, removal/restoration, source-based labels and illustrative contours/hatching. No mesh was reshaped into missing anatomy. Hatching is decorative tonal treatment, not measured muscle-fibre direction. These are educational exposure views, not operative approaches or clinically validated dissection sequences. No new paid library, font, texture or anatomy service is required.

Source recovery

A further 159 source concepts were recovered on 6 September 2026: 117 vessel entries, 25 connective-tissue entries, 12 organ entries, 3 sternal parts and 2 hand lumbrical groups. Selected vessel and rectal components were separated from the heart, liver and large-intestine display aggregates to avoid drawing them twice. Their common source coordinates were preserved. Vascular coverage is an incomplete segment subset, not a validated continuous tree. No AI-generated anatomical mesh was admitted. All anatomy remains unvalidated; source identity and automated geometry checks do not replace independent anatomical review.

Dedicated shoulder viewer

Eleven source meshes were selected, converted from OBJ to GLB and transformed together into the shoulder viewer coordinate system. Surface normals were recalculated. Three deltoid parts are selected as a single muscle. The upper arm is cropped for display; explode mode intentionally separates structures. Colours, fine illustrative hatching and outlines were added. Hatching does not represent measured muscle fibres.

The biceps mesh includes both muscle and its proximal tendon; the tendon is not independently segmented. This model represents source anatomy, not an individual patient, and is not independently clinically validated. No endorsement by the source institution is implied.

Further gap recovery

62 further version 4.0 source entries add 22 whole-disc surfaces, four hand interosseous sets, four interosseous membranes, two calcaneal tendons, two trochlear nerves, ten organ entries and eighteen head/neck connective entries. Eight separate GLB bundles preserve existing meshes. Disc interiors, complete nerve routes and individually numbered interossei are not supplied. Pelvic-floor and optic-nerve alternatives remain held for anatomical review. The older v3 archive was researched but not included because its body geometry differs; no v3 share-alike material or newly generated anatomy is distributed. All new assets remain CC BY 4.0 with the same required DBCLS credit.

Latest source detail

Subsequent version-4 additions include 36 vessel/organ/orbital entries, 22 deep-brain entries, eleven connective/deep-spinal entries and 32 dental/orbital entries. The last subset contains 28 individual secondary teeth (without third molars), two common tendinous rings and two superior-oblique trochleae in two additional bundles. All earlier geometry and identities remain unchanged. The same CC BY 4.0 grant, DBCLS attribution, common transform, normal recalculation, GLB conversion and authored display colours apply. No clinical tooth numbering, internal dental tissues, copied atlas illustrations or validated nerve passages/attachments are included. Short original draft teaching notes do not imply clinical approval. No source institution endorsement is implied.
