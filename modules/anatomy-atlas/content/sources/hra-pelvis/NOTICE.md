Visible Medicine modifications: selected source surfaces; repacked accessor storage; positive uniform position scale of 10 with Float32 storage; source-local IDs and display names; educational material colours. Original triangle order, normals and other attributes are preserved. Source ancestors have identity transforms. There is no mirroring, fitting, smoothing, welding, hole filling or generated anatomy. Production delivery uses byte-exact meshopt compression; transport hashes differ from the canonical GLB.

Six original groups are withheld: left/right round ligaments (source label/side conflict), abdominal ostium and cornua groups (identity/extent uncertainty), and anterior/posterior uterine wall alternatives (overlap, including 234 shared triangles; one duplicate face in the anterior alternative). They remain available in the retained source audit, not the displayed model. This is not approval of the remaining anatomy.

This separate source assembly is not registered to the main atlas or patient CT/MRI. Open cut boundaries and multiple source shells remain. Not a complete female body, complete organ wall/lumen, pelvic floor, nerve network or operative plane. Clinical and device validation are pending.
