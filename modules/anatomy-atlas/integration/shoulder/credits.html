This module contains eleven version-4 source meshes grouped into nine right-shoulder selections. It is not a complete shoulder or whole-body dataset. The biceps long head is a muscle selection, not an independently segmented tendon.

Adaptations: subset extraction, common axis/centre transform and scale, vertex welding, smooth normals, GLB encoding, display cropping, material colours, contours and illustrative hatching. Hatching does not measure fibre direction. Exploded positions are non-anatomical teaching arrangements. Source geometry is unchanged by website integration.

Source institution endorsement is not implied. Anatomical boundaries, teaching and clinical suitability require radiologist review. Source anatomy is not a registered patient model. No patient study is included.
