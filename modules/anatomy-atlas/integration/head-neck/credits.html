The module reuses version-4 source surfaces, their existing coordinate transforms and documented display corrections. Source files were converted to GLB, separated or grouped by identity, normal-smoothed where recorded, and recoloured. Diagrammatic shading is not measured muscle-fibre direction. Exploded positions are teaching layouts, not anatomical positions.

Head/neck and thorax share one delivery without merging their source identities. Nested eye, brain, ventricular, visual-pathway, cricothyroid and arterial studies retain their provenance. The thorax adds the existing cardiac chamber spaces, great-vessel context and lung branch groups with airway context. These are source groups, not complete vascular networks or validated named clinical segments. Source counts do not establish complete anatomy, continuity or patient registration.

Abdomen retains the version-4 hepatic/biliary, pancreatic and renal studies with their source relationship context. The two specimens below open separately; neither is registered to the version-4 body or to patient imaging.
