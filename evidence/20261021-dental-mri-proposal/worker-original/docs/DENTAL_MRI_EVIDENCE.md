# Dental MRI teaching draft — evidence and limits

Scope: four shared methods orientations for the 28 exact secondary-tooth FMA IDs in `lib/head-detail.ts` at frozen source `485df97c2530e8e81a8d39a5ee61073ce76d93db`. This is editorial content only. The selected meshes show whole exteriors; they are not scans or validated internal dental anatomy. Main integration must bind exact structure identities and source revision. No name or FMA-only fallback is authorized.

Primary study checks (original paraphrase, no copied figures or assets):

- [Hövener et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22707436/): in-vivo 3 T turbo spin echo gave soft-tissue contrast but no solid-tooth signal in that protocol. Hard-component depiction with UTE/ZTE was demonstrated on extracted human/equine teeth at 7 T/9.4 T. Those high-field ex-vivo results are not routine-patient equivalence.
- [Bracher et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23420857/): 39 patients completed conventional and dedicated UTE dental MRI in a caries investigation. Filling artifacts prevented evaluation of some teeth. The study does not validate a universal replacement test, a diagnosis here, or reliable separation of every enamel and dentine boundary.
- [Zaike et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40301177/): 21 healthy volunteers had the left mandibular first-molar region examined with a 3 T microscopy coil. PDW, T1W and T2W performance was compared under specified conditions; these results cannot be transferred to every tooth, side, acquisition or the selected mesh.

The module makes no claim of acquired images, image registration, scan matching, internal segmentation, dental numbering, third molars, treatment guidance or clinical review. Radiologist sign-off remains revision-bound and pending. No private data, figures or downloaded scans were used.
