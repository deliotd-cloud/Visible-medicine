# Visible Medicine logo review

This remains a review-stage identity exercise. Nothing on the live header, splash screen, favicon or social card has been replaced.

## Approved direction

The approved matched system is stored without reinterpretation:

- `public/brand/review/visible-medicine-light-approved.png`
- `public/brand/review/visible-medicine-dark-approved.png`

The endorsement remains the standard typographic phrase `by Elivion`. The separate Elivion emblem must not replace the initial letter in this lockup.

## Correction recorded

An earlier hand-built SVG reconstruction changed the proportions and visual character of the approved artwork. It was rejected and removed. No simplified Elivion mark should be invented from the raster reference.

## Production boundary

The `/brand-preview` route displays only the exact review images and is marked `noindex`. A final vector master should be commissioned or traced from a locked source after the following are approved:

1. scan-frame VM geometry;
2. wordmark typeface and spacing;
3. light and dark palette values;
4. location and scale of `by Elivion`;
5. final production sizing for the standard typographic `by Elivion` endorsement.
