# Ventricular guided learning and optional CSF movement

Local website import of Atlas source `0e988a782fb3e6f71bb9220b53aebeedbadd30f9`.
The normal exact-source generator produces all four Atlas runtimes and the
protected Clinical Review graph. No generated module is hand edited.

In Head & neck, choose the brain's ventricular study. Guided learning offers
five source-bound stops. The collapsed **CSF motion · schematic** panel has
explicit Start/Pause controls; motion is off initially. The full-body guided
library also exposes this ventricular walkthrough without a pop-out window.

The illustration uses 160 non-selectable cyan markers, forty inside each of
the four existing ventricular spaces. A four-second reversible cycle and its
axis, amplitudes and density are arbitrary display choices—not measured
physiology, net circulation, CFD, pressure, obstruction or clearance.
Unsegmented foramina, cerebral aqueduct, outlets and subarachnoid spaces are
not animated. No CT/MRI registration or acquired scan is connected.

Reduced-motion, hidden-page, not-ready, cut, exploded and exam states suppress
playback. Ending a guided sequence restores the previous manual controls/view.
Long reading remains inside the sidebar; explanations are closed by default.

All 108 nested source packets remain unchanged. Four ventricular teaching
contexts gain the walkthrough and CSF metadata; 104 other teaching contexts
retain their exact prior material. The new unchecked display/teaching
`csf-motion` items and teaching `guided-learning` item require the owner's
revision-bound radiologist review. CSF is not an extra teaching tab: each
ventricular context has eight earlier topics plus guided learning.

Code/captions are original project MIT material. Derived marker coordinates
retain existing BodyParts3D CC BY 4.0 credit. Primary references are linked,
not imported figures, scans or flow data. No model, dependency, font, texture,
mandatory fee, patient data, access grant or desktop PACS change is added.
