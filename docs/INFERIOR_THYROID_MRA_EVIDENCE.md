## Evidence checked on 20 October 2026

[Kuşkonmaz and Yıldız](https://doi.org/10.7727/wimj.2015.288) describe an unusual
right-sided arterial course through thyroid tissue on MRA. The publisher
[case manuscript](https://www.mona.uwi.edu/fms/wimj/system/files/article_pdfs/kuskonmaz_et_al-risky_thyroid_biopsy.pdf),
page 2 and page 7 caption, supports that specific observation. Acquisition
parameters and a normal-vessel detection rate are not supplied there.

[Coşkun et al., 2015](https://www.ajemjournal.com/article/S0735-67571500051-0/fulltext)
describe proximal and distal left inferior thyroid aneurysms on neck MRA. Its
case paragraph and figure 4 caption support the abnormal-vessel example. The
discussion also reports an earlier examination that did not establish an
aneurysm's origin. Neither source validates visibility across routine structural
MRI sequences, a normal reference-vessel shape, or a patient-to-Atlas transform.
