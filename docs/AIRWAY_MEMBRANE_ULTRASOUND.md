# Airway membrane ultrasound orientation

Three existing head/neck selections receive two shared original draft lessons:
the median cricothyroid ligament (FMA55237), and the right/left thyrohyoid
membranes (FMA55133/FMA55134). Their complete source identities are pinned in
`content/airway-membrane-ultrasound-pins.json`; altered source, geometry, side or
review status receives no lesson from this addition. No anatomy is added.

Evidence checked on 1 November 2026:

- [ASRA airway sonoanatomy](https://asra.com/the-asra-family/trainees/trainee-videos/videos/asra-news/2021/08/02/pocus-spotlight-airway): anatomical orientation and imaging landmarks. This is society educational material.
- [Stopar-Pintaric et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26297210/): primary volunteer/cadaver study; the available abstract supports membrane identification, not independently visible right/left model halves. Only its anatomical observation informs this draft.

The wording distinguishes a membrane region from its model surface and from
air-interface echoes. It supplies no procedure, scan, patient transform, clinical
approval or permission to reuse publisher figures. The median/side-specific
surface correspondence remains unvalidated. Other ligaments, laryngeal nerves
and unsupported ultrasound entries retain their prior status.

All text is original factual synthesis with reading links. No image, figure,
table, article text, new font, model, dependency or subscription is imported.
Existing source-mesh notices remain unchanged. Radiologist review must cover the
exact wording, source extent and distinction between a shared imaging landmark
and an independently resolved selected structure.

Regenerate using `node scripts/regional-teaching-snapshots.mjs`; verify with
`node --test scripts/test-airway-membrane-ultrasound.mjs`. Full runtime, website
delivery and clinical review remain separate release requirements.
