# Hippocampal clinical teaching draft

The existing left and right hippocampal lessons now appear in the folded clinical
panels of the native CT, MRI and linked 3D Atlas. The original memory-network,
pathology and imaging summaries are reused, not reauthored as a second source.
Each panel includes modality cautions, an unscored reflection and reference links.
No new anatomy asset, scan processing or learner toolbar is introduced.

## Review the teaching

In Clinical Review, open Head learning and choose Hippocampal clinical
correlations in the structure and evidence picker. The two notes link to the
exact existing CT, MRI and 3D targets. Choose Hippocampal clinical corrections,
or open Private corrections & saved history inside the clinical section, to
record private draft notes or correction requests. The form is offered only
when the head manifest and complete clinical packet verify.

Saved history remains private to the authorized account and institution, separate
from all earlier anatomy and clinical packets. Concurrent saves cannot overwrite
an earlier version. If the source or session changes, keep your edits and reload
before saving. Do not include patient data. Download working copy preserves the
form and its revisions locally; confirm the file saved and keep it private.
The download is not a confirmed server save or automatic import.

These corrections do not modify teaching, masks or bindings and do not grant
clinical or publication approval.

## Clinical validation

Verify clinical wording and usefulness, both native masks and their laterality,
the original source lesson and the linked 3D targets. The model comprises coarse
hippocampal surfaces, not separately validated subfields, microscopic anatomy or
normative volumetry. The CT, MRI and reference model are separate subjects;
the links compare anatomy semantically, not in registered patient space.

Retain the distinction between the hippocampus as part of a memory network and
an individual memory assessment. The pathology discussion is teaching context,
not evidence of sclerosis or epileptogenicity in the displayed subject. The
MRI lesson cites the 2019 ILAE consensus; it does not establish that the teaching
study acquired that protocol. A reformat cannot supply an unacquired sequence
or contrast. Earlier anatomy reviews and automated checks do not approve this
clinical packet.
