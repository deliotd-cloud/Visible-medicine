# Hepatic vascular X-ray orientation drafts

Two original reading notes are proposed for four existing nested selections:

| Concept | Existing FMA selections | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hepatic-arterial | FMA14778, FMA14779 | Contrast angiography orientation |
| hepatic-portal | FMA15414, FMA15415 | Contrast portography orientation |

The notes use [Choi et al. (2021)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8415139/)
and [Salei et al. (2022), Anatomy/Figure 1](https://pubs.rsna.org/radiology/doi/10.1148/rg.220020).
Their precise reference keys are registered in `nested-teaching.ts` and each
paragraph is attached explicitly through `hepatic-teaching.ts`. No figures,
classification table, patient material or publisher prose is redistributed.
Citation links are not image-reuse licences. Existing authored-text and model
licensing remains unchanged; no dependency or fee is added.

The source groups retain their existing anatomical identities. The middle
hepatic venous tributary, biliary group, source-file fragments, root-body lessons
and all other modalities are outside this change. This is neither a complete
vascular tree nor a new scan correspondence. It does not give a catheter route,
pressure measurement, perfusion territory or diagnosis.

The drafts remain subject to revision-bound radiologist review. They must enter
the normal renderer revision, Review export and website build before becoming
available to learners. Existing review invalidation is retained; no previous
approval is carried forward automatically.
