# MRI-only guided comparison sequences

Two new two-step orientation sequences extend the existing head learning flow:

- Paired amygdalae: left, then right.
- Midline landmarks: partial corpus callosum, then pituitary gland.

These are editorial comparisons, not a continuous neural pathway or a new
segmentation. MRI Guided learning offers seven sequences; CT retains the five
original shared sequences. The existing 3D tour library adds a small MRI-only
group beneath the original five, behind the existing coverage disclosure.

## Navigation and missing anatomy

Stable sequence IDs are `mri-amygdalae` and `mri-midline-landmarks`. Changing an
order requires a new ID; existing URL slots must not be reused. Canonical links
carry only the known concept, tour ID, zero-based step and exact head revision.
The generic 3D destination also requires its exact source tuple. Generated
modules receive only their existing allowlisted focus parameters, not tour
metadata, files, scan identifiers or answers.

Previous/Next preserve the canonical slots. Missing or changed native entries
stay unavailable at their original positions, with no substitute selection,
teaching answer or quick check. Representation-specific practice eligibility
remains enforced. MRI-only CT links are absent, not empty anchors; modality-aware
image parsing rejects a copied MRI-only concept/tour on CT. Browser restoration
uses the current modality, and finishing removes tour metadata without losing
the exact generic focus.

Native files must be reopened locally in a different tab. The tour does not
unlock paid lectures or other independently entitled resources. No native data,
accepted boundaries, private geometry, generated module, access policy or
desktop clinical PACS changes.

## Limits and remaining review

MRI and generic 3D anatomy are different, unregistered subjects. Callosal extent
is partial; these regional masks do not establish nuclei, connecting tracts,
pituitary lobes, endocrine function or diagnostic normality. CT callosal subpart
points and pituitary fossa are not equivalent substitutes.

This change adds guided comparison navigation, not an automatic camera sweep or
patient-space synchronization. Existing model controls/motion remain untouched.
No new source passage, figure, image, mesh, dataset, font, dependency or fee is
introduced; factual-reference audit remains in `HEAD_MRI_ONLY_TEACHING.md`.

Radiologist review should inspect step order, exact native laterality/extent,
partial-mask limits, generic target anatomy and the linked teaching against the
exact new revision. Synthetic UI/software checks do not replace signed-in review
or clinical acceptance. Public deployment remains separately gated.
