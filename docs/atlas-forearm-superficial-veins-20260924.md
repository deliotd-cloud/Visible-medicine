# Superficial forearm veins: shared-viewer source integration

The 24 September generated regional viewer is sourced from Atlas commit
`4e35cae205387f1416cbf4ab98cb9f02ab9b0646`. Its new forearm study focuses
the eight separately supplied left/right cephalic, basilic, median cubital
and median antebrachial vein surfaces, using the existing radius/ulna for
orientation. It offers side simplification, selection, isolation and
reversible removal. The surfaces are not a reconstructed continuous route.

This is draft teaching anatomy, not patient anatomy, venepuncture guidance,
haemodynamic modelling or CT/MRI/US registration. Skin, nerves and validated
access anatomy are absent; a radiologist must review the source positioning
and notes on this exact revision. Didanix Education/light, Atlas, case and
lecture access remain independent. No clinical approval or paid access change
is implied.

The offline upgrade plan accepted 12 scopes and preserved 135 model hashes
and 142 paths, with zero additional model bytes. It reported no patient data
in the export. The generated module and inventory are pinned to manifest
SHA-256 `9146bc4550c20b2e4e3e3cdfec61da7003a93e240da0c0df17777ab78e2967d9`
and inventory SHA-256
`b4b91927b37958f446aadb50e83d6a5698b01cf78bcd65fc8f344cf59d13072b`.
The private native-MRI checker is excluded from `source-inputs.json`.

Source tests, website checks and a build are required before saving or
publication. Protected delivery packaging and owner-only access must be
verified separately before calling any deployment current. Full historical
contract replay, real-device/accessibility acceptance, radiologist sign-off
and all first-release gates remain open.
