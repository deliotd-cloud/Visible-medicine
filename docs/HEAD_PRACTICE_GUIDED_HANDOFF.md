section offers the relevant Guided learning sequence at that exact structure.

current quiz and immutable first-attempt results alone. Pending questions and
unfinished sets expose no new hints.

source-pinned 3D destinations. Revision, label, laterality, native identity and
representation must match; stale or unknown entries receive no suggestion.

existing tour keep their comparison link without an invented sequence.

synchronization. It adds no teaching claims, scan uploads, score persistence,
approval, access grants or new assets. Existing draft learning and independent
entitlement boundaries remain unchanged. The existing compact layout is reused.
