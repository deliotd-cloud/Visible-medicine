# Compact contextual head comparison

The head comparison toolbar keeps the confirmed structure, live connection
status and source/revision-bound CT/MRI actions visible. Change structure and
More learning are native disclosures; on desktop their bounded panels overlay
the model instead of increasing the toolbar's grid height. Unselected anatomy
offers an explicit Choose linked anatomy control.
At narrow widths panels anchor to the full toolbar with an inset, rather than
the small summary, so the chooser and learning panel stay inside the viewport.

Escape returns focus to the disclosure's own summary. Moving focus outside
closes that panel; a window blur does not discard its unsent choice. Native
disclosures retain their mounted selection across close/reopen and confirmed
model updates. A brief ready/connecting/ready transition unmounts the guarded
form but retains only its unsent canonical anatomy ID on the surviving toolbar
DOM owner; a new toolbar or refused source discards it. No browser storage or
automatic navigation is used. Opening a chosen structure is explicit. Comparison links continue
to follow the confirmed model focus, not an unsent choice.

Connection, unavailable, guided and practice states retain their source/activity
guards. There are no new image mappings, patient-space registration or clinical
approvals. CT/MRI remain separate subjects. Lecture access remains independently
checked; no menu unlocks paid resources. Teaching and all source/model bytes are
unchanged. Renderer/Review bindings are advanced normally without carrying old
clinical decisions forward. The exact previous delivery and checklist39 records
are frozen under tests/fixtures/head-comparison-before-compact.

Actual candidate/installed browser dimensions and checks are recorded in the
dated local checkpoint. Unit markup and CSS assertions alone do not prove a
compact layout, keyboard/assistive acceptance or clinical readiness. The full
71-requirement/eight-idea roadmap and its statuses/acceptance remain intact.
