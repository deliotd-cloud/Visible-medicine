# Visible Medicine naming decision and earlier platform options

These are strategic working options, not cleared marks. Complete company-name, trademark, domain, app-store and relevant medical/education market checks before adopting a permanent name.

| Rank | Name | Positioning | Watch-out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Visible Medicine** | Clearest umbrella for Atlas, Courses and Studio; immediately understandable to institutions. | Descriptive and therefore less distinctive. |
| 2 | **Elivion Learn** | Friendly, broad and able to extend beyond one imaging specialty. | “Learn” is widely used and may be harder to protect. |
| 3 | **Elivion Academy** | Established, premium and institution-ready. | Can imply that Elivion itself awards credentials. |
| 4 | **Elivion Scholar** | Strong learner and research association. | Sounds more individual than institution-platform oriented. |
| 5 | **Elivion Lumen** | Suggests illumination, visibility and visual understanding. | Meaning is indirect and requires brand explanation. |
| 6 | **Elivion Forma** | Distinctive; evokes structure, anatomy and formation. | Less immediately descriptive in English. |
| 7 | **Elivion Praxis** | Emphasises applied learning and case-based practice. | Academic term may feel less accessible to early learners. |
| 8 | **Elivion Campus** | Clearly supports institutions, cohorts and hosted teaching. | May feel narrower for independent learners. |
| 9 | **Visible Medicine Studio** | Makes educator creation the commercial centre of gravity. | Conflicts with the current Studio section name. |
| 10 | **Elivion Nexus** | Communicates a connected learning and institution platform. | Broad technology term with weaker medical specificity. |

## Recommendation

Use **Visible Medicine** as the working launch name because it makes the product hierarchy easiest to understand: Visible Medicine contains Atlas, Courses and Studio; Didanix remains the clinical PACS. If a more protectable permanent brand is preferred after validation, investigate **Elivion Lumen** and **Elivion Forma** first, then retain “Education” as a descriptor.
