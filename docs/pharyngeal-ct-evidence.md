# Longitudinal pharyngeal muscle CT evidence — 31 October 2026

**Disposition:** Keep all six CT placements pending. A primary report describes manual segmentation of all three named muscles from one CT angiogram, but its accessible abstract also states that the resulting meshes were refined with anatomical literature and medical illustration. It does not establish which boundaries were independently visible on the scan, side-specific extent, reproducibility, or routine CT visibility. No anatomical/clinical approval or readiness promotion follows.
