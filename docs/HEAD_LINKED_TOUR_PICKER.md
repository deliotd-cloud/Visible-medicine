# Linked tours without scrolling to the library

The head-and-neck 3D wrapper offers a compact **Linked tours** disclosure beside
its comparison controls, both during exploration and during a linked tour. The
existing coverage/source library stays available below the model.

Choose one of the seven source-bound sequences, then **Start at step 1**. Changing
the dropdown alone does not navigate or change the current comparison. Starting
an active sequence restarts at its first canonical step; this is not a resume or
progress record. The active sequence is preselected during a tour.

The picker starts in the existing generic 3D Atlas. Five sequences have CT/MRI
targets; two are MRI-only. MRI-only options remain labelled as such and do not
acquire CT targets. Links are constructed from the existing allowlisted sequence
IDs, canonical first concept and exact source bindings, not from arbitrary
submitted URLs or the live selection inside the iframe.

The native disclosure is closed initially. Enter/Space opens it; Tab reaches the
labelled required select and submit button. Escape closes it and returns focus to
its summary. Desktop uses a small overlay so opening the picker does not reduce
the model's vertical space. At narrow widths it expands inline to avoid an
off-screen popup. Existing notices and comparison limits remain available.

No sequence content, order, revision, medical fact, geometry, accepted mask or
review decision changes. The original five-sequence and independent MRI-only
packet identities remain unchanged. These compare concepts across separate,
unregistered subjects; the picker adds no camera animation or spatial transform.

No persistence, patient data, network data API, cross-frame messages, dependency,
font, asset, licence obligation, fee, permission or entitlement change. Browser
verification is local and scan-free; it does not replace signed-in review or the
owner's revision-bound clinical acceptance.
