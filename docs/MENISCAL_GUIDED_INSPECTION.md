## Teaching and rights

Original short draft captions cite Brody et al., *The meniscal roots: gross
anatomic correlation with 3-T MRI findings* (2007), DOI10.2214/AJR.06.0509:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17449741/

The main agent inspected the primary indexed abstract. It supports orientation
of root attachments relative to the tibial plateau/cruciate insertions in the
study, not this private acquisition's protocol, annotation extent, normality or
diagnosis. A whole-meniscus annotation does not prove coverage of each horn,
body or root. No publisher figure/text, scan, model, dataset, font, dependency
or fee was added. Existing asset notices and licenses are unchanged.
