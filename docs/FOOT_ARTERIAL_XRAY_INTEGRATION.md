# Foot arterial X-ray teaching integration

Twelve foot arterial selections now have original draft X-ray teaching in the
isolated website candidate. The six bilateral groups are dorsalis pedis, medial
plantar artery, lateral plantar artery, plantar arch, deep plantar connector and
the superficial medial plantar branch. These are teaching additions, not new
geometry or acquired radiographs. Radiologist review is still required.

## Teaching and review boundaries

Normal projection radiography does not delineate these small arterial lumens.
Teaching about radiographic calcification does not establish patency, flow or
occlusion. Contrast angiography is a separate acquisition, and anatomical
variation does not imply a complete arch or universally suitable procedure.
The two venous arch X-ray placements and four branch ultrasound placements were
not filled by this batch. Semantic anatomy links do not register a patient scan.

The full source transition checks 9,936 topic placements: exactly twelve change
from pending to draft and the other 9,924 remain unchanged. All 1,104 source
fingerprints remain unchanged. Current totals are 9,314 draft, 583 pending and
39 generated topic placements, not unique reviewed lessons or a completion rate.
Changed teaching revisions invalidate stale review packets; they do not migrate
an earlier clinical decision to the new teaching.

## Validation and remaining acceptance

The website test exercises the imported production resolver, generated regional
reader, Review worksheet/parser and decision API with synthetic requests. It
checks all twelve identities against their immutable parent, the unchanged
licensed bundles, fresh output arrays, wrong identity/topic refusals and stale or
forged review refusal before storage. Immutable introduction evidence is retained
separately so later legitimate teaching can advance without rewriting history.

Build validation uses only three named local source-comparison modules from the
private original plexus/optic/disc review prototypes, retaining zero admissions.
Credentials, scans and the private regional MRI runtime binding are not copied
into the candidate. These private inputs and build products are excluded from
