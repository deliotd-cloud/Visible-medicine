## Conditions for the website

Follow [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en):

- Retain supplied creator credit, copyright/licence notices and source links.
- Link the licence and identify modifications, including retained notices of
  previous modifications. Describe any new crops, overlays or redactions honestly.
- Do not imply endorsement by the source authors or institutions.
- Do not apply additional terms or technical restrictions to these images that
  prevent recipients exercising the licensed rights. Keep proprietary questions,
  course content and service terms distinct from the source images' CC BY licence.

For the pilot, draft attribution to preserve with the eventual displayed cases:

> Images from GRAZPEDWRI-DX, version 2, by Eszter Nagy, Michael Janisch, Franko
> Hrzic, Erich Sorantin and Sebastian Tschauner. Source:
> https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.14825193.v2. Licensed under CC BY 4.0:
> https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Selected for educational review;
> original PNG pixel files retained. Questions are separate draft teaching content.

The viewer's reversible display transforms do not alter the stored PNGs. If any
future export changes the pixels, update the modification statement and bind it
to the exact new rendition. Preserve any source-provided notices and cite the
accompanying paper as requested by the dataset authors.

## Separate privacy/release decision

CC BY 4.0 grants copyright-related permissions held by the licensor. Section 2(b)
does not grant privacy, publicity or other personality rights. The licence does
not certify the privacy of each local file or the clinical accuracy of draft
answers. The owner's explicit instruction remains **keep the six-case images
local until their review is finished**. This licence finding does not override
that hold or constitute clinical, public-sharing or learner-release approval.
